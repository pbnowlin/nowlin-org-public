How to play on one device

  • Tap a piece to see where it can go — dots are quiet moves, rings are captures.
  • Tap a highlighted square to move there. Tap the same piece again to cancel.
  • When a pawn reaches the far side, pick its promotion — queen, rook, bishop or knight.
  • With auto-flip on, the board turns after every move. Turn it off to keep White at the bottom, and use Flip board whenever you like.
  • Undo takes back the last move. New game resets the board.

Rules in full: castling (king and rook unmoved, no pieces between, king not in or through check), en passant, promotion, check, checkmate and stalemate are all enforced. A game ends in checkmate, stalemate, or when one side has only its king left against a lone king.
