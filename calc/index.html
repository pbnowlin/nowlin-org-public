What this is

A pocket TI-89, rebuilt for the web. The real one is a graphing calculator with a computer algebra system — this page gives you the parts students actually reach for every day, without the menu maze.

  • Home screen — arithmetic, trig, logs, powers, roots, factorials, π and e. Type sin(pi/2) or 2^10 and hit Enter.
  • Graphing — enter up to three functions in Y =, set the window, and plot them. Click the graph to trace.
  • Calculus — numeric derivative at a point, definite integral, and solving f(x) = 0 by Newton's method.
  • Degrees or radians — tap MODE to switch. It stays on the screen so you always know which you're in.
