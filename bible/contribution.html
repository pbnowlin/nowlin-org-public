WHY YOUR PARTNERSHIP MATTERS

We serve the Body of Christ by providing free in-depth study tools of God's Word and free online resources to millions of people all over the world each year. All this is made possible through God's grace and those He leads to support our work for Him. We see supporters of Blue Letter Bible as ministry partners and "cheerful givers" (2 Corinthians 9:7-8) with whom we are building long-term and mutually beneficial relationships.

Once again, we do not solicit donations, yet we often receive requests from users of the Blue Letter Bible asking how they may give to the ministry's efforts.
