Two-Age Theology

The branch of theological study focusing particularly upon the already/not yet eschatology presented in Scripture (e.g., the fact that we are already raised up with Christ and seated in the heavenlies (Eph 2:6), but still await bodily resurrection (1Cr 15:50ff); or that we are already glorified (Rom 8:30), but glorification is the last stage of our salvation and still awaiting consummation).
