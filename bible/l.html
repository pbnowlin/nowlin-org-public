Christian Resources

To access these resources you must be connected to the internet.

Koinonia House
Co-Founder of the Blue Letter Bible website. Koinonia House was the ministry of Chuck and Nancy Missler. Its purpose is to encourage and facilitate the serious study of the Bible as the inerrant Word of God!

JoshuaNet
Providing information relating to Christianity and its influence in the world.

Study.Bible (formerly called The Blue Letter Bible Institute)
Study.Bible, was formerly called the Blue Letter Bible Institue. It is a ministry of Blue Letter Bible offering courses in Biblical studies geared toward laymen in order to strengthen the understanding of those desiring to study the Bible and its themes in greater depth without enrolling in an accredited school. Classes are offered for free and may be completed at the student's own pace.

Living in Christ Ministries
Living in Christ Ministries is directed by Pastor Bob Hoekstra and focuses on teaching the Body of Christ to live and grow in the power and grace of our Lord Jesus Christ.

The Word For Today
The Word For Today was an outreach of Calvary Chapel Costa Mesa. Pastor Chuck Smith came alongside the ministry of the Blue Letter Bible at its outset, providing his text and audio commentaries on the Bible. Our grateful appreciation to Pastor Chuck.

Thru The Bible Radio
Thru The Bible Radio with Dr. J. Vernon McGee is a 30-minute Bible study radio program that takes the listener through the entire Bible in just 5 years, going back and forth between the Old and New Testaments.

Calvary Chapel Library
An extensive library of text and audio commentary by Calvary Chapel pastors. A number of pastors from Calvary Chapel affilates have provided content for the Blue Letter Bible. Here are links to their websites:

Firefighters for Christ
Bible study teaching and personal testimonies available for distribution on cassette tape and CD. Much of the library is available for listening online.

Enduring Word Media
Bible study teaching and commentaries of Pastor David Guzik.

Safe Harbor International
Existing to strengthen, encourage, and build up other brothers and sisters throughout the world who are living in fear, hopelessness, desperate need, or are suffering persecution for their faith in our Lord Jesus Christ.

Lambert Dolphin's Library
Essays and articles speaking to a host of Christian topics.

The Ray Stedman Memorial Library
Collecting the works and exposition of the late Pastor Ray Stedman.

All About God
A Christian community for seekers, skeptics and believers.

The Temple Mount
Articles and updates revolving around the Temple in Jerusalem.

Hope for Today
Hope for Today is dedicated to spread the Bible teaching ministry of David Hocking through the avenues of radio, tapes, and publications.

Creation Today
Kent Hovind's website dedicated to the proclamation of factual, scientific evidence supporting the biblical record of creation and the history of the world.

The Cyber Hymnal
Over 6,900 hymns and Gospel songs, very well organized, with lyrics, sheetmusic, MIDI sound files, biographies, pictures, search tools, and more.

The Jesus Film Project
The Jesus Film Project seeks to give everyone in the world one chance to hear the gospel in their own language. So whether a person speaks Swahili, French, or a language whose name is extremely difficult for most to pronounce, he or she will encounter the life and message of Jesus in a language "of the heart." This powerful film has had more than 5 billion viewings worldwide since 1979.

Understand the Times
The international outreach of Roger Oakland, dedicated to evangelizing the lost and equipping the church for discernment. Biblical insights that help to understand the past, what's happening in the world today, and what is yet to take place in the future.

Michael Sewell Ministries
Evangelizing through dramatic plays and music.

Christian Classics Ethereal Library
Classic Christian books in electronic format, selected for your edification. There is enough good reading material here to last you a lifetime, if you give each work the time it deserves.

Bible Foundation
An excellent site for downloadable Bible materials.

Biblical Studies Foundation
A host of items both interesting and edifying articles, book reviews, questions and answers, Theological Journals, etc.

As Chosen To Serve Ministries
Dedicated to providing the tools, free of charge, to help in communicating God's plan, and His message of salvation.

ICLnet
A Guide to Christian literature on the Internet.

According 2 Prophecy Ministries
A world of information about Biblical Prophecy.
