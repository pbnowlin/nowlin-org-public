1826-1888

Information about Cyber Hymnal Website
Born: March 14, 1826, Buckland, Massachusetts.

Died: April 14, 1888, Boston, Massachusetts.


Courtesy of Chautauqua Institution Archives

After moving to Boston as a teenager, Sherwin studied music under Lowell Mason. He later worked at the New England Conservatory of Music, and taught singing in Massachusetts and New York. He was the first music director at the Chautauqua Movement near Chautauqua, New York, and was musical editor at Biglow and Main publishers.
