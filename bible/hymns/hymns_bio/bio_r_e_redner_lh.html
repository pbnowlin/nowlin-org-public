Born: December 15, 1830, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania.

Died: August 29, 1908, Hotel Marlborough, Atlantic City, New Jersey.

Buried: Woodland Cemetery, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania.

Redner worked in the real estate business in Philadelphia, and also played the organ at four different churches during his life. He spent the longest time (19 years) at Holy Trinity Church, where he also helped greatly expand the Sunday School program.
