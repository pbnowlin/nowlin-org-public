Collins graduated from Oxford around 1854. He was ordained an Anglican priest, but moved to Roman Catholicism in November 1857. In 1860, he joined the Cistercian Order. His works include:

  • Hymns for Missions, 1854
  • Life of the Rev. Father Gentili, 1861
  • The Spirit and Mission of the Cistercian Order, 1866
