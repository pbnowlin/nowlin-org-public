Franz Wilhelm Abt

1819-1885

Born: December 22, 1819, Eilenburg, Germany.

Died: March 31, 1885, Wiesbaden, Germany.

Abt attended the Thomasschule and the University of Leipzig. He conducted in Zurich, Switzerland, and in Brunswick. In 1872, he traveled to America for a successful performing tour. He wrote over 3,000 works during his life.
