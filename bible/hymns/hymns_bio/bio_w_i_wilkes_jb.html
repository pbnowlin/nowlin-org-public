Born: 1785, London, England.

Died: 1869, London, England.

Wilkes studied at the Royal Academy of Music, then played the organ at St. David’s Church, Merthyr Tydfil; Llandaff Cathedral; and Monkland church, near Leominster (1860). He contributed to the first edition of Hymns Ancient and Modern.
