1836-1879

Information about Cyber Hymnal Website
Born: December 14, 1836, Astley, Worcestershire, England.

Died: June 3, 1879, Caswall Bay, near Swansea, Wales.

Buried: Astley, Worcestershire, England, the city of her birth. On her tombstone was the Scripture verse she claimed as her own: “The blood of Jesus Christ cleanseth us from all sin” (1 John 1:7).

Daughter of hymnist William Havergal, Frances was a bright but short lived candle in English hymnody. She was baptized by hymnist John Cawood. She was reading by age four, and began writing verse at age seven. She learned Latin, Greek and Hebrew, and memorized the Psalms, the book of Isaiah, and most of the New Testament. Her works include:

  • Ministry of Song, 1870
  • Under the Surface, 1874
  • Life Chords, 1880
