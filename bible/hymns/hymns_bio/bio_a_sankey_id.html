1740-1802

Born: August 30, 1874, Edinburgh, Scotland.

Died: December 30, 1915, on the steam ship Korona, while going to a Caribbean honeymoon with his wife Ann.

Buried:at sea.

Allan was the son of Ira Sankey and Frances Victoria Edwards. He married twice, to Frances Wann (1899) and Ann Underhill Meighan (1915).

He studied civil engineering and architecture at Princeton University, graduating in 1897.

In 1898, he became president of the Biglow & Main music publishers in New York City. He also served as vice-president of the Leeds and Catlin Phonograph Company, and on the Advisory Board of the Greenich [sic] Bank. His works include:

  • The Male Quartette, with George Stebbins
  • Best Endeavor Hymns, with John Clements (New York: Biglow & Main, 1907)
  • Hallowed Hymns, New and Old (New York and Chicago, Illinois: Big­low & Main, 1908) (sold almost 200,000 copies in the first four months)
