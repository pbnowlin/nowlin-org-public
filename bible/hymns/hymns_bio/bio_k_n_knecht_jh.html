Born: September 30, 1752, Biberach, Würtemberg, Germany.

Died: December 1, 1817, Biberach, Würtemberg, Germany.

Knecht played the organ at Biberach for many years. He was also Konzertmeister in Stuttgart for two years. His works include:

  • Erklärung einiger…missverstandener Grundsätze aus der Voglerschen Theorie, 1785
  • Gemeinnütziges Elementarwerk der Harmonie und des Generalbasses, 1792
