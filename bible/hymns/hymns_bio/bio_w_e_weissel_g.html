Born: 1590, Domnau (near Königsberg).

Died: August 1, 1635, Königsberg.

Georg was the son of Johann Weissel, judge and later Bürgermeister at Domnau. Georg studied at the University of Kö­nigsberg (1608-1611), then for short periods at Wittenberg, Leipzig, Jena, Strassburg, Basel, and Marburg. In 1614, he was appointed rector of the school at Friedland, near Domnau, but resigned this post after three years and returned to Kö­nigsberg to resume his studies in theology. Finally, in 1623, he became pastor of the newly built Altrossgart church in Kö­nigsberg, where he stayed the rest of his life.
