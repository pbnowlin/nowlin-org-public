Born: October 7, 1834, Warren, Massachusetts.

Pseudonyms:

  • Nevers
  • Karl Reden
  • Revons

Died: October 18, 1918, Highwood, New Jersey.

Converse studied music in Leipzig, Germany, and law in America (Albany University). He worked as an attorney in Erie, Pennsylvania. He wrote a number of pieces of music, including tunes for Ira Sankey and William Bradbury.
