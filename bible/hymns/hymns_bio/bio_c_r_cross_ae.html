Born: December 30, 1864, Manchester, New Hampshire.

Died: April 23, 1943, Manchester, New Hampshire.

Cross attended Amherst College and the Andover Theological Seminary. He pastored in Cliftondale, Massachusetts (1892-1896); Springfield, Massachusetts (1896-1901), the Old South Church, Boston, Massachusetts (1901-1912); and Milford, Massachusetts (1916-1925). His works include:

  • Pass on the Torch, 1929
  • Thunder Over Jerusalem, 1936
