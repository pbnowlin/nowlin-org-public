Born: March 30, 1877, Mansfield, Nottinghamshire, England.

Died: December 29, 1949, Bournemouth, Hampshire, England.

Greatorex attended Derby School and St. John’s College, Cambridge. He served as assistant music master at Uppingham School, and music master at Gresham’s School in Holt, Norfolk, England, from 1911 to 1949.
