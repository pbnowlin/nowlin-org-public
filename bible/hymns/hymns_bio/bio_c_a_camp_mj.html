Born: November 25, 1871, Chanute, Kansas.

Died: May 25, 1937, Chicago, Illinois.

A banker’s daughter, gifted pianist and singer, Mabel went to a girls’ school in Steubenville, Ohio. She married lawyer Norman H. Camp, and they became Christians together at one of Dwight Moody’s Bible Union classes taught by William F. Newell.
