Born: November 9, 1882, New York City.

Died: May 1, 1963, Englewood, New Jersey.

Cory attended Brearley School and Reynolds School in New York, and her family belonged to the Brick Presbyterian Church in New York City. She married Robert Haskell Cory in 1911.
