After attending the Dissenting Academy in Exeter, England, Heath became the pastor of a Presbyterian church in Honiton, Devon, in 1770. Later he served as a Unitarian minister. His works include:

  • Hymns and Poetic Essays Sacred to the Public and Private Worship of the Deity, and to Religious and Christian Improvement, 1781
  • A History of Bristol, 1797
