Born: October 7, 1835, Spa Villa, Bath, England.

Died: March 10, 1917, Newport, Monmouthshire, England.

Pierpoint attended Queen’s College at Cambridge University, and later taught classics at Somersetshire College. He later lived at Babbicombe in Devonshire, and elsewhere, and occasionally taught. His works include:

  • The Chalice of Nature and Other Poems, undated, but reissued in 1878 as Songs of Love, the Chalice of Nature, and Lyra Jesu.
