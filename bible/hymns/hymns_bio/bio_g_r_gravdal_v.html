Born: June 19, 1966, Haugesund, Norway.

Farsund, Norway

Gravdal grew up on a small farm in Gravdal, Strandebarm, Hardanger. He was educated at the Norwegian Agricultural University at Ås (1992). Since 1994, he has been teaching at the Lyngdal Jordbruksskole agricultural college in Lyngdal, Vest-Agder. His works include:

  • Dine fedrars Gud: Nokre songar ved eit tusenårsskifte (God of Your Ancestors: Some Songs at a Millennium Change), 1999
