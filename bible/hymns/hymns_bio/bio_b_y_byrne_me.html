Born: July 2, 1880, Dublin, Ireland.

Died: January 19, 1931, Dublin, Ireland.

Byrne attended the Dominican Convent in Dublin, and the University of Ireland. She worked for the Board of Intermediate Education, and helped compile the Catalog of the Royal Irish Academy. She also contributed to the Old and Mid-Irish Dictionary and Dictionary of the Irish Language.
