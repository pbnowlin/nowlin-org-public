Born: Circa 1590, Niedersachsen (Lower Saxony), Germany.

Died: 1667, Hamburg, Germany.

Schop became a member of the Royal Danish Court musicians in Copenhagen, and, in 1621, leader of the Ratsmusik (municipal musicians) group in in Hamburg, Germany. He was a well known violinist in addition to his compositions.
