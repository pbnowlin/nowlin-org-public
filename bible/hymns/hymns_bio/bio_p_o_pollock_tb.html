Born: May 28, 1836, Strathallan, Isle of Man.

Died: December 15, 1896, London, England.

Pollock was educated at Trinity College in Dublin, Ireland. After taking Holy Orders, he served at St. Luke’s Church, Leeds, and St. Thomas’ Church, Stamford Hill, London. Then, for the remainder of his life, he ministered to the poor at St. Alban’s Mission in Birmingham.
