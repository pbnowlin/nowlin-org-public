Born: Circa 1808, possibly in Sawbridgeworth, Hertfordshire, England.

Died: February 6, 1841, Alphington, near Exeter, England.

Eldest daughter of John Malcolm, G.C.B., Margaret was married in 1827 to Alexander Thomas Cockburn-Campbell, one of the founders of the Plymouth Brethren in England. Her hymns were printed in lithograph form from her manuscript for private circulation. Some of these were published posthumously in the Plymouth Brethren’s Psalms and Hymns (London: Walter, 1842).
