Born: 1760, Plymouth, England.

Died: March 19, 1825, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania.

Buried: St. Paul’s Protestant Episcopal Church, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania.

Husband grew up in Plymouth, where he served as clerk at Surrey Chapel. He emigrated to America in 1809, and taught music in Philadelphia and worked as the the clerk at St. Paul’s Protestant Episcopal Church.
