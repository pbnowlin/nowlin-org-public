Information about Cyber Hymnal Website
Born: April 30, 1835, East Fishkill, New York.

Died: August 28, 1922, Orange, New Jersey.

Buried: Reformed Church, Hopewell Junction, New York.

Wortman graduated from the New Brunswick Theological Seminary in 1860. For many years, he served as pastor at the Reformed Church in Saugerties, New York.
