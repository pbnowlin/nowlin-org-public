Born: April 5, 1792, Milford, Vermont.

Died: March 12, 1871, Brooklyn, New York.

Pond conducted the New York Sacred Music Society and the New York Academy of Sacred Music. His works include:

  • Union Melodies, 1838
  • United States Psalmody, 1841
  • The Book of Praise (Dutch Reformed Church in America), 1866
