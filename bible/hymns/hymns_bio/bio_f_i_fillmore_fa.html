Born: May 15, 1856, Paris, Illinois.

Fillmore and his brothers founded the Fillmore Brothers Music House in Cincinnati. He had a wonderful voice, and as an effective gospel singer was unsurpassed in his day. James Henry Fillmore was his brother.
