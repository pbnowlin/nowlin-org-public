Born: March 22, 1819, Manchester, New York.

Died: January 18, 1875, Elkhorn, Wisconsin.

Buried: Hazel Ridge Cemetery, Elkhorn, Wisconsin.

Webster both composed and performed popular music. He studied with Lowell Mason and was active musically in New York, New Jersey and Connecticut. In 1851, he moved to Madison, Indiana, followed by Chicago, Illinois (1855); Racine, Wisconsin (1856); and finally Elkhorn, Wisconsin (1859). Webster wrote over a thousand ballads and many hymns during his lifetime.
