Born: July 22, 1836, Brighton, Sussex, England.

Died: August 3, 1897, Islington, London, England.

Emily’s father was Edward Bishop Elliott, rector of St. Mark’s Church in Brighton, and her aunt was hymnist Charlotte Elliott. For six years, Emily served as editor of The Church Missionary Juvenile instructor. Her  works include:

  • Chimes of Consecration, 1873
  • Chimes for Daily Service, 1880
  • Under the Pillow, which included the second part of Chimes of Daily Service

Hymns:

  1. Let Us Keep the Feast
  2. There Came a Little Child to Earth
  3. Thou Didst Leave Thy Throne
