Born: November 22, 1850, Woodstone, New Jersey.

Died: June 29, 1922, Elmer, New Jersey.

Conover attended Pennington Seminary and Dickinson College. He became a Methodist Episcopal minister in 1874, and his later years, joined the Hymn Writers’ and Composers’ Society in New York City.
