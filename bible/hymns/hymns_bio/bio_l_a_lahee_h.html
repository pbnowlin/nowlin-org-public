Born: April 11, 1826, London, England.

Died: April 29, 1912, London, England.

Lahee studied under John Goss and William Bennett. He played the organ at several churches, including Holy Trinity Church, Brompton, from 1847 to 1874. In 1855, he and William Irons published their Metrical Psalter.
