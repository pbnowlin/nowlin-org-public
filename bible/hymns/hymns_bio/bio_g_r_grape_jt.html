Born: May 6, 1835, Baltimore, Maryland.

Died: November 2, 1915, Baltimore, Maryland.

Grape belonged to the Monument Street Methodist Church in Baltimore, where he directed the choir and was active in the Sunday School. Late, he was choir director at the Hartford Avenue Methodist Church.
