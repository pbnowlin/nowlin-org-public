Born: 1874, Dymock, Gloucestershire, England.

Died: February 7, 1956, Bierton, Buckinghamshire, England.

Newbolt attended St. John’s College at Oxford University (BA 1895, MA 1912), and was ordained in the Church of England in 1900. He served at Wantage; St. Mary’s, Affley; the Missionary College in Dorchester (1910-1916); All Angels, Brighton (1916-1927); and was canon of Chester Cathedral (1927-1946).
