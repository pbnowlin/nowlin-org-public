Died: February 8, 1902.

Traveling Bachelor of Cambridge University (B.A. 1850, M.A. 1860), Blunt took Holy Orders in 1851. From 1856 to 1860, he was incumbent of Crew Green, Cheshire. In 1860, he moved to the Rectory of St. Luke’s, Chelsea. He wrote his hymns for festivals at St. Luke’s.

Hymns:

  1. Evening Comes, May We, O Lord
  2. From Meadows Bright with Blossom
  3. Here, Lord We Offer Thee
  4. Here, on This Our Festal Day
