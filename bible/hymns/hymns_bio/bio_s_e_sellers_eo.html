Information about Cyber Hymnal Website

Born: October 29, 1869, Hastings, Michigan.

Died: October 19, 1952, Eola, Louisiana.

Sellers’ works include:

  • Personal Evangelism, 1923
  • How to Improve Church Music, 1928
  • Elements of Music Notation and Conducting, 1938
  • Worship, Why and How, 1941

Sellers also helped edit The New Baptist Hymnal in 1926. The New Orleans Baptist Theological Seminary named the E. O. Sellers Music Building after him.
