Born: June 4, 1820, Alexandria, Virginia.

Died: July 18, 1889, Ocean Grove, New Jersey.

Elvina married Richard Hall and, after his death, Thomas Meyers, a Methodist minister of the Baltimore, Maryland, Conference. She attended the Monument Street Methodist Church in Baltimore.
