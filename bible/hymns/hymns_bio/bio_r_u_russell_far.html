Born: July 11, 1849, Richmond, Surrey, England.

Died: March 30, 1914, London, England.

Third son of John, first Earl Russell, Rollo was educated at Christ Church, Oxford (B.A., 1873). A Fellow of the Royal Meteorological Society, his hymns appeared in his Break of Day (London: 1893).

Hymns:

  1. Christian, Rise and Act Thy Creed
  2. Come, Holy Spirit, Kind to All
  3. O God, Whose Voice the Angels Hear
