Born: September 20, 1795, Eisleben, Germany.

Died: November 7, 1857, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania.

Zeuner played the piano and organ, as well as composing, and was a court and military musician. In the early 1800’s, he emigrated to Boston, Massachusetts, where he became president of the Handel and Haydn Society. He moved to Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, around 1839, where he served as a church organist.
