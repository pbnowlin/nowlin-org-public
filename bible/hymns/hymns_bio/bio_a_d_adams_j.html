1863-1954

Born: September 9, 1863, Ipswich, Suffolk, England.

Died: July 15, 1954, York, England.

Adams, a member of the Society of Friends (Quakers), was a progressive teacher, and leader of a local adult school in Frimley, England. She preferred to remain anonymous, so little else is known about her.
