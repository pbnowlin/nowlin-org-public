Born: February 8, 1865, Yates City, Illinois.

Died: September 1, 1936, Santa Barbara, California.

Pseudonyms:

  • Lewis Edgar
  • Edgar Lewis
  • Mary Slater

Jones was a classmate of evangelist Billy Sunday at Moody Bible Institute. After graduation, he worked for the Young Men’s Christian Association in Davenport, Iowa; Fort Worth, Texas; and Santa Barbara, California. Hymn writing was his avocation.

Hymns:

  1. I’ve Anchored in Jesus
  2. There Is Power in the Blood
  3. We Shall See the King Some Day
