Born: 1859, Glasgow, Scotland.

Brownlie attended the University of Glasgow and the Free Church College. An ordained minister, his works include Hymns and Hymn Writers of the Church Harmony (1899), a commentary on the 1898 Church Hymnary (hymnal of the Presbyterian Church of the United Kingdom).
