Born: 1698, Wareham, Dorsetshire, England.

Died: September 26, 1768, Poole, Dorsetshire, England.

Buried: Poole, Dorsetshire, England.

Knapp was the parish clerk at St. John’s Church in Poole for four decades. His works include:

  • A Sett of New Psalms and Anthems in Four Parts (London: 1738)
  • New Church Melody (London: 1753)
