Born: February 8, 1844, Bordentown, New Jersey.

Gilder attended seminary in Flushing, New York. He became associate editor of Scribner’s Monthly in 1870, and editor-in-chief in 1881. His poetical works include:

  • The New Day, 1875
  • Lyrics, 1885
  • Two Worlds, 1891
  • Poems and Inscriptions, 1901
  • In the Heights, 1905
