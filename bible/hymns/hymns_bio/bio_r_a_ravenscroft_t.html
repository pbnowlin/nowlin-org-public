Ravenscroft became a chorister at Chichester Cathedral, England, in 1594. He also served at St. Paul’s cathedral as a chorister, and wrote music for actors there. From 1618-1622, he was the music master for Christ’s Hospital. Some of his work may be found in:

  • A Briefe Discourse (London, England: 1614)
  • The Whole Booke of Psalmes (London, England: 1621)
