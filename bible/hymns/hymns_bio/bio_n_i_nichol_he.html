Born: December 10, 1862, Hull, Yorkshire, England.

Died: August 30, 1926, Skirlaugh, Yorkshire, England.

Pseudonym: Colin Sterne (an anagram of the letters from his name).

Originally planning to be an engineer, Nichol switched his studies to music, graduating from Oxford University with a Bachelor of Music degree in 1888. Most of his hymn tunes were written for Sunday School anniversaries.
