Born: December 13, 1837, Staines, Middlesex, England.

Died: March 1, 1904, Chichester, Sussex, England.

Hodder moved to New Zealand in 1856 as part of a social experiment, but returned to England in 1861 and became a civil servant. In 1897, he retired to Henfield, Sussex. His works include:

  • Memories of New Zealand Life, 1862
  • New Sunday School Hymn Book, 1863 (enlarged edition, 1868)
  • The Life of a Century, 1900
