Born: December 19, 1806, Penderlwyngoch, Gwnnws, Cardiganshire, Wales.

Died: November 14, 1885, Llanddoget Rectory, Denbighshire, North Wales.

Edwards received his Bachelor of Arts degree from Oxford University, and was ordained three years later. After 1843, he served as vicar of Rhosymedre, North Wales.
