Born: June 13, 1861, Somersworth, New Hampshire.

Died: November 14, 1953, Middletown, Connecticut.

Son of Calvin S. and Eliza Chase Harrington, Karl earned his A.B. degree in 1882, and his A.M. in 1885, from Wesleyan University in Middletown, Connecticut. He studied at the University of Berlin (1887-1889) and Yale University (1890-91). He taught high school in Westfield, Massachusetts (1882-1885); Latin at Wesleyan Academy in Wilbraham, Massachusetts (1885-1887); was a Latin tutor at Wesleyan University (1889-91); Latin professor at the University of North Carolina, Chapel Hill (1891-1899); University of Maine (1899-1905); and Wesleyan University (1905-?). While at the University of North Carolina, he directed the Glee Club. His works include:

  • The Roman Elegiac Poets, circa 1914
  • Richard Alsop, “a Hartford Wit”
  • Walks and Climbs in the White Mountains, 1926
