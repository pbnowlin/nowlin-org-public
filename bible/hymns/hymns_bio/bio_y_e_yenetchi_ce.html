Born: March 17, 1953, Southbridge, Massachusetts.

Carl Yenetchi attended Norwich University in Northfield, Vermont, and the Swedenborg School of Religion in Newton, Massachusetts.  He was ordained by the General Convention of the New Jerusalem on June 29, 1980. He has served as Pastor in Elmwood, Massachusetts; Chaplain of the Massasoit School in Braintree, Massachusetts, and Urbana University, Urbana, Ohio; as Pastor of the Palos Verdes Society of the New Church in Rancho Palos Verdes, California. He is currently (2001) Pastor of the LaPorte New Church Swedenborgian in LaPorte, Indiana, where he lives with his wife Betty. He has three grown children and three grandchildren.
