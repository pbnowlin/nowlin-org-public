Albert Simpson Reitz

1879-1966

Information about Cyber Hymnal Website
Born: January 20, 1879, Lyons, Kansas.

Died: November 1, 1966, Inglewood, California.

Buried: Inglewood Park Cemetery, Inglewood, California.

After ordination, Reitz pastored at the First Baptist Church, Berlin, Wisconsin (1918-1921); the Rosehill Baptist Church, Los Angeles, California; and the Fairview Heights Baptist Church, Inglewood, California (1926-1952). He wrote over 100 hymns in his lifetime.
