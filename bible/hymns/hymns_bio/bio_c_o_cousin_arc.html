Born: April 27, 1824, Kingston-upon-Hull, Yorkshire, England.

Died: December 6, 1906, Edinburgh, Scotland.

Daughter of a doctor, Ann married William Cousin, pastor of the Free Church of Mel­rose, Scotland. She contributed many poems to various periodicals; seven hymns to The Service of Praise (Edinburgh, Scotland, 1865); and one to Psalms and Hymns for Divine Worship, 1866, the hymnal of the English Presbyterian Church. Four of her hymns were included in the Scottish Presbyterian Hymnal, 1876.
