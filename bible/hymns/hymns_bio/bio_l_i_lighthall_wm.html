Born: 1865, Ormstown, Quebec, Canada.

Died: 1949, Round Lake, New York.

Buried: Rouses Point, New York.

Lighthall, a station agent for the Delaware & Hudson Railroad for over 30 years, had a number of poems published in the Troy, New York, Times.
