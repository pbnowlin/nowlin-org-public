Born: July 9, 1843, Napoleon, Ohio.

Died: June 14, 1901, Cleveland, Ohio.

Hudson served as a nurse in the American Civil War. Afterward, he taught music at Mount Vernon College in Alliance, Ohio, and served as a Methodist Episcopal minister. His works include:

  • Salvation Echoes, 1882
  • Gems of Gospel Songs, 1884
  • Songs of Peace, Love and Joy, 1885
  • The Temperance Songster, 1886
  • Songs of the Ransomed, 1887
