Born: June 13, 1816, London, England.

Died: September 26, 1876, London, England.

Rimbault played the organ at the Swiss Church in Soho, London, England. He helped found the Musical Antiquarian Society in 1840, and composed operettas and popular and religious music. His works include Early English Organ Builders and Their Works (1865).
