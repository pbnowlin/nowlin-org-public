Gisborne was educated at St. John’s College, Oxford, England, where he was 5th Wrangler of the year (M.A. 1783). Subsequently, he became a Prebendary of Durham. His works include:

  • Sermons
  • Duties of Men
  • Duties of Women
  • Poems Sacred and Moral, 1799; 3rd edition 1803
  • Walks in a Forest, a volume of poems

Gisborne also helped Jonathan Stubbs compile A Selection of Psalms and Hymns for Public and Private Use (Uttoxeter, England: Richards, 1805).
