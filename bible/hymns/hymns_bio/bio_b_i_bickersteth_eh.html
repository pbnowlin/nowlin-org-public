Born: January 25, 1825, Islington, England.

Died: May 16, 1906, London, England.

Bickersteth was an honor graduate of Trinity College, Cambridge. He served as vicar of Christ Church, Hampstead; dean of Gloucester; and bishop of Exeter (1885-1900). He edited three hymnals, and wrote at least 30 hymns of his own.
