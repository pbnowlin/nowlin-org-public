Born: February 16, 1790, Montjoie, near Aix-la-Chapelle, France.

Died: November 2, 1845, Paris, France.

D’Urhan was a well known musician, and played the violin in the Paris Opera. He also played the organ at St. Vincent’s church in Paris.
