Born: May 9, 1807, Ossett, Yorkshire, England.

Died: March 7, 1835, Bradford, Yorkshire, England.

Taylor went to school in Bradford and Manchester, and worked for a merchant and a printer. He later studied at Airedale Independent College, then pastored at Howard Street Chapel in Sheffield (1830-1831). His hymns and poems were published posthumously in Memoirs and Select Remains, 1836.
