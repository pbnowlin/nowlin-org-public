Born: October 28, 1720, Burford, Oxfordshire, England.

Died: London, England.

Buried: Moravian Cemetery, Chelsea, England.

Humphreys trained for the ministry at a Dissenters’ school. In 1740, he began helping the Wesleys at the Foundry. He later became associated with Whitefield, then the Presbyterians.
