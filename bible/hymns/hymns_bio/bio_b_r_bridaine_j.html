Born: March 21, 1701, Chusclan, France.

Died: December 22, 1767, Roquemaure, France.

Bridaine was educated at the Jesuit college of Avignon, and the Seminary of the Royal Missions of St. Charles of the Cross. He was famous as an orator, with a very unconventional, spontaneous preaching style. In his day, he was one of the best known men in France, and the Catholic Encyclopedia has a detailed article on him. His Cantiques Spirituels was published in 47 editions.
