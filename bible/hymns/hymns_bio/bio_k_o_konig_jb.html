Baptized: January 28, 1691, Waltershausen (near Gotha), Germany.

Buried: April 2, 1758, Frankfurt-am-Main, Germany.

König studied under George Philipp Telemann at St. Catherine’s Church in Frankfurt, and later succeeded him. His works include Lieder-Schatz (Frankfurt-am-Main, Germany: 1738).
