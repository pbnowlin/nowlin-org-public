Albert Midlane

1825-1909

Hymns Supplied Through the Gracious Generosity

Information about Cyber Hymnal Website
Born: January 23, 1825, Newport, Isle of Wight.

Died: February 27, 1909, at his home, Forest Villa, St. John’s Road, Newport, Isle of Wight, United Kingdom.

Buried: Carisbrooke Cemetery, Isle of Wight.

A businessman by trade, Midlane credited his Sunday School teacher with inspiring him to write poetry. He wrote over 300 hymns in his life. His works include:

  • Poetry Addressed to Sabbath; School Teachers, 1844
  • Vecta Garland, 1850
  • Leaves from Olivet, 1864
  • Gospel Echoes, 1865
  • Above the Bright Blue Sky, 1867
  • Early Lispings, 1880
