1856-1922

Information about Cyber Hymnal Website
Born: April 21, 1856, near Medford, New Jersey.

Died: September 25, 1922, Norman, Oklahoma.

Oatman attended Herbert’s Academy in Vincentown, New Jersey, and the New Jersey Collegiate Institute, Bordentown, New Jersey. He was ordained as a Methodist Episcopal minister, but his pastoral work was confined to local churches, as he worked full time in the retail and insurance industries. He wrote lyrics for 5,000 Gospel songs.
