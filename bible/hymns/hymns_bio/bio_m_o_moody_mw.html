Born: March 20, 1870, Chicago, Illinois.

Died: August 20, 1963, East Northfield, Massachusetts.

May was the daughter of hymnist Daniel Webster Whittle, and wife of Will R. Moody (son of 19th Century evangelist Dwight Lyman Moody).
