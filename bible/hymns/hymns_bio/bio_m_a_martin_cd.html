Born: August 26, 1866, Jordan, Nova Scotia, Canada.

Died: March 9, 1948, Atlanta, Georgia.

Civilla taught school before marrying Walter Stillman Martin. Together, they produced a number of Gospel songs.

Hymns:

  1. God Will Take Care of You
  2. His Eye Is on the Sparrow
