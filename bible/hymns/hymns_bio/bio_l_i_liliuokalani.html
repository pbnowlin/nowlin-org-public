Born: September 2, 1838, Honolulu, Hawaii.

Died: November 11, 1917, Honolulu, Hawaii.

Musically, Queen Liliuokalani of Hawaii is best known for her song “Aloha Oe” (Farewell to Thee). The music has been adapted to at least two Gospel songs:

  1. He Lives on High
  2. He’s Coming Soon
