Born: 1752, Newport, Monmouthshire, England.

Pseudonym: Sylvestris.

Died: August 24, 1817, Birmingham, England.

Buried: Birmingham, England.

Brewer trained for a career in commerce, but instead became a congregational minister instead. He began preaching at 22 years of age, and pastored at Rodborough, Gloucestershire; Sheffield (1783); Carr’s Lane Chapel, Birmingham (1798); and the Livery Street Chapel, Birmingham. A memoir of him appeared in the Evangelical Register, 1835.
