Born: August 13, 1821, Plymouth, Massachusetts.

Died: November 13, 1890, Boston, Massachusetts.

Dexter attended Yale University and Andover Theological Seminary. He served as a Congregational minister in Manchester, New Hampshire, and was active as an author and editor.
