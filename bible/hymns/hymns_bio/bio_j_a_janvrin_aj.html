Born: December 13, 1846, Jersey, United Kingdom.

Janvrin worked extensively with the Church Missionary Society (CMS) and associated organizations, writing a hymn for the CMS’ anniversary annually for at least 10 years. In 1903, she edited the letters of Bishop Ridley of New Caledonia, which were published as Snapshots from the North Pacific.
