Born: October 7, 1802, Måsøy, Norway.

Died: October 8, 1880.

Buried: Vår Frelsers (Our Savior’s) Cemetery, Oslo, Norway.

Son of a priest, Landstad was ordained in 1827, and worked in many parishes, spending his last years in Kristiania (Oslo). He worked on a hymnal for the Lutheran Church of Norway, known as “Landstad’s hymnal” (1869). He also published a folk song collection (1852-53).

Hymns:

  1. Before Thee, God, Who Knowest All
  2. Full Many Shall Come
