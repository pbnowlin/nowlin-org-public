Born: August 29, 1872, Hilversum, Overijssel, Holland (birth name: Hendricus de Fluiter).

Died: After 1965.

De Fluiter’s family emigrated to Cleveland, Ohio, in 1882. When Henry grew up, he became an apprentice sign painter. His life changed, though, after attending a revival by Dwight Moody and Ira Sankey in Cleveland, Ohio. Inspired by that experience, he enrolled at the Moody Bible Institute in Chicago, Illinois, and went on to a career in evangelism.
