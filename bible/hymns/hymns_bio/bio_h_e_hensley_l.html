Born: May 20, 1824, Bloomsbury, Middlesex, England.

Died: August 1, 1905, in a railway train near Great Ryburgh, Norfolk, England.

Educated at Trinity College, Cambridge, Hensley graduated in 1846 as Senior Wrangler and first Smith’s Prizeman. From 1846-1852, he was a Fellow and Assistant Tutor at Trinity College. He took Holy Orders in 1851, and served as Curate at Upton-with-Clavey, Bucks; vicar of Ipolyts-with-Great-Wymondly, Hertfordshire; vicar of Hitchin, Hertfordshire; Rural Dean, 1867. His works include:

  • Household Devotions
  • Shorter Household Devotions

Hensley’s hymns appeared in Hymns for the Sundays after Trinity (London: Bell & Daldy, 1864) and Hymns for the Minor Sundays from Advent to Whitsuntide (London: Bell & Daldy, 1867).
