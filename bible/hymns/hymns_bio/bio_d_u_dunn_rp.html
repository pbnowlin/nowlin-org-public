Died: August 28, 1867.

A Baptist, Dunn was for some time a professor at Brown University, Providence, Rhode Island. His hymns were mainly translations from Latin and other sources.

Hymns:

  1. Jesus, Jesus, Visit Me
  2. Jesus, Our Fainting Spirits Cry
  3. We Sinners, Lord, with Earnest Heart
  4. No, No, It Is Not Dying
