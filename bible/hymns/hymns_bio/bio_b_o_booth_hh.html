Born: August 26, 1862, Penzance, Cornwall, United Kingdom (his birth name was recorded as Herbert Henry).

Died: September 25, 1926, Yonkers, New York.

Fifth child of Salvation Army (SA) founders William and Catherine Booth, Herbert attended Allesley Park College and the Congregational Institute, Nottingham, England. In the early 1880’s, he was active in SA work in France, and wrote some of his early songs in French. In 1883, he helped found the SA musical department at Clapton. He went on to direct SA work in England (1888), Canada (1892-195), and Australia (1891-1901). Around 1902, he went on extensive lecture tours in America, South Africa, Australia, and New Zealand.

Booth’s works include:

  • Songs of Peace and War, 1890
  • The Salvation Soldier’s Song Book, 1897
