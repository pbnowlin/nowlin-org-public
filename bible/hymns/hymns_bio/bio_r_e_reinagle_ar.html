Born: August 21, 1799, Brighton, England.

Died: April 6, 1877, Kidlington, Oxfordshire, England.

Buried: St Mary’s Church, Kidlington, Oxfordshire, England.

Reinagle was the organist at St. Peter’s in Oxford, England, 1822-1853 (his uncle, also named Alexander, was a conductor, composer, and teacher in Baltimore and Philadelphia). His works include:

  • Psalm Tunes for the Voice and the Pianoforte, 1830

Music:

  1. Beati Immaculati
  2. Ben Rhydding
  3. St. Peter
