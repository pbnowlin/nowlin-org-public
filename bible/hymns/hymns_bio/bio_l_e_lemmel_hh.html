Born: November 14, 1863, Wardle, England.

Died: November 1, 1961, Seattle, Washington.

Lemmel’s family emigrated from England to America when she was 12 years old. They first settled in Mississippi, then moved to Wisconsin. A gifted singer, Helen studied in Germany for four years. Upon her return to America, she began giving concerts and traveling on the Chautauqua circuit. Eventually, she became a vocal music teacher at the Moody Bible Institute in Chicago, Illinois. After retirement, Lemmel moved to Seattle, Washington, where she was a member of the Ballard Baptist Church.
