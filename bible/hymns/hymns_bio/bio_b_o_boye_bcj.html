1742-1824

Information about Cyber Hymnal Website
Born: March 7, 1742, Gentofte, Denmark.

Died: October 17, 1824.

Boye married a supreme court judge in Copenhagen, Denmark. As a hymnist, she was involved with Guldbergs og Harboes Psalmebog (Guldberg’s and Harboe’s Psalter), to which she contributed 146 hymns. She also produced “national dramatic writing.”

Hymns:

  1. Han er opstanden! Store Bud!
    • He Is Arisen! Glorious Word!
  2. Os er idag en Frelser foedt
    • Rejoice, Rejoice This Happy Morn
