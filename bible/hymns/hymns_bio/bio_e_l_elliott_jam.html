Died: November 3, 1841.

Daughter of John Marshall of Hallsteads, Ullswater, England, Julia married Rev. Henry Venn Elliott in 1883. Her hymns appeared anonymously in her husband’s Psalms and Hymns for Public, Private and Social Worship (1835), but in the index to the “3rd thousand” (1839), her initials were added.

Hymns:

  1. Father, if That Gracious Name
  2. Father, Who the Light This Day
  3. Great Creator, Who This Day
  4. I Would Believe, but My Weak Heart
  5. My God, and Can I Linger Still
  6. O Not When o’er the Trembling Soul
  7. O Thou, Who Didst This Rite Reveal
  8. Soon, Too Soon, the Sweet Repose
  9. Welcome to Me the Darkest Night
