Born: 1700, Dunchurch, Warwickshire, England.

Died: October 7, 1783, St. Heots, Huntshire, England.

Tans’ur wrote some 100 psalm and hymn tunes, anthems, a Te Deum, and other material. His works include:

  • A Compleat Melody: or, The Harmony of Scion, 1734
  • The Melody of the Heart, 1735
  • The Royal Melody Compleat, 1754

Music:

  1. Bangor
  2. St. Martin’s
