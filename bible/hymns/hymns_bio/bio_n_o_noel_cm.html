Born: April 10, 1817, Teston, Kent, England.

Died: December 7, 1877, St. Marylebone, Middlesex, England.

Buried: Abbey Church, Romsey, Buckinghamshire, England (beside her father Gerard Thomas Noel, who had been vicar there).

Noel began her most productive writing in middle age, after a prolonged illness; she was an invalid for the last quarter century of her life. Her works include:

  • The Name of Jesus and Other Verses for the Sick and Lonely, 1861 (later editions were titled The Name of Jesus and Other Poems)
