Information about Cyber Hymnal Website
Born: November 10, 1483, Eisleben, Germany.

Died: February 18, 1546, Eisleben, Germany.

Buried: Wittenberg, Germany.

Luther began the Protestant Reformation in 1517, and the Lutheran denomination bears his name to this day (click here for more information). In addition to being a reformer, he was quite active in the area of church music.
