Born: April 14, 1875, Easton, Maryland.

Died: December 24, 1949, Lewes, Delaware.

Poole attended Washington College in Chestertown, Maryland. He became an ordained Methodist minister in 1900, and pastored in the Wilmington Conference for 35 years.
