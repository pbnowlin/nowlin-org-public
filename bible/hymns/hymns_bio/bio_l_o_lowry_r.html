Born: March 12, 1826, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania.

Died: November 25, 1899, Plainfield, New Jersey.

Buried: Hillside Cemetery, Plainfield, New Jersey.

Lowry attended the University of Lewisburg, and later Bucknell University, where he became a professor of literature. He was ordained as a Baptist minister and pastored at West Chester, Pennsylvania; Bloomingdale Baptist Church, New York City; Hanson Place Baptist Church, Brooklyn, New York; First Baptist Church, Lewisburg, Pennsylvania; and Park Avenue Baptist Church, Plainfield, New Jersey. He also worked as a music editor at the Biglow Publishing Company. He wrote about 500 Gospel tunes, and jointly edited the following volumes:

  • Happy Voices, 1865
  • Gospel Melodies, 1868
  • Bright Jewels, 1869
  • Pure Gold, 1871
  • Royal Diadem, 1873
  • Temple Anthems, 1873
  • Hymn Service, 1871, 1872 & 1873
  • Tidal Wave, 1874
  • Welcome Tidings, 1877
  • Chautauqua Carols, 1878
  • Gospel Hymn and Tune Book, 1879
  • Good as Gold, 1880
  • Our Glad Hosannas, 1882
  • Joyful Lays, 1884
  • Glad Refrain, 1886
