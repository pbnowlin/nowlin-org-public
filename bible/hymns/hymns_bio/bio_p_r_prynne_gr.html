Born: August 23, 1818, West Looe, Cornwall.

Died: March 25, 1903, Plymouth, England.

Educated at St. Catherine’s College, Cambridge (BA 1839, MA 1861), Prynne took holy orders in 1841. In 1848, he became the vicar at St. Peter’s in Plymouth, England. His works include:

  • Parochial Sermons, 1846
  • Plain Parochial Sermons, 1856
  • Hymnal Suited for the Services of the Church, 1858
  • The Dying Soldier’s Visions, and Other Poems and Hymns, 1881
