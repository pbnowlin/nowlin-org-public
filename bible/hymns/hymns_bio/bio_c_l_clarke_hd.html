Born: 1888, Cardiff, Wales.

Orphaned at an early age, Clarke moved to London, then to America. He attended the Moody Bible Institute in Chicago, Illinois, then went into composing, music publishing, and evangelism. He served as song leader for Harry Vom Bruch and Billy Sunday, being so impressed by Sunday that he established the Billy Sunday Memorial Chapel in Sioux City, Iowa (where he served as pastor until 1945). Clarke also worked in the evangelism field in Garard’s Fort, Pennsylvania, and South Milford, Indiana.
