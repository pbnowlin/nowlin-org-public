Born: February 17, 1816, New York City.

Died: April 23, 1888, New York City.

Hopper graduated from New York University and Union Theological Seminary. He was ordained a Presbyterian minister in 1842, and pastored in Greenville, New York; Sag Harbor, Long Island; and at the Church of the Land and the Sea in New York City.
