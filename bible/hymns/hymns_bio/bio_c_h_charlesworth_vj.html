Born: April 28, 1839, Barking, Essex, England.

Charlesworth was educated at Homerton College. In 1864, he became co-pastor at the old Surrey Chapel. In 1869, he became headmaster of Charles Spurgeon’s Stockwell Orphanage. His works include:

  • The Life of Rowland Hill
  • Flowers and Fruits of Sacred Song and Evangelistic Hymns (in conjunction with J. Manton Smith)
