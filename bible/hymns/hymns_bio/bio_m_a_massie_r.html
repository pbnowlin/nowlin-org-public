Born: June 18, 1800, Chester, Cheshire, England.

Died: March 11, 1887, Pulford Hall, Coddington, England.

Massie was an Anglican rector in Eccleston, England. He is best known for his translations of German hymns by Martin Luther, Paul Gerhardt, and others. His works include:

  • Martin Luther’s Spiritual Songs, 1854
  • Lyra Domestica, 1860
