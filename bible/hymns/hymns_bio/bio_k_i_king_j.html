Born: 1789, Kingston upon Hull, Yorkshire, England.

Died: September 12, 1858, Kingston upon Hull, Yorkshire, England.

King graduated from Queen’s College, Cambridge, and was ordained in 1814. His first post was evidently as curate of Wellington with Eyton, Shropshire; he became perpetual curate of Christ Church, Hull, in 1822. He later served as curate to Mr. J. Eyton, of Wellington, Shropshire, England. His works include:

  • A Treatise on Conscience, 1838
  • Memoir of the Reverend Thomas Dykes, of Hull, 1849
