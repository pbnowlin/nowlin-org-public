Born: 1827, Scarborough, England.

Potter joined the Roman Catholic church in 1847, and subsequently took Holy Orders. For many years he filled the Chair of Pulpit Eloquence and English Literature at the Foreign Missionary College of All Hallows in Dublin, Ireland. His works include:

  • The Spoken Word; or, The Art of Extemporary Preaching
  • Sacred Eloquence, or, The Theory and Practice of Preaching
  • The Pastor and His People
  • Legends, Lyrics, and Hymns, 1862

Died: 1873, Dublin, Ireland.
