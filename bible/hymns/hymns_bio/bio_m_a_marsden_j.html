Born: December 21, 1777, Warrington, Cheshire, England.

Died: August 11, 1837, Hoxton, London, England.

Marsden was a Wesleyan Methodist missionary who emigrated to Nova Scotia in 1800. He moved to Bermuda in 1808, to America in 1812, and returned to England in 1814. He preached on the Plymouth Dock circuit around 1815.
