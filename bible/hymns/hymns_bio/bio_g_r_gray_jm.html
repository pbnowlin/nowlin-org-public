Courtesy of Moody Bible Institute

Born: May 11, 1851, New York City.

Died: September 21, 1935, Chicago, Illinois.

Buried: Woodlawn Cemetery, New York City.

Gray was for some time rector of a Reformed Episcopal church in Boston, Massachusetts. He was president of the Moody Bible Institute, Chicago, Illinois, 1904-1934.
