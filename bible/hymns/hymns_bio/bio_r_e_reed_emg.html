Born: March 31, 1885, Islington, Middlesex, England.

Died: June 4, 1933, Barnet, Hertfordshire, England.

Reed attended St. Leonard’s School, St. Andrew’s, and the Guildhall School of Music in London. She edited Music and Youth (1923-1926), and helped edit Panpipes and The Musical Student.
