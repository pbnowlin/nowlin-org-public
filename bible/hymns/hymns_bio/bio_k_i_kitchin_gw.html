Born: December 7, 1827, Naughton, Suffolk, England.

Died: October 13, 1912, Durham, England.

Son of a minister, Kitchin attended King’s College School and College and Christ Church College in Oxford, England. He served as Dean of Winchester, then Durham (1909-1912), and the first Chancellor of the University of Durham.
