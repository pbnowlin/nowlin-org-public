Born: June 8, 1660, Husum, Schleswig (a Danish area then, German now).

Died: May 29, 1722, Bremen, Germany.

Laurenti’s real name was Lorenz Lorenzen. He entered the University of Rostock in 1681. After a year and a half there, he went the University of Kiel to study music. While in Kiel, he adopted a Latinized name, which was not uncommon those days. In 1684, he became cantor and director of music at the Bremen cathedral. He wrote at least three dozen hymns in his lifetime, one of the best hymn writers of the Pietistic school.
