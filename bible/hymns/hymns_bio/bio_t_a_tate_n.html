Born: 1652, Dublin, Ireland.

Died: August 12, 1715, Southwark, London, England.

Buried: St. Paul’s Cathedral, London, England.

Tate graduated from Trinity College in Dublin, Ireland. He was a distinguished playwright and in 1692, became Britain’s poet laureate. His collaborative works with Nicholas Brady include:

  • A New Version of the Psalms of David, 1696
  • Psalter, 1702
