Born: March 10, 1831, Wellington, Somerset, England.

Died: March 12, 1895.

Baynes was educated at St. Edmund Hall, Oxford (B.A. 1856, M.A. 1859). Ordained in 1855, he held successively the curacy of Christ Church, Blackfriars; the perpetual curacy of St. Paul’s, Whitechapel; of Holy Trinity, Maidstone; and of St. Michael’s, Coventry. In 1870 he was bishop designate of Madagascar, but resigned in 1871. In 1873 he was appointed canon of Worcester Cathedral, and in 1880 vicar of Holy Trinity, Folkestone. His works include:

  • Lyra Anglicana, 1862 (editor)
  • English Lyrics, 1865 (editor)
  • The Canterbury Hymnal, 1864, and the Supplemental Hymnal, 1869 (London: Houlston & Wright)  (editor)
  • The Illustrated Book of Sacred Poems (London: Cassell & Co. (editor)
  • Autumn Memories and Other Verses (London: Houlston & Wright, 1869)
  • Home Songs for Quiet Hours, 1878
  • Hymns for Home Mission Services in the Church of England, 1879
  • At the Communion Time
