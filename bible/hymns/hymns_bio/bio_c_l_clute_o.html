Born: March 11, 1837, Bethlehem, New York.

Died: January 27, 1903, Los Angeles, California.

Clute pastored in Vineland, New Jersey; Keokuk and Iowa City, Iowa; and Pomona, California. He also served as president of the Agricultural College of Michigan.
