Winfield Scott Weeden

1847-1908

Information about Cyber Hymnal Website

Born: March 29, 1847, Middleport, Ohio.

Died: July 31, 1908, Bisby Lake, New York.

Buried: Woodlawn Cemetery, New York City. His tombstone bears the title of his hymn: “I Surrender All.”

Weeden’s works include:

  • The Peacemaker, 1894
  • Songs of Sovereign Grace, 1897
  • Songs of the Peacemaker, 1895

Music:

  1. I Surrender All
  2. I Wandered in the Shades of Night
  3. My Mother’s Prayer
