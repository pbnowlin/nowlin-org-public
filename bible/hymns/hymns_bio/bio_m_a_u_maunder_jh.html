Born: 1858, Chelsea, England.

Maunder attended the the Royal Academy of Music, and played the organ St. Matthew’s, Sydenham (1876-7); St. Paul’s, Forest Hill (1878-9) and at churches in Blackheath and Sutton. He provided accompaniment for concerts in Albert Hall, and, starting in 1881, conducted of the Civil Service Vocal Union. His works include:

  • “Olivet to Calvary”
  • “Bethlehem”
  • “Penitence, Pardon and Peace”
  • “The Martyrs,” a cantata for men’s voices
