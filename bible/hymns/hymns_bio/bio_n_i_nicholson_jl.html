Born: Circa 1828, Ireland.

Died: November 6, 1876, Washington, DC.

Buried: Philadelphia, Pennsylvania.

Nicholson emigrated to America around age 25. He lived in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, for almost two decades, and was a member of the Wharton Street Methodist Episcopal Church there. Around 1871, he moved to Washington, DC, where he found a clerk’s job in the Post Office Department.
