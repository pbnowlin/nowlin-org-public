Mary was the daughter of George Henry Hooper of Stanmore, Middlesex, England. In 1841 she married Joseph Maude, sometime vicar of Chirk, near Ruabon, and canon of St. Asaph (?-1887). Her works include:

  • Twelve Letters on Confirmation, 1848
  • Memorials of Past Years, 1852
