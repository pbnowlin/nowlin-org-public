Born: October 7, 1746, Boston, Massachusetts.

Died: September 29, 1800, Boston, Massachusetts.

Billings was one of the first major American composers. His works include:

  • The New England Psalm-Singer, 1770
  • The Singing Master’s Assistant, 1778
  • Music in Miniature, 1779
  • The Psalm-Singer’s Amusement, 1781
  • Suffolk Harmony, 1786
  • The Continental Harmony, 1794
