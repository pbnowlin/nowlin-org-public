Born: April 29, 1584, Fraustadt, Schlesien (Silesia).

Died: December 1, 1635, Oberpritschen, Posen.

Teschner became cantor and teacher at Zum Kripplein Christi church, Fraustadt, in 1609. In 1614 he was appointed pastor at Oberpritschen.
