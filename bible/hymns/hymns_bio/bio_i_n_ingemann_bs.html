Born: May 28, 1789, Thor Kildstrup, Falster Island, Denmark.

Died: February 24, 1862.

Buried: Klosterkirken, Sorö, Zealand, Denmark.

From 1822 until his death, Ingemann was Professor of Danish Language and Literature at the Academy of Sorö, Zealand, Denmark. He was also a noted poet: his collected works, published in 1851, fill 34 volumes.
