Born: 1789, near Bunessan, Isle of Mull, Inner Hebrides.

Died: May 21, 1872, Isle of Mull.

Daughter of a Baptist cleric and wife of a small land holder, Mary lived in the village of Cancan. She wrote both songs and poetry in Gaelic.
