Born: November 12, 1818, Newcastle-upon-Tyne, England.

Died: June 10, 1888, Hartlepool, Cleveland, England.

Hemy played the organ at St. Andrew’s Roman Catholic Church in Newcastle, and later taught music at Tynemouth and at St. Cuthbert’s College, Ushaw, Durham. His works include:

  • Crown of Jesus Music, 1864
  • Royal Modern Tutor for the Pianoforte
