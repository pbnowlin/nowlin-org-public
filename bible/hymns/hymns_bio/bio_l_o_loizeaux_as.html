Born: February 12, 1877, Vinton, Iowa.

Died: May 7, 1962, Towson, Maryland.

Buried: Druid Ridge Cemetery, Baltimore, Maryland.

Loizeaux was a manager with the Baltimore Consolidated Light, Heat and Power Company. He was involved with the Baltimore School of the Bible, and editor of the monthly Christian magazine Help and Food.
