Born: May 6, 1915, Camden Town, London, England.

Carter attended Christ’s Hospital and Balliol College at Oxford, then taught at Frensham Heights School. He served in a Quaker ambulance unit in World War II. After the war, he became a freelance writer and broadcaster.
