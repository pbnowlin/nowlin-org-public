Born: October 23, 1862, Motala, Sweden.

Died: July 1, 1914, Stockholm, Sweden.

Storm joined the Salvation Army in 1880, reaching the rank of lieutenant colonel, and becoming financial secretary in 1892. He became disabled in 1899, but continued to serve.
