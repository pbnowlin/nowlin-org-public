Born: 1856, India.

Pseudonym: R. E. (“Royal Engineer”?)

Son of an Army officer, Turton was educated at Clifton College, England, and the Royal Military Academy. He was commissioned in 1876 as a Royal Engineer lieutenant. His works include:

  • Hymns Written by a Layman Between the Festivals of All Saints, 1880 & 1881
  • Truth of Christianity, The Plantagenet Ancestry, 1928
  • The Marine Shells of Port Alfred, 1932

Hymns:

  1. And Now Our Eucharist Is O’er
  2. Behold! The Star Is Shining
  3. Thou, Who at Thy First Eucharist Didst Pray
