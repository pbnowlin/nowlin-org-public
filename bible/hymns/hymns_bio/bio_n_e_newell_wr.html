Born: May 22, 1868, Savannah Ohio.

Died: April 1, 1956, DeLand, Florida.
Courtesy of Moody Bible Institute

Newell graduated from Wooster College, Ohio, in 1891, then attended Princeton and Oberlin Seminaries. After ordination, he became pastor of Bethesda Congregational Church in Chicago, Illinois. In 1895, he became assistant superintendent of the Moody Bible Institute in Chicago. He was also known for his Biblical commentarvies on Romans, Hebrews, and the Book of Revelation.
