Trygve Alvgeir Bjerkrheim

1904-2001

Hymns Supplied Through the Gracious Generosity

Information about Cyber Hymnal Website
Born: August 26, 1904, Bjerkreim, Rogaland, Norway.

Died: February 27, 2001, Aker Hospital, Oslo, Norway.

Buried: Following a funeral in Sinsen Church, Oslo, Bjerkrheim was buried at Lø­ken Cemetery, in Hø­land, Akershus, Norway, the community where he grew up.

Bjerkrheim wrote about 15,000 songs and poems. He translated a number of hymns from English to Norwegian, and many of his own works have been translated into other languages.
