Mary Ann Pepper Kidder

1820-1905

Information about Cyber Hymnal Website

Born: March 16, 1820, Boston, Massachusetts.

Died: November 25, 1905, Chelsea, Massachusetts.

Buried: Woodlawn Cemetery, Revere, Massachusetts.

Kidder was blinded as a teenager, but fortunately for hymn lovers, had her sight restored after a few years. She belonged to the Methodist Episcopal Church, and lived 46 years in New York City.

  1. Above the Waves of Earthly Strife
  2. Did You Think to Pray?
  3. Is My Name Written There?
