Julian is best known as a student of hymnology. The standard reference work in this field is his massive Dictionary of Hymnology: Origin and History of Christian Hymns and Hymnwriters of All Ages and Nations, Together with Biographical and Critical Notices of Their Authors and Translators. This work has been revised and reprinted several times; its publication dates include:

  • London: J. Murray, 1892
  • London: J. Murray, 1908 (this may be the edition revised by James Mearns (1855-1922), vicar of Rushden, Hertfordshire)
  • Grand Rapids, Michigan: Kregel Publications, 1985
