Born: February 21, 1672, Brauchitschdorf, Schlesien (Silesia).

Died: February 12, 1737, Schweidnitz, Schlesien.

In 1702, Schmolck became pastor of Schweidnitz, Silesia, one of the three Friedenskirchen (Protestant churches) in Catholic Silesia. He became much involved in polemic struggles with the Catholic Gegenreformation (counter-reformation). In the last years of his life he became blind and paralyzed.
