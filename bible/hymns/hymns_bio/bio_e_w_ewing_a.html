Born: 1830, Aberdeen, Scotland.

Ewing studied law, but preferred music, which he studied in Heidelberg, Germany. A member of the Harmonic Choir in Aberdeen, he brought his newly written tune “Ewing” to practice one night, and that choir was the first to ever sing it.
