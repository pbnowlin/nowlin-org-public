Born: December 27, 1838, Islington, London, England.

Died: August 29, 1898, Penmaenmawr, Wales.

Sarah was sister to Eugene Stock, secretary and historian of the Church Missionary Society. Her hymns appeared in the Church Missionary Hymn Book (London: 1898) and Hymns of Consecration (London: 1902). Her prose works include:

  • Lessons on Israel in Egypt, 1874
  • The Child’s Life of Our Lord, 1879
  • Bible Stories from the Old Testament, 1882
  • The Story of Uganda and the Victoria Nyanza Mission (New York: Fleming H. Revell Company, 1892)
