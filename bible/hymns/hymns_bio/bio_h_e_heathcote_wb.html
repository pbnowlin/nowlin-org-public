Died: August 1862.

Heathcote was educated at New College, Oxford (B.C.L. 1840). He was for a while Fellow and Tutor at his college; precentor of Salisbury Cathedral; chaplain to the Bishop of Salisbury; and Select Preacher at Oxford. His works include:

  • The Psalter Pointed to the Gregorian Tones
  • Prayers for Children Especially in Parochial Schools, with a Morning and and Evening Hymn (Oxford, England: 1846)
