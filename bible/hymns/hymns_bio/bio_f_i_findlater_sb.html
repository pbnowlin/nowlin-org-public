Born: November 26, 1823, Edinburgh, Scotland.

Died: December 25, 1907, Torquay, Devonshire, England.

Sarah’s husband was Erich John Findlater, Free Church of Scotland pastor at Lochernhead, Perthshire, Scotland. In 1855, Sarah and her sister Jane Laurie Borthwick co-produced a book of translations of German hymns titled Hymns from the Land of Luther.

Translations:

  1. God Calling Yet
  2. O Happy Home
  3. Rejoice, Rejoice, Believers
