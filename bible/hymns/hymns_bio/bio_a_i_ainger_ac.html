Born: July 4, 1841, Blackheath, England (does anyone know which Blackheath this is?)

Died: October 26, 1919, Eton, England.

Son of an Anglican priest, Ainger attended Eton and Trinity College, Cambridge, where he graduated in 1864. He went on to teach at Eton until retiring in 1901. His works include:

  • Memories of Eton Sixty Years Ago, 1917
  • English-Latin Verse Dictionary, with H. G. Winkle
