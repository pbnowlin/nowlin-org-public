Eliza Sibbald Dykes Alderson

1818-1889

Information about Cyber Hymnal Website

Born: 1818, Kingston upon Hull, Humberside, England.

Died: 1889, Wakefield, West Yorkshire, England.

Buried: Kirkthorpe, West Yorkshire, England.

In 1850, Eliza married Rev. W. Alderson, chaplain to the West Riding House of Correction, Wakefield. It was there she wrote the majority of her hymns. Her brother was composer John Bacchus Dykes.

Hymns:

  2. Lord of Glory, Who Hast Bought Us
