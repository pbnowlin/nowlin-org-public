Born: January 10, 1867, Orange, New Jersey.

Died: June 19, 1954, New York City.

Merrill was a graduate of Rutgers College (BA 1887, MA 1890) and Union Theological Seminary (BD 1890). He pastored at Presbyterian churches in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania; Chicago, Illinois; and the Brick Presbyterian Church in New York City (1911-1938). His works include:

  • Footings for Faith, 1915
  • Christian Internationalism, 1919
  • The Common Creed of Christians, 1920
  • The Freedom of the Preacher, 1922
  • Liberal Christianity, 1925
  • Prophets of the Dawn, 1927
  • The Way, 1933
  • We See Jesus, 1934
