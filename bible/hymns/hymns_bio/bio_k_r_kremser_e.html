Born: April 10, 1838, Vienna, Austria.

Died: November 27, 1914, Vienna, Austria.

Buried: Zentralfriedhof, Vienna, Austria.

In 1869, Kremser became chorus master of the Vienna Männergesangverein. His works include:

  • Sechs Altniederländiche Volkslieder (Leipzig, Germany: 1877)
  • Wiener Lieder und Tänze
