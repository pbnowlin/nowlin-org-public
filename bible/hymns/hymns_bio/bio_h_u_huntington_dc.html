Born: April 27, 1830, Townshend, Vermont.

Died: February 8, 1912, Lincoln, Nebraska.

Huntington attended Syracuse University in New York, and was ordained a Methodist Episcopal minister in 1853. He pastored in Rochester, New York (1861-1871 & 1876-1879); Syracuse, New York (1873-1876); Olean, New York (1885-1889); Bradford, Pennsylvania (1882-1885 & 1889-91); and Lincoln, Nebraska (1891-1896). He served as chancellor of Nebraska Wesleyan University (1898-1908), and in 1908 became professor of English Bible there.
