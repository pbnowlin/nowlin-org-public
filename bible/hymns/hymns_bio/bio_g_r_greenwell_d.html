Born: December 6, 1821, Greenwell Ford, Lanchester, Durham, England.

Died: March 29, 1882, Clifton (near Bristol), England.

Greenwell lived at Ovingham Rectory, Northumberland (1848); Golborne Rectory, Lancashire; Durham (1854); and Clifton, near Bristol. Her works include:

  • Poems, 1848
  • The Patience of Hope, 1860
  • Carmina Crucis, 1869
  • The Life of Lacordaire
  • A Present Heaven
  • Two Friends
  • Songs of Salvation, 1873

W. Dorling published Life in 1885.
