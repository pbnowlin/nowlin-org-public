Born: September 1851, Westleton, Suffolk, England.

Died: August 8, 1922, London, England.

Jude played the organ at the Blue Coat Hospital in Liverpool, England, and at Stretford Town Hall near Manchester. He composed operettas and several songs, and edited:

  • Monthly Hymnal
  • Minister of Music
  • Music and the Higher Life, 1904
  • Mission Hymns, 1911
  • Festival Hymns, 1916
