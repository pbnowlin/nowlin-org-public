Information about Cyber Hymnal Website

Born: March 15, 1859, Mount Holly, New Jersey.

Died: March 16, 1942, Trenton, New Jersey.

Buried: Greenwood Cemetery, Trenton, New Jersey.

Zelley attended Pennington Seminary and Taylor University, where he earned his PhD. and divinity degree. Ordained a Methodist minister, he worked in the New Jersey Conference, retiring in 1920. He wrote over 1,500 poems, hymns, and Gospel songs.
