Sebastien Besnault

Hymns Supplied Through the Gracious Generosity

Information about Cyber Hymnal Website

Besnault was a Priest of St. Maurice, Sens, in 1726. He contributed to the Cluniac Breviary, 1686, and the Paris Breviary, 1736.

Hymns:

  1. Debilis cessent elementa legis
    • The Ancient Law Departs
  2. Felix dies quem proprio
    • O Happy Day, When First Was Poured
