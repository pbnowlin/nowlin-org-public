Born: October 20, 1892, Kalamazoo, Michigan.

Died: February 9, 1965, Chicago, Illinois.

Loes studied at Moody Bible Institute, Chicago, Illinois; the American Conservatory of Music; the Metropolitan School of Music; and the Chicago Musical College. From 1927-1939, he served in musical positions in Baptist churches in Okmulgee and Muskogee, Oklahoma. From 1939 to retirement, he served on the music faculty at the Moody Bible Institute.
