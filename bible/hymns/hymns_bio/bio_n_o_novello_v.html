Born: September 6, 1781, London, England.

Died: August 9, 1861, Nice, France.

Novello studied organ under Samuel Webbe, and served as organist at the Portuguese embassy. In 1811,  he founded Novello and Company, and was a charter member of the Philharmonic Society. In 1847, he retired to Nice, and his son Alfred took over Novello and Company.
