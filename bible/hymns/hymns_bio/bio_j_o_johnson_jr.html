1873-1954

Information about Cyber Hymnal Website
Born: August 11, 1873, Jacksonville, Florida.

Died: November 11, 1954, New York, New York.

Johnson attended Atlanta University and the New England Conservatory of Music. He and his brother James belonged to the song writing team of Cole and Johnson Brothers, writing over 200 songs. He also edited a number of collections of African-American music.
