Born: 1772, Bath, Somersetshire, England.

Died: June 22, 1850, Uxbridge, England.

Walford attended Homerton Academy, and was ordained a Congregational minister. He pastored in Suffolk, Norfolk, and Uxbridge, and tutored classics at Homerton.
