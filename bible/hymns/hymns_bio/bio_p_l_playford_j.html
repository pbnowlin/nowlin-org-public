Born: 1623, Norwich, England.

Died: November 1686, London, England.

A bookseller and music publisher, Playford opened a shop at the Temple Church in London around 1647. He was appointed clerk of the church in 1653, and retired in 1684. His works include:

  • The Whole Book of Psalms
  • The English Dancing Master, 1651
  • A Musicall Banquet, 1651
  • Catch That Catch Can, 1652
  • A Breefe Introduction to the Skill of Musick, 1654
