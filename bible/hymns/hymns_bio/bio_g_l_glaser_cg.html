Born: May 4, 1784, Weissenfels, Germany.

Died: April 16, 1829, Barmen, Germany.

Gläser received his musical training first from his father, then at St. Thomas’ School in Leipzig. When he moved to Barmen, he taught voice, piano, and violin. He was well known for writing and conducting choral music.
