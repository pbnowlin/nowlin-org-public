Born: July 12, 1848, Aberdeen, Scotland.

Died: September 15, 1917, Hampstead, London, England.

Hunter was educated at Mansfield College, Oxford, and became a Congregational minister. He served in York, Hull, Glasgow, and London. His works include:

  • Services for Public Worship, 1886
  • Hymns for Faith and Life, 1899
