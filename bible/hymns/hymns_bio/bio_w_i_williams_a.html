Born: 1731, possibly in London, England.

Died: 1776, London, England.

Williams was a music publisher and clerk of Scots Church, London. His works include:

  • The Universal Psalmodist, 1763, 1769
  • Royal Harmony, 1766
  • Harmonia Coelestis, 1775
  • Psalmody in Miniature, 1778
  • The New Universal Psalmodist, 1770
  • An Ode or Anthem for the New Year, circa 1775
  • Two New anthems for Christmas-Day, circa 1775
  • A New Christmas Anthem, circa 1780
