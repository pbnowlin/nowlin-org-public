Born: June 9, 1824, Hancock, New Hampshire.

Died: 1908.

A Unitarian, Love married Dr. Frederick L. H. Willis in 1858. She lived for many years in Rochester, New York; by 1907, she was living in Glenora, New York, on Seneca Lake.
