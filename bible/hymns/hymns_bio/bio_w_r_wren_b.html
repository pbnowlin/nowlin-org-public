Born: June 3, 1936, Romford, Essex, England.

Wren is an internationally published hymn writer whose work appears in hymnals from all denominations and traditions. Ordained in Britain’s United Reformed Church, he holds B.A. and D.Phil (Ph.D.) degrees from Oxford University. He currently lives in Atlanta, Georgia. On 1, July 2000, he was appointed the first John and Miriam Conant Professor of Worship at Columbia Theological Seminary in Atlanta. His works include:

  • Education for Justice
  • Praising a Mystery (Carol Stream, Illinois: Hope Publishing Company, 1985)
  • What Language Shall I Borrow? (Crossroad Books, 1989)
  • New Beginnings (Carol Stream, Illinois: Hope Publishing Company, 1993)
  • Piece Together Praise: a Theological Journal, 1996
  • Faith Looking Forward
  • Praying Twice: The Music and Words of Congregational Song (Westminster John Knox Press, 2000)

For more information, visit his home page.
