Born: August 19, 1813, Islington, Middlesex, England.

Died: February 2, 1906, Newport, Isle of Wight.

Jemima was the daughter of Thomas Thompson, a founder of the Brit­ish and For­eign Sailors’ Society. She began writing early, being first published in the Juvenile Magazine when she was 13. She mar­ried Con­gre­ga­tion­al min­is­ter Samuel Luke when she was 20 years old. From 1841 to 1845, she edited The Missionary Repository. Her works in­clude:

  • The Female Jesuit, 1851
  • A Memoir of Eliza Ann Harris, of Clifton, 1859
