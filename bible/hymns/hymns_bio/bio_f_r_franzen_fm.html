Born: February 9, 1772, Oulu, Uleåborg, Finland.

Died: August 14, 1847, Härnösand, Sweden.

Franzèn studied theology in Turku, Åbo Akademi, and later in Uppsala, Sweden. In 1810, he moved to Sweden. In 1834, he became bishop of Härn­ö­sand. He also served as secretary of the Swedish Academy, and published a hymnal together with Johan Wallin.
