Born: October 25, 1564, Nürnberg, Germany.

Died: June 8, 1612, Frankfurt am Main, Germany.

Hassler studied music under his father, and with Andrea Gabriele in Venice, Italy. He served as organist for Count Octavian Fugger of the famous Fugger family in Augsburg, Germany; at the Frauenkirche in Nürnberg; and for Prince Christian II of Saxony. His works include:

  • Cantiones Sacrae, 1591
  • Neue Deutsche Gesang, 1596
  • Kirchengesänge, Psalmen und Geistliche Lieder, 1608
