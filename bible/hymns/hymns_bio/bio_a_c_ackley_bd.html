Bentley DeForest Ackley

1872-1958

Information about Cyber Hymnal Website

Born: September 27, 1872, Spring Hill, Pennsylvania. He was brother to Alfred Ackley.

Died: September 3, 1958, Winona Lake, Indiana.

Buried: Oakwood Cemetery, Warsaw, Indiana. Homer Rodeheaver lies nearby.

Ackley showed musical talent early, learning the melodeon, piano, coronet, clarinet and piccolo as a boy. After moving to New York City in 1888, he began playing the organ in several churches. In 1907, he  joined the Billy Sunday-Homer Rodeheaver evangelism team as secretary-pianist, and traveled with them for eight years. As a composer and editor with the Rodeheaver Company, he wrote over 3,000 Gospel tunes.
