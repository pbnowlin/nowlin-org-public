Born: February 3, 1888, Greenfork, Indiana.

Died: January 3, 1958, Winona Lake, Indiana.

Blanche attended the Indianapolis Conservatory of Music and the American Conservatory of Music, Chicago, Illinois, and was the composer and pianist for her husband Virgil.
