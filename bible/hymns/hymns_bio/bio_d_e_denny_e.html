Born: October 2, 1796, Tralee Castle, County Kerry, Ireland.

Died: June 13, 1889, London, England.

Edward Denny, son of E. Denny, fourth baronet, of Tralee Castle, County Kerry, succeeded his father as baronet in August 1831. He was a member of the Plymouth Brethren, and contributed much to their hymnody. His works include:

  • A Selection of Hymns (London: Central Tract Depot, 1839)
  • Hymns and Poems (London: 1848, third edition 1870)
