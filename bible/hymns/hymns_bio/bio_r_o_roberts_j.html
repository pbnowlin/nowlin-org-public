Born: December 22, 1822, Tanrhiwfelen, Aberystwyth, Wales.

Died: May 6, 1877, Vron, Caernarvonshire, Wales.

Pseudonym: Ieuan Gwyllt (John Wild).

Roberts began preaching in 1856, was ordained a Methodist minister in 1859, and pastored in Aberdare and Capel Cock. He was also an accomplished musician, conducting music festivals and serving as judge at the local eisteddfodau. His works include:

  • Caniadau y Cyssegr, 1839
  • Llyfr Tonau Cynulleidfaol, Methodist hymnal, 1859 (editor)
