Born: January 22, 1792.

Died: May 17, 1840.

Lucy was the only daughter of Orlando, 2nd Baron and 1st Earl of Bradford. She married in 1820 to William Wolryche Whitmore of Dudmaston, Shropshire, England. In 1824, she published Family Prayers for Every Day in the Week, containing 14 original hymns (second edition, 1827).
