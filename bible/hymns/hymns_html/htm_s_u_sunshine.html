SUNSHINE IN THE SOUL

“His splendor was like the sunrise; rays flashed from His hand.” Habakkuk 3:4

Information about Cyber Hymnal Website
Words: Eliza Edmunds Stites Hewitt, in Glad Hallelujahs, by William Kirkpatrick and John Sweney (Philadelphia, Pennsylvania: Thomas T. Tasker, Sr., 1887). Hewitt, a Philadelphia school teacher, suffered a severe back injury; after six months in a cast, she was finally able to take a simple walk in the park. The joy she felt at her recovery prompted these words.

Music: John Robson Sweney

There is sunshine in my soul today,
More glorious and bright
Than glows in any earthly sky,
For Jesus is my Light.

A carol to my King,
And Jesus, listening, can hear
The songs I cannot sing.
