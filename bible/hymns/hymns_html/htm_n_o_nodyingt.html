A land by faith I see,
Where saints shall ever be
Free from mortality,

No dying there,
No dying there,
In that fair, heav’nly land,

There friends shall meet again,
In happiness to reign,
While through that blest domain,
No dying there.

There tears are wiped away,

No dying there.
