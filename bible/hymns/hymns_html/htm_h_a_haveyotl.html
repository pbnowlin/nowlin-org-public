A vast fortune was left in the hands of a minister for one of his poor parishioners. Fearing that it might be squandered, if suddenly bestowed upon him, the wise minister sent him but a little at a time, with a note, saying, “This is thine; use it wisely; there is more to follow.” Brethren, that’s just the way God deals with us.
