Words: Ed Beaty and Leonard E. Smith, 1994:

“Resting In You” was written by Ed and myself when we worked together on the same worship team leading worship in our church. We were simply overwhelmed by the idea that our lives are really safe in God’s hands no matter what may be happening.

Music: Ed Beaty and Leonard Smith, 1994; arranged by Richard W. Adams, 1997.
