LORD JESUS, THINK ON ME

“Order my steps in Thy Word: and let not any iniquity have dominion over me.” Psalm 119:133

Words: Synesius of Cyrene, circa 430 (Μνωεο Χριστε); translated from Greek to English by Allen William Chatfield, Songs and Hymns of the Greek Christian Poets, 1876.

Music: “Southwell,” William Daman, 1579

Lord Jesus, think on me
And purge away my sin;
From earthborn passions set me free
And make me pure within.

Lord Jesus, think on me,
With many a care oppressed;
Let me Thy loving servant be
And taste Thy promised rest.

Lord Jesus, think on me
Amid the battle’s strife;
In all my pain and misery
Be Thou my Health and Life.

Lord Jesus, think on me
Nor let me go astray;
Through darkness and perplexity
Point Thou the heavenly way.

Lord Jesus, think on me
When floods the tempest high;
When on doth rush the enemy,
O Savior, be Thou nigh!

Lord Jesus, think on me
That, when the flood is past,
I may th’eternal brightness see
And share Thy joy at last.

Lord Jesus, think on me
That I may sing above
To Father, Spirit, and to Thee
The strains of praise and love.
