Bob Cottrill was serving Grace Church in Newmarket, Ontario, Canada, in the area of Christian education and music. The senior pastor used to have the congregation sing the chorus of the song “I’m Glad I’m a Part of the Family of God.” One day, seeking variety, he asked, “Bob, could you write us something on the same theme that we could use?” This hymn is the result.
