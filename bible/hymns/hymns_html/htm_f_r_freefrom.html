Words: Philip Paul Bliss, Sunshine for Sunday Schools, 1873.

Just before Christmas, 1871, Mrs. Bliss asked a friend, “What shall I get for my husband as a Christmas present?” and, at the suggestion of this friend, purchased and presented him with the bound volume of a monthly English periodical called Things New and Old. Many things in these books of interpretation of Scripture and illustrations of Gospel truth were blessed to him, and from the reading of something in one of these books…suggested this glorious Gospel song.

Daniel W. Whittle, Memoirs of Philip P. Bliss
