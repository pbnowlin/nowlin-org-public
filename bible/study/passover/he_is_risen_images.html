Illustrations of the Death and Resurrection of Christ

"And the angel answered and said unto the women, Fear not ye: for I know that ye seek Jesus, which was crucified. He is not here: for he is risen, as he said. Come, see the place where the Lord lay." Mat 28:5-6

Special Note: Some of our illustrations are from books as old as 1560. We hope no one is stumbled by old-fashioned illustrations of the life of Jesus Christ. They merely stand as visual representations in order to describe a biblical event in the same manner that an illustrated children's Bible seeks to obtain that very goal. If you find this type of imagery offensive, please email us!
