Daughters of Men

There are only two families in the ages before the flood, the sons of God and the daughters of men (Gen 6:1-8). The descendants of Seth were the sons of God (Gen 4:25; Gen 5:3-32). There was only one other family (Gen 4:9-23). Therefore, the daughters of men were descendants of Cain.
