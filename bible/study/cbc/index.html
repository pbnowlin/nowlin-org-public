Condensed Biblical Cyclopedia
About the CBC
The Condensed Biblical Cyclopedia was written by Ashley S. Johnson in 1896. It was "designed for those who have but little time for study."

Thanks to the Sword Project for providing this public domain text. Please note that the entries listed below are organized chronologically rather than alphabetically.
