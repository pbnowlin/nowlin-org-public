Last Days of Abraham

The closing years of Abraham's life were distinguished by

  1. his marriage to Keturah (Gen 25:1-2);
  2. the birth of Esau and Jacob (Gen 21:5; Gen 25:1-26);
  3. giving his property to Isaac and sending the sons of his concubines away with gifts (Gen 25:5-6).
