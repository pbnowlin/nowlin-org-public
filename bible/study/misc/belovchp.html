Beloved Chapters of the Bible

Throughout life, Christians continually reach out to the word of God for strength, comfort, and guidance. These golden chapters are a small presentation of scripture meant to especially enrich the abundant life for Jesus Christ. Within these influential verses, the fundamental nature of God's incredible supremacy and sanctity are revealed.
