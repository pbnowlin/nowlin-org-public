Church

THE CURRENT TEACHING OF THE MORMON CHURCH:

The "True Church" is the Mormon Church. The church Christ set up was lost until Joseph Smith restored it.

"My object in going to inquire of the Lord was to know which of all the sects was right, that I might know which to join. I was answered that I should join none of them, for they were all wrong…"

The Pearl of Great Price, Joseph Smith 2:16-17

THE BIBLE SAYS ABOUT THE CHURCH:

1. THE CHURCH WAS NEVER LOST!

The gates of hell will not prevail against it. Mat 16:18
Lo, I am with you always. Mat 28:20

2. CHRIST IS THE HEAD OF THE CHURCH:

Christ is the head of the Church and saviour of the body. Eph 5:23
Christ is head of the body. Col 1:18

3. ALL BELIEVERS ARE MEMBERS OF THE CHURCH:

All believers have been baptized into one body. 1Cr 12:13-20
Believers are one in body though they are many in number and diversity Rom 12:4-8
Though it is difficult to understand (a mystery) all believers are of the same body and follow the same Gospel Eph 3:5-7
All believers are unique and especially joined together Eph 2:10-22
All believers are one family in Jesus Gal 3:26-29
All believers are spiritually one, just as Jesus and the Father are one Jhn 17:11-13
All believers were designed by God to be one body Col 3:15

4. THE TRUE CHURCH IS A WITNESS OF JESUS:

They practice an earth wide witness of Jesus Act 1:8
Their message includes salvation in Jesus's name Act 4:12
Their message includes a confession of Jesus before man Luke 12:8-9
Their faith includes an experience of Jesus in their midst. Mat 18:20

5. THE TRUE CHURCH IS LOCATED WHERE BELIEVERS ARE:

Gathered in my name, I am there Mat 18:19-20
