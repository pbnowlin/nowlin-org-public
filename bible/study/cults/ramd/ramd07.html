THE CURRENT TEACHING OF THE MORMON CHURCH:

Baptism Into the "True Church"

WHAT THE BIBLE SAYS ABOUT BORN-AGAIN

1. THE NEW BIRTH MEANS REGENERATION, WITH ALL BELIEVERS BEING EQUALLY RENEWED. IT HAS NOTHING TO DO WITH BELONGING TO A CHURCH OR BAPTISM.

Being born again, not of corruptible seed, but incorruptible 1Pe 1:23
Be a new creation 2Cr 5:17
Ye must be born again Jhn 3:7
Except ye be born again, cannot see kingdom Jhn 3:3
All believers are united under one fold, one shepherd. Jhn 10:15-16

2. THE NEW BIRTH IS NECESSARY BECAUSE OF:

All have sinned Rom 3:23
Wages of sin, death; gift of God, eternal life, Rom 6:23
Man's inability to renew himself in his present state Jhn 3:3,5
Man's human flesh alone cannot accommodate eternal life Jhn 3:6
Man is under a curse and will die Eph 2:1
Deeds for the law, no man justified Rom 3:20
Offend one point of the law, guilty of all Jam 2:10

3. THE NEW BIRTH IS INTRODUCED TO MAN BY:

The Holy Spirit Jhn 3:5, 8
The Word of God. Jam 1:18; 1Pe 1:23
Man's Obedience Rom 6:17-18

4. THE STEPS TO RECEIVING THE NEW BIRTH:

We acknowledge that NO ONE is good enough to receive Rom 3:10
We admit that sin brings death to any man Rom 6:23
We confess that we, too, have sinned Rom 3:23
We declare that Christ died for our sins Rom 5:8
We recognize Jesus as Lord and put our trust in Him Rom 10:9-10
Call on the Lord in prayer Rom 10:13
We accept the gift of eternal life 1Jo 5:11-13; Jhn 5:24
We seek to discover and live out God's plan for our life. Jhn 10:10; 2Cr 5:17; 1Th 5:18

5. WE MUST CONFESS JESUS AS LORD:

Confess Jesus before men Mat 10:32-33
Confess with thy mouth, believe in thy heart Rom 10:9-10
