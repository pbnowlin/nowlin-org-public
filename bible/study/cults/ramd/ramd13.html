The Gospel

THE CURRENT TEACHING OF THE MORMON CHURCH:

The L.D.S. Gospel is its church system and doctrines.

"…With the loss of the gospel, the nations of the earth went into a moral eclipse called the Dark Ages, Apostasy was universal."…The true gospel of Jesus Christ was restored to earth in the last days through the instrumentality of Joseph Smith. It is found only in the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-Day Saints."

MORMON DOCTRINE pgs. 43,33,334.

WHAT THE BIBLE SAYS ABOUT THE GOSPEL

1. THE GOSPEL HAS NOT BEEN LOST, AND NEVER WILL BE:

Though we, or an angel from heaven preach any other Gospel unto you than that which we have preached unto you, let him be accursed. Gal 1:8
My words shall not pass away. Mat 24:35
Word of our God shall stand for ever. Isa 40:8
Word preserve from this generation for ever. Psa 12:6-7
He had the eternal gospel to proclaim. Rev 14:6
If the gospel is hid, its hid from those who are lost. 2Cr 4:3-4

2. THE GOSPEL IS THE MESSAGE OF CHRIST DEATH, RESURRECTION AND ATONEMENT FOR OUR SINS:

Gospel is preached, received and people are saved. 1Cr 15:1
Saved, if we believe Christ died, buried, and rose again on the third day. 1Cr 15:2-4
The Gospel saves instantly Act 2:37-42; Rom 10:8-13
Gospel, the glory of Christ 2Cr 4:4

3. THE BIBLE DESCRIBES THE GOSPEL:

God Rom 1:1
Christ 2Cr 2:12
Peace Eph 6:15

4. THE BIBLE DEFINES THE GOSPEL:

Of supernatural origin Gal 1:10-12
God's power 2Cr 2:12
Mystery Eph 6:13

5. THE BIBLE SAYS THE GOSPEL IS THE SOURCE OF:

Men are begotten through Gospel 2Cr 4:15
Peace Eph 6:15
Immortality to light through the gospel 2Ti 1:10

6. THE BIBLE SAYS THE GOSPEL IS PROCLAIMED BY:

Old Testament Gal 3:8
Prophets Rom 1:1-2
Jesus and John Mar 1:14-15
Chosen Men 1Pe 1:12
He had eternal gospel to proclaim Rev 14:6

7. THE BIBLE SAYS THE GOSPEL IS PROCLAIMED TO:

All people Mar 16:15-16
At all times Rev 14:6
With boldness Eph 6:19

8. THE BIBLE SAYS THE ONE WHO BELIEVES IN THE GOSPEL…

Is sealed into the family of believers by the Holy Spirit Eph 1:3
Will defend the Gospel and those that proclaim it Phl 1:7, 17, 27

9. THERE IS A PENALTY FOR PREACHING "ANOTHER" GOSPEL.

Penalty For Preaching "Another" Gospel Gal 1:6-9
