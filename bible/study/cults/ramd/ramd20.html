Polytheism
(Belief in a number of Gods, Webster's II New Riverside Dictionary Pg. 541)

THE CURRENT TEACHING OF THE MORMON CHURCH:

The Mormon Church believes in more than one God.

"I will teach on the plurality of Gods…we have three Gods anyhow, and they are plural "HISTORY OF THE CHURCH 6:475, 76 Joseph Smith, Jr. "…and you have got to learn how to be gods yourselves, and to be kings and priests, the same as all gods before you, namely by going from one small degree to another…" TIMES AND SEASONS August 1, 1844, Joseph Smith, Jr.

WHAT THE BIBLE SAYS ABOUT POLYTHEISM:

The Jewish affirmation of faith, states the belief in One True God. This is known as Monotheism, all born-again believers are Monotheist.

1. THE BIBLE TEACHES MONOTHEISM

Before me there was no God formed, neither shall there be after me. Isa 43:10
The Lord our God is one Lord Deu 6:4
Thou shall have no other Gods before me. Exd 20:3
Beside me there is no God. Isa 44:6
There is no God I know not any. Isa 45:8
I am God and there is none like me. Isa 48:9

NOTE: The Mormon Church is a non-Christian Cult since they believe in more than one God.
