About the Authors

ERICH GRIESHABER is the founder of The Freedom Seminars, a non-denominational and independent effort to evangelize the cultist with biblical truth. Erich and his wife, Jean, were Jehovah's Witnesses for five years before resigning in October, 1974, and accepting Jesus Christ as their personal Lord and Savior.

The Grieshabers began their ministry by personally witnessing for Jesus in the homes of many Jehovah's Witnesses, on street corners, and in Kingdom Halls. Erich then began counseling the families and friends of those still in Watchtower bondage. After extensive research and study, Erich has become widely known and respected throughout the United States as a leading authority in the field of the cults and occult.

Erich and Jean have shared their testimony on major television and radio stations while holding seminars throughout the United States on the cults and occult.
