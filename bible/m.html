Our Mission

Our mission is to facilitate in-depth study of God's Word through an on-line interactive reference library continuously updated from the teachings and commentaries of selected pastors and teachers who hold to the conservative, historical Christian faith.

We desire to operate the Blue Letter Bible as a ministry. This is a zero revenue project. There will be no charge for any services. Development and programming labor, hardware and webspace, and this CD have been provided. We do not ask for donations, however, many people have expressed the desire to assist this ministry project. If the Lord is leading you in this direction, click on the following for more information. In addition, many individuals and ministries have donated content to the library. We wish to thank all of them and ask for God’s blessing upon their ministry.
