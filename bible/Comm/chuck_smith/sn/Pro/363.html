I. OFTEN REPROVED.
A. God reproves us.
1. He speaks to our hearts.
a. Warns against sin.
b. Warns against judgment.
B. Conscience reproves us.
1. Every time we sin:
a. Lie (white lie).
b. Steal (they owed it to me).
c. Cheat (everyone does it).
2. How we try to still our conscious.
C. Friends occasionally reprove us.
1. Parents.
2. Acquaintances.
3. Minister.
II. HARDENETH HIS NECK.
A. "I don't care what anyone says, I'll do it."
B. High Rigger.
III. SHALL SUDDENLY BE DESTROYED.
A. Bill Owens.
IV. THAT WITHOUT REMEDY.
A. As a tree falleth.
