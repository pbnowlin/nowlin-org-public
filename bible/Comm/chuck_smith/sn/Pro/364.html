"NO REMEDY"
Intro: Remedy is a cure. Doctors prescribe remedies for certain illnesses. Some things No Remedy.
I. HE BEING OFTEN REPROVED.
A. Heard over and over gospel message.
B. God reproves us.
1. Many harden their hearts to this reproof.
C. God's Spirit does not always strive with man.
II. SHALL SUDDENLY BE DESTROYED.
A. Life is uncertain.
1. One person dies every 16 seconds.
B. Belshazzar.
C. Mt. Pelee.
D. God promises sudden death for those who harden their hearts.
1. Can't depend on death bed repentance.
2. God never breaks promise.
III. DEATH WITHOUT REMEDY.
A. Tragic thought.
1. No hope through eternity.
B. Lost forever in hell.
1. I don't believe in hell.
2. Salvation Army Lassie.
C. Gnat around a cattle.
1. Reproof, bat him away.
2. Intoxicated by light finally plunges to fiery death.
