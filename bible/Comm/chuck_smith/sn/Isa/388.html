I. PRESIDENT TRUMAN'S RECENT STATEMENT.
A. This is in future.
B. At the present Joel 3:9,10.
1. Greatest armament race.
a. Russia for last five yrs. 50%.
b. U.S. now again begining to devote to war.
II. COMING WAR WILL DIFFER FROM ALL OTHERS.
A. Atomic weapons to be used.
1. Effects of bomb.
a. Death by concession.
b. Death by burning.
c. Death by flying fragments.
d. Death by radiation.
e. Death by perversion of cell structure.
f. Zech 14:12.
III. II PETER 3:10-14 END OF MILLIENIUM.
A. Rev. 6: 12-17 tells of results of this coming war.
