"GOD'S BLESSED MAN"
What it means to be blessed of God.
I. WHAT HE DOES NOT DO.
A. Walk in counsel of ungodly.
1. Advice cheap and plentiful.
2. Bad company is dangerous.
B. Stand in the way of sinners.
1. No hypocrisy here!
C. Sit in seat of scornful.
1. How terrible is a critical spirit.
2. You can find mistakes in everyone.
a. "Love covers a multitude of sins."
II. WHAT HE DOES DO.
A. Delights in law of the Lord.
1. Word of God thrills.
B. Meditates in Word day and night.
Ill. WHAT GOD DOES FOR HIM.
A. Plants him as a tree near rivers of water.
1. Rivers without water.
2. Solid life.
3. Healthy life.
4. Beautiful life.
B. Brings forth fruit through his life.
C. Sustains his life.
1. Leaf also shall not wither
D. Prospers him.
1. King Asa
