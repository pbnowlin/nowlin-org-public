"GOD'S APPOINTED TIMES"
I. GOD HAS SET A TIME TO RESTORE ISRAEL VS. 13.
A. Moses spoke of it in Deut.
1. Deut. 28:34 promise to scatter.
2. Deut. 30:3,4 promise to restore.
B. Ezekiel's vision of valley of dry bones.
C. Jeremiah 46:27,28.
D. Whole thrust of Hosea.
E. It would appear that, that set time is near.
1. The nation was reborn May 14th 1948.
a. Zech. 12 Jerusalem will become the center of attention.
b. Israel has declared Jerusalem her capitol.
2. Today tension is mounting in the Middle East.
a. Iran-Iraq war grinds on.
b. Syria and Jordan massing troops.
c. The whole area as a boiling cauldron.
II. GOD'S SET TIME FOR THE RETURN OF JESUS CHRIST.
A. Vs. 16 "When the Lord shall build up Zion...He shall appear in His GLORY!"
1. His first appearing was to endure the shame of the cross to redeem the world.
a. "He was to be despised and rejected by men."
b. Mocked, scourged, pierced, crucified.
2. He coming again in glory.
a. "Then shall they see the sign of the Son of Man coming in clouds and great glory!"
B. We see the way Israel is advancing today.
1. Turning the waste places into gardens.
2. Causing the deserts to blossom as a rose.
3. Filling the earth with fruit. Isa. 27:6
C. The set time for the return of Jesus Christ is near.
III. GOD HAS A SET-TIME FOR YOUR SALVATION.
A. "Behold, now is the accepted time." "Now is the day of salvation." II Cor 6:2
B. Isa. 55:6 "Seek ye the Lord while He may be found, call upon Him while He is near."
C. God's time for you is always now.
1. He wants to work in your life today.
IV. GOD'S APPOINTED TIME FOR YOUR DEATH. HEB. 9:27
"As it is appointed unto man once to die."
A. We are told to prepare for that day.
1. "Prepare to meet thy God."
2. The preparation necessary.
a. He is a Holy God.
b. I am a sinful man.
c. I can't stand in the presence of His holiness covered with sin.
V. GOD'S APPOINTED TIMES WILL COME.
