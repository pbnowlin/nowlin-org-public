"PRAISING GOD"
Intro: A Psalm of David... A continual occupation.
I. "I WILL BLESS THE LORD AT ALL TIMES."
A. There are times when it is easy.
1. That spontaneous reaction to His grace.
2. Experience in market.
B. There are times when praise is act of faith.
1. I cannot see His hand in a particular circumstance.
2. And being strong in faith, he gave glory to God.
3. Experience in Corona.
II. PRAISE IS CONTAGIOUS.
A. "The humble shall hear and be glad."
1. Aura & Alpha hoaky.
a. "No man lives unto himself."
b. My attitudes pushed off on others.
c. People with a continual heavy spirit.
d. People with happy light spirit.
B. "O magnify the Lord with me, and let us exalt His name together."
1. Public praise.
a. How not (drawing attention to self).
b. How to (in song).
III. CAUSES FOR PRAISE.
A. I sought the Lord and HE HEARD ME.
B. They looked and WERE LIGHTENED.
C. This poor man cried...SAVED from troubles.
