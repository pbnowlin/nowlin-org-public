"GOD IS FOR ME"
I. THE IMPORTANCE OF RIGHT CONCEPTS OF GOD.
A. Most of the concepts people have of God or His word or His Son, they have learned from His enemies.
1. From the beginning, Satan has sought to distort the character of God.
a. He suggested to Eve.
1. God has lied to you. "You will not die."
2. God is trying to keep you from something good.
3. God is not fair.
2. His attacks have not changed with the centuries.
a. Vicious attacks against God's word.
1. Blatant frauds and lies have been fostered by men of science to try to prove men evolved rather that was created.
2. They want you to believe that you evolved from the image of an ape rather than created in the image of God.
3. Despite the increasing evidence to the contrary through the research into the DNA cell they hold fast to their sinking ship.
4. Some have been quoted as saying "but if evolution is not true that leaves us with no alternative but God."
5. How well Paul described them. Rom. 1:21
b. All kinds of suggestions that God is not fair.
1. What about the savages?
2. Why so much suffering in the world?
3. How could a God of love send a man to hell? I hope you don't find out.
c. Men feel God's law is against their best interest God wants to keep you from the good life.
1. God said "Keep your mind pure."
2. Flee fornication.
B. The distorted views of God provoke wrong responses to God.
1. Man feels that God is against him.
a. Against your having joy.
b. See God like KGB.
c. Picture God as standing over them with club.
d. How distorted. When God said to Adam, "What have you done?" Not harsh voice of arresting officer. Sob of heartbroken Father.
e. See Jesus as He weeps over Jerusalem.
2. Man feels he cannot trust God.
a. This is the evil subtle suggest of the Positive Confession or prosperity heresy.
b. For my best good I must direct God according to my desires.
c. To commit yourself to God's will can result in sickness or poverty.
d. I always receive a certain amount of letters when I speak out against heresy. People send me their testimonies.
3. I cannot praise a God who I think is against me and I cannot trust.
II. DAVID SAID, "GOD IS FOR ME!"
A. I will praise His Word.
B. I will trust Him.
C. I will not be afraid what man will do.
D. I will praise Him.
E. Paul asked, "If God be for us..."
F. This truth only for His children.
The hand of the LORD is against the wicked
