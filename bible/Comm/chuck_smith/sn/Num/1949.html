Chapter 26
- Verse 33: Zelophehad had not sons, only daughters.
- Verse 52: Instructions concerning the apportionment of the land.
- Verse 61: The two sons of Aaron who were known for offering false fire.
Chapter 28
- Verse 3: The daily offering two lambs; one in morning, one in evening. 3 quarts finely ground flour, 3 pints oil. 3 pints of wine with each lamb.
- Verse 9: The Sabbath offering two lambs. 6 quarts of flour mixed with oil. Regular drink offering.
- Verse 11: The first of each month, two young bullocks, 1 ram, 7 lambs. 9 quarts of flour mixed with oil for each bull. 6 quarts of flour mixed with oil for ram. 3 quarts of flour mixed with oil for each lamb. 6 pints of wine with each bull. 4 pints with the ram. 3 pints for each lamb. 1 male goat for a sin offering.
Passover: The first day after is a Sabbath day and the beginning of the feast of unleavened bread and each day there is offered to the daily sacrifices, also the same offerings that were made on the first day of each month.
- Verse 26: Feast of Pentecost. Same as the first of the month offerings.
Chapter 29
- Verse 1: Trumpets. Same as the first day of month offering, only doubled. Total of 4 bullocks. Two rams. 16 lambs. Two goats. 24 quarts of flour mixed with oil. 68 pints of wine. Tenth day of 7th month: Regular two lambs of daily burnt offering. Same as 1st of month offerings. 1 bull for a sin offering for Aaron and family. 1 goat for sin offering for people.
- Verse 12: 15th day of month. Feast of Tabernacles: 13 bullocks. Two rams. 14 lambs. Nine quarts of flour for each bull. Six quarts for each ram. Three quarts for each lamb. One goat for sin offering.
Days of the Festival
- 2nd day of festival. 12 bulls. Two rams. 14 lambs. Plus goat for sin offering.
- 3rd Day 11 bullocks.
- 4th 10.
- 5th Nine plus 1 goat each day for sin offering.
- 6th Eight.
- 7th Seven.
