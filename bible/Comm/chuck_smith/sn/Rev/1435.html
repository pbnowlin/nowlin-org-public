a. "These signs shall follow."
b. "Ask whatsoever ye will in faith."

1. Covered our eyes.

3. Must be concerned for lost.

1. Takes work to win souls.
2. Takes more than belief in God's Word.

1. "He that thinketh, He standeth."
2. "Wrestle not against flesh and blood."
