"THE TWO WITNESSES."
I. THEIR TIMES.
A. Not gospel times (no traces of church).
1. Their ministry is that of judgment.
2. Call fire on enemies.
B. Evil times, elements of hell on earth.
C. Times of intense supernaturalism and miracles.
D. These are judgment times on earth.
II. THEIR WORK.
A. Witnesses (prophetic witnesses).
B. Angel speaking (my witnesses).
C. Exercise of judgment.
1. Fire.
2. Shut up heaven.
3. Water to blood.
4. Smite with plagues.
D. Ministers to the Jews.
E. Enoch perhaps to Gentiles - not a Jew.
III. THEIR END.
A. Immortal till work is done.
B. When work completed they are vanquished.
C. Dead bodies denied burial.
D. Great joy at their death. Vs. 9. All see dead bodies.
E. Bodies lie 3 1/2 days - 3 1/2 yrs.
F. They do not remain dead - great fear.
G. They ascend into heaven.
H. Earthquake on earth.
