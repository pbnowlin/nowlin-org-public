"NO PLACE TO HIDE."
I. WHAT A HIDING PLACE DOES.
A. Brings comfort in danger.
1. Danger minimized by hiding place.
a. Storm cellars in midwest.
B. Encourages the pursued.
1. David pursued by Saul.
a. "The Lord is my refuge."
C. Gives shelter and safety to helpless.
1. Air shelters.
II. REPORT ON A BOMB AT BIKINI BEACH.
A. The awfulness; there was no place to hide.
1. Radioactivity all over.
2. Sand from bottom of ocean floor.
III. THE GREAT DAY OF THE LORD.
A. True, He is God of love and mercy.
1. Also has another side.
a. Judgment and wrath.
