LIFE AT SEED TIME
I. RESULT OF CERTAIN SOWING TERRIBLE.
A. Reap the Whirlwind
1. Sow wild oats -- de bauched, drunken.
2. Immoral theories go far beyond original intent.
a. Ajullah.
3. Heresies in church lead to unexpected evils.
a. Young man that devil made flies.
b. Joe Jeffries.
4. Tolerance of sin in family.
a. Eli.
5. Tolerance of sin in self.
II. RESULT OF CERTAIN SOWING FAILURE.
A. "It hath no stalic."
1. Self conceit endeavors vainly to produce reports.
2. Self righteousness tries to produce salvation.
3. Human wisdom tries to make new Gospel.
4. Mere idlers and talkers effect to be useful all talk no stalk.
III. RESULT OF SOME SOWING UNSATISFACTORY "THE BUD SHALL YIELD NO MEAL."
A. No real fruit from life.
1. Lived for fame, found vanity.
2. Lived for self, found misery.
Without God, nothing wise.
