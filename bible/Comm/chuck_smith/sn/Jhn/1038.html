"A FEARFUL AWAKENING."
I. DEATH AND JUDGMENT, TWO REALITIES FOR SINNERS
"It is appointed unto man once to die, and after that the judgment."
A. Both inescapeable for sinner (wages of sin).
1. Some think they can escape judgment.
B. Both bypassed by believer.
1. Death is to be present with the Lord.
a. "Fallen asleep."
b. "I am the Resurrection and the Life."
2. Judgment for unbeliever.
a. John 3:18; 5:24
II. JUDGMENT SCENE (Rev. 20:11-15).
A. Not a trial (I will not be asked questions).
1. No chance to give a defense. "The books are opened and judgment comes from these."
B. Judgment is final!
1. God's sentence cannot be appealed.
2. God allows man to rebel now, also Satan.
C. Judgment to be based on this:
1. Works, degrees of guilt.
2. Name in book of life.
3. Newgate Prison.
