I. CHRIST.
A. Who He is.
1. Creator.
2. Sustainer.
3. Adored One.
4. Ruler of universe.
B. Why He came? (Not to condemn).
1. World was lost.
2. Satan was in control.
3. To save the world.
a. Save from what?
1. Sin and its consequences.
II. GREATEST SIN OR CONDEMNATION?
A. Rejecting Christ.
1. He is the only hope of salvation.
2. Without Him we are lost.
a. Bottom of shaft.
b. Perishing in ocean.
B. Already condemned.
1. No further indictment needed.
2. No need for trial.
a. However, God will allow this.
III. HEB. 10:26-31.
A. No excuse.
