I. FIVE OUTLETS OF SPIRITUAL POWER.
A. Life.
B. Lips.
C. Service. Half an hour of life in India.
D. Money.
E. Prayer.
F. He will do through prayer what He otherwise could not do. Opens a universe.
II. PRAYER; A SPIRITUAL CONFLICT.
A. Rightful Prince is Jesus.
B. Pretender who is like Saul.
C. Force versus love.
D. Man spirit being (Embodied).
1. Real conflicts of Spiritual sort.
E. Satan is a spirit being (Unembodied).
1. God does not use force.
2. Satan's chief weapon.
3. Spiritual conflict, he is at disadvantage defeated.
III. PRAYER PROJECTING ONE'S SPIRIT PERSONALITY.
A. Prayer is a spirit force.
1. Takes on characteristic of one praying.
2. He is a spirit being so it becomes a spirit force.
3. Projecting personality.
B. Unembodied spirits capacities.
1. Flying in from Cuba.
2. Not limited by material obstacles
3. Prayer is really projecting my spirit, that is, projecting my real personality to the spot concerned and doing business there with other spirit beings.
4. Praying of Alaskans.
5. Praying for Missions.
IV. REWARDS OF PRAYER.
Dwight Moody & invalid.
