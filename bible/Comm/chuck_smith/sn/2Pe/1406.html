I. WHOSE DAMNATION SLUMBERETH NOT.
A. Atheist who asked God to strike him dead.
B. Minister who smokes cigarette while preaching.
C. People who mock, ridicule and curse God.
II. GOD TOO GOOD TO PUNISH ANYONE, ESPECIALLY ME.
A. Angels.
B. Old world (saved Noah).
C. Sodom & Gomorrha.
1. God hates your sin just as violently!
2. People suffering in hell, now God is less angry with.
3. Vengeance is mine fearful thing.
a. Your damnation slumbereth not.
III. THAT YOU ARE STILL LIVING AND NOT PUNISHED AS YET NO
CONDOLENCE. (Sins piling up as reservoir behind dam).
IV. I'VE KNOWN THE LORD AND HE WON'T PUNISH ME (vs. 20, 21...).
