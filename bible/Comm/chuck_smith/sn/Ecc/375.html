"GOD REQUIRETH THAT WHICH IS PAST"
I. OUR PAST IS FULL OF SIN.
A. Many would like to forget past.
B. Some try to reform for future.
1. Most generally find life too set in habit routine.
C. Say contrary true.
1. Able to effect complete reformation.
a. Gambler.
b. Drunk.
II. ONLY THING THAT WILL HELP PAST IS THE BLOOD OF CHRIST.
A. Sin born into us.
B. Sin overcomes us daily.
C. Only power to overcome sin in Christ.
