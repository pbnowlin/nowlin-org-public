I. NO ROOM IN THE INN.
A. Black mark in history.
1. Christ creator, prince of Glory.
a. Born in manger.
2. Cruel hearted man.
B. Surely had the Innkeeper known - story would be different.
1. I wonder.
II. CHRIST EXCLUDED EVEN TODAY.
A. Men know who He is.
B. They have heard of His love.
C. Yet refuse to give room in their hearts for Him.
1. Life too full of pursuit of pleasure "No room for Christ."
a. World always seeking pleasure.
2. Too busy amassing wealth. "No room for Christ."
3. Too busy with my friends. "No room for Christ."
D. What is more important than Christ.
III. CHRISTMAS TIME ROOM WOULD BE MADE FOR HIM.
A. Replaced by Santa Claus.
