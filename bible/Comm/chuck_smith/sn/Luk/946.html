"WHERE IS YOUR FAITH?"
I. FAITH IS THE ABSOLUTE ESSENTIAL TO SALVATION.
A. "Without faith it is impossible to please God."
B. "Believe on the Lord Jesus Christ and thou shalt."
C. John 3:16.
D. "Where is your faith?" We trust man more than God.
1. "I have great faith."
a. "Size of grain of mustard seed."
E. Many haven't enough faith for healing.
F. Many haven't enough faith for finances.
G. If you don't have enough faith for the little things how can you have enough for salvation.
II. INWARD FAITH HAS AN OUTWARD EXPRESSION.
A. Hence question "Where is your faith?
B. James says "Show me your words."
C. These signs shall follow them.
1. Where is your faith.
III. "LORD INCREASE OUR FAITH."
A. "Faith cometh by hearing."
B. We don't read our Bibles enough.
C. I say and I can prove you can't be a Christian.
