"THE WAY OF REVIVAL"
I. MANIFESTATION OF THE SPIRIT NEEDED TO DRAW MEN.
A. Witness of what you have seen and heard.
1. Too many haven't seen anything.
2. Witnessing as to what you have seen God do.
a. A person's curiosity aroused.
3. Woman of Samaria, "Come see a man."
B. To convince man.
1. Text.
2. Acts 13, Paul, Barnabus, Isle of Paphos.
a. When he saw what was done, believed.
3. All the arguing in the world will not convince like one miracle.
4. Philip to Natagnel to answer doubt, come and see.
C. To convict man.
1. Too many times we try to do this.
2. The work of the Spirit will do this.
a. When they come in they feel the presence of God.
b. Nothing is hid before Him.
