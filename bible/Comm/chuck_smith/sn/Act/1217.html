"WHAT'S NEW?"
I. WORD "NEW" ADJECTIVE OF EVERY GENERATION.
A. Use it in all fields.
1. Cars "Buy a new one why?" "Old one worn out?"
2. Hairstyles.
B. Tragic when religion approached this way.
1. "New faith for a new day."
2. Apostles creed, Bishop said he could still sing it as a piece of liturgy, but he could no longer speak it from his heart.
C. New theological ideas of the Bible.
1. Liberalism of 25 years ago outmoded.
II. THE IMPORTANCE OF UNCHANGING STANDARDS.
A. N B. of standard weights and measures.
1. Confusion if standards not mentioned.
2. Farmer in England.
B. Christ and His word unchanging.
1. Some think that these old truths are new.
