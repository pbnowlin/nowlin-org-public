Intro. Looking at characteristics of the men that God used, men of prayer; men of faith; men who had come to the cross in their own lives. Now we see another characteristic; they were men of the word.
I. NOTE HOW PETER HERE BEGINS TO POINT OUT THE TRUTHS OF THE SCRIPTURES TO THE PEOPLE.
A. In those days they did not have family Bibles.
1. The scriptures were all handwritten.
2. They were written by separate books.
3. The books were in scrolls.
4. They did not have concordances.
5. The scriptures had to be in their hearts.
6. It is interesting to me how that Peter quoted at length from several of the books of the Old Testament in the different sermons that he preached.
B. As he wanted to call to their minds the prophecy of Moses in Deuteronomy, he could not just look up the passage in a book, nor could he say, hand me the Deuteronomy scroll, John. It had to be in his heart.

III. HOW TO STUDY THE BIBLE.
A. Get an understanding of the whole picture. A good survey of the Bible.
1. It is necessary to see the whole picture to better understand the parts.
2. One of the great problems is that people come with an isolated scripture usually taken out of context, and declare their difficulty of understanding the scripture. Or they want you to explain the passage apart from the context.
B. Seek the help and guidance of the Holy Spirit to help you to understand.
1. Jesus promised that the Comforter which was the Holy Spirit who the Father would send in His name, that He would teach us all things and bring all things to our remembrance which He had spoken.
1JO 2:27 But the anointing which ye have received of him abideth in you, and ye need not that any man teach you: but as the same anointing teacheth you of all things, and is truth, and is no lie, and even as it hath taught you, ye shall abide in him.
