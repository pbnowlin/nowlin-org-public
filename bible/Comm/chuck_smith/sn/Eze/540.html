I. "WHAT GOD HAS SPOKEN SHALL COME TO PASS."
A. Many places God assures the certainty of His Word.
1. Consistent with logic - for as God He is:
a. All knowing - past, present, future.
b. Absolute truth & purity.
c. Eternal - "that which shall be has already been."
B. Thus when God speaks we had better pay attention.
II. "THE WORD OF THE LORD CAME TO ME SAYING," - 26:1-14.
A. Seven things are spoken of Tyre.
1. Nebuchadnezzar will take the city. 1-2
2. Other nations to help fulfill prophesy. 1-5
3. City to be made flat like top of rock. 1-2000
4. Become a place to spread nets. 1-10
5. Stones & timbers laid in the sea. 1-10
6. Other cities fear at fall of Tyre. 1-10
7. Old city never to be rebuilt. 1-20
1. in 400,000,000
III. WHEN GOD SPEAKS WE MUST TAKE HEED.
A. Romans 6:23.
B. Acts 4:12.
