"GOD SOUGHT FOR A MAN & FOUND NONE"
I. TO LEAD CHILDREN OF ISRAEL OUT OF EGYPT.
A. Turned to Moses in wilderness.
1. "O Lord, I am not eloquent..."
II. TO PROPHESY TO HIS PEOPLE.
A. Isaiah.
1. God had to wait until his idol died before he could see Him.
III. JONAH TO WARN NINEVAH - "IN THE YEAR KING UZZIAH DIED, I SAW THE LORD."
IV. TO PREACH GOSPEL OF GRACE TO GENTILES.
A. Peter & disciples too bound with old customs.
1. Had to call Paul & smite him down.
V. TO CONFORM COMPLETELY TO HIS WILL.
A. To stand in the gap.
