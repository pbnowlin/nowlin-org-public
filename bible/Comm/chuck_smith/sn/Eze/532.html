I. "THEREFORE I WILL JUDGE YOU."
A. The highest court of the Universe.
1. Carl Chessman trickery, maneuvering.
2. No appeal beyond.
B. I will.
1. Judgment is yet future.
2. Judgment is certain.
3. Some feel a false ease.
II. ALTERNATIVE.
A. Interior.
1. Repent.
a. Not remorse.
b. Sorry enough to quit.
B. Exterior.
1. Turn from transgressions.
a. Disobedience to God's will.
III. SO SIN SHALL NOT BE YOUR RUIN.
A. Sin has ruined many lives of great potential.
a. Saul - Lust, jealousy, uncontrolled temper.
b. Samson - Lust, bad environment.
c. Solomon - Idolatry - "loved wives more than God."
B. For why will ye die?
a. Eventual result of sin.
1. SPIRITUAL DEATH!
