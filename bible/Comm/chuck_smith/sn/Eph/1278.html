I. PAUL DESIRED THAT THEY MIGHT UNDERSTAND HIS KNOWLEDGE IN THE MYSTERY OF CHRIST.
A. This knowledge he did not receive from man but by direct revelation from the Lord.
B. When you have received from the Lord an understanding of an area of scripture, it is your desire to share with others that they might experience the blessings that you received.
1. I can remember how when I began to understand a little of the grace of God, how blessed I was. I was freed from years of legalism.
2. Just to know that God loved me and excepted me in spite of all my faults and shortcomings.
3. I was so blessed and filled with the message of grace that I desired to share it with everyone.
C. A large part of the epistle is devoted to the subject of the mystery that God was now revealing to the prophets and apostles by the Spirit.
1. This is why Paul refers to "When you read."
2. This was a letter meant to read to the whole church at Ephesus.
II. THIS MYSTERY WAS NOT MADE KNOWN DURING THE OLD TESTAMENT PERIOD.
A. There were some hints, indicating that some of the Gentiles would be saved, but the idea that they could become fellow heirs was a totally foreign concept to the Jew.

B. Quite often that which is referred to as a mystery in the New Testament, means that it is a truth that God had not revealed in previous times but is now making known.
1. Jesus said,
MAT 13:17 For verily I say unto you, That many prophets and righteous men] have desired to see [those things] which ye see, and have not seen [them]; and to hear [those things] which ye hear, and have not heard [them].
2. Paul said,
ROM 16:25 Now to him that is of power to stablish you according to my gospel, and the preaching of Jesus Christ, according to the revelation of the mystery, which was kept secret since the world began,
3. Peter said,
1PE 1:10 Of which salvation the prophets have inquired and searched diligently, who prophesied of the grace [that should come] unto you:
1PE 1:11 Searching what, or what manner of time the Spirit of Christ which was in them did signify, when it testified beforehand the sufferings of Christ, and the glory that should follow.
1PE 1:12 Unto whom it was revealed, that not unto themselves, but unto us they did minister the things, which are now reported unto you by them that have preached the gospel unto you with the Holy Ghost sent down from heaven; which things the angels desire to look into.
B. Is now revealed to the prophets and apostles by the Spirit.
1. This revelation of the mystery was not something that was given exclusively to Paul.
2. Peter was the first one to whom it was revealed.
a. His vision on the rooftop of the house of Simon.
b. Do not call unclean, what I have cleansed.
c. He was apprehensive when he came to house of Cornelius, a Gentile, a Roman soldier.
ACS 10:28 And he said unto them, Ye know how that it is an unlawful thing for a man that is a Jew to keep company, or come unto one of another nation; but God hath shewed me that I should not call any man common or unclean.
3. Though Peter opened the door to the Gentiles, it was Paul that truly invited them all to come in.

III. THE THREE THINGS THAT GOD HAS GRANTED TO THE GENTILES.
A. That they should be fellowheirs.
ROM 8:15 For ye have not received the spirit of bondage again to fear; but ye have received the Spirit of adoption, whereby we cry, Abba, Father.
ROM 8:16 The Spirit itself beareth witness with our spirit, that we are the children of God:
ROM 8:17 And if children, then heirs; heirs of God, and jointheirs with Christ; if so be that we suffer with [him], that we may be also glorified together.
1. We sing "Children of God O glorious calling." But do you realize just how glorious that is?
2. "Beloved now are we the sons of God, but it does not yet appear what we shall be, but we know that when He shall appear we shall be like Him, for we shall see Him as He is."
3. I am that child of the King.
4. My destiny is to rule and reign with Him.
5. I am an heir to vast riches beyond man's capacity to imagine.
"He owns the cattle on a thousand hills,
the wealth in every mine.
He owns the rivers and the rocks and rills,
the sun, the stars that shine.
Wonderful riches more than tongue can tell,
He is my Father so they're mine as well,
He owns the cattle on a thousand hills,
And I know He will care for me.
GAL 3:29 And if ye [be] Christ's, then are ye Abraham's seed, and heirs according to the promise.
GAL 4:6 And because ye are sons, God hath sent forth the Spirit of his Son into your hearts, crying, Abba, Father.
GAL 4:7 Wherefore thou art no more a servant, but a son; and if a son, then an heir of God through Christ.
B. We are of the same body.
GAL 3:26 For ye are all the children of God by faith in Christ Jesus.
GAL 3:27 For as many of you as have been baptized into Christ have put on Christ.
GAL 3:28 There is neither Jew nor Greek, there is neither bond nor free, there is neither male nor female: for ye are all one in Christ Jesus.
COL 3:11 Where there is neither Greek nor Jew, circumcision nor uncircumcision, Barbarian, Scythian, bond [nor] free: but Christ is] all, and in all.
1. There is only one body of Christ. It is made up of all true believers in Jesus Christ, Jesus has broken down every racial barrier that man has ever built up against some one of another color or race.
2. There are always those groups who are seeking to make exclusive claims, as though they were the only true church. That is one of the signs of cultism. But it is found in Baptist churches, as well as the Church of Christ and many others.
3. That idea of if you leave us you are leaving Christ, and your salvation is fallacious.
4. May we ever be held together by love, and never by fear.
ROM 12:4 For as we have many members in one body, and all members have not the same office:
ROM 12:5 So we, [being] many, are one body in Christ, and every one members one of another.
1CO 12:12 For as the body is one, and hath many members, and all the members of that one body, being many, are one body: so also [is] Christ.
C. That we are partakers of His promise in Christ by the gospel.
GAL 3:14 That the blessing of Abraham might come on the Gentiles through Jesus Christ; that we might receive the promise of the Spirit through faith.
1. What is that promise?

1JO 5:13 These things have I written unto you that believe on the name of the Son of God; that ye may know that ye have eternal life, and that ye may believe on the name of the Son of God.
IV. THESE ARE THE THREE ASPECTS OF THIS MYSTERY.
A. That the Gentiles are fellow heirs with the Jews.
B. That we are all in the same body, the body of Christ.
C. That we can all partake of His promise in Christ.
