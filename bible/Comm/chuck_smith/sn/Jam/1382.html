DOERS OF THE WORD.
I. "BE YE DOERS OF THE WORD."
A. Hearing alone does not justify.
1. Christ said "He that hath my command..."
2. Rom. 2:13, Jews mistaken notion.
a. Just having the law doesn't save.
B. Our actions, not our words are counted.
1. Parable, Matt. 21:28-32.
2. Matt. 7:21.
C. Just having full Gospel message is not enough.
II. DECEIVING YOUR OWN SELVES.
A. Meeting Abraham Lincoln.
B. Working in sanitarium.
1. Many who deceived themselves.
III. LOOKING INTO MIRROR.
A. See all flaws and imperfections.
1. Near-sighted man in picture gallery.
IV. TO HAVE REVIVAL WE MUST:
A. Pray more.
B. Give more energy to God.
C. Study the Word more.
D. Give more time to God.
E. Make Christ most important one in life.
