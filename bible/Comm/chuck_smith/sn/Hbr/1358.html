"CHRIST ABLE TO SAVE TO THE UTTERMOST"
I. FIND ONE TO DEAL WITH.
A. Prayer.
B. Uncle John Vassar.
II. BEGIN A CONVERSATION.
A. At altar.
B. Other places.
1. "Do you think life is worth living?"
2. What is the best thing in life?
3. Handing a good tract.
III. FIND OUT WHERE THEY STAND.
A. Asking him question.
B. Watching his face.
1. Face revealing.
C. Observing tone and manner.
1. Many times they tell you they are saved.
2. On what do you base your hope?
D. By Holy Spirit.
IV. LEAD HIM AS DIRECTLY AS POSSIBLE TO CHRIST.
A. Primary purpose.
1. Not joining church.
2. Give up habits.
V. SHOW HE HAS FORGIVENESS.
VI. SHOW HIM HOW TO MAKE SUCCESS.
