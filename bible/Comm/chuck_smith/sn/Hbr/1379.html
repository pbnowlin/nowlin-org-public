I. HEALING AND MIRACLES.
A. Blind, lame, halt, deaf, etc.
B. Feeding of the 5,000.
C. Commanding the elements, wind and waves.
1. Faith to the grain of a mustard seed.
D. Forgiving sins.
II. ALSO SAME IN LIKES AND DISLIKES.
A. He loves the:
1. Humble (Matt. 5).
2. Meek.
3. Merciful.
4. Hungry.
5. Pure in heart.
6. Children and a child-like faith.
B. He hated:
1. Hypocrisy (Matt. 23).
2. Half-hearted.
a. Except a man forsake, etc. not worthy.
III. HE KNEW MEN (John 1).
A. Knew the thoughts and hearts (sin against Holy Spirit).
B. Woman of Samaria.
IV. O JERUSALEM, JERUSALEM.
A. "He will take you under His wings."
