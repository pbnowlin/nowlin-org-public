"THE SIN OF NEGLECT"
I. THE WARNING.
A. Give more earnest heed.
1. Not a time for slacking off.
2. Not a time for easing off.
B. "Lest at anytime we should let them drift away."
1. The subtlety with which Satan works.
2. The drifting is slow and almost imperceptible.
II. WHAT IT MEANS TO NEGLECT SALVATION.
A. Refusing to act.
1. Man drowning, thrown a life buoy.
B. Written to Christians.
1. Do nothing about it.
a. Drift away from communicating with God.
b. Drift away from reading the Word.
c. Drift away from assembling together.
