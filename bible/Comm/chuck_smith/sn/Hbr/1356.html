I. THE PERILS OF UNBELIEF.
A. It keeps us man-centered instead of God-centered.
1. We measure by human resources.
B. It is destructive.
C. It is contagious.
1. Ten spies.
II. THE FOLLY OF UNBELIEF.
A. It robs us of God's blessings.
1. Rest.
2. Joy.
3. Provision.
4. Salvation.
B. It produces opposite reactions.
1. I'm weeping when I should be rejoicing.
2. I'm complaining when I should be thanking.
3. (Jacob) Cursing when I should be praising.
III. THE CAUSE OF UNBELIEF.
A. Satan "hath God said..."
1. "The god of this world hath blinded..."
IV THE CURE OF UNBELIEF.
A. Prayer.
B. The Word of God.
