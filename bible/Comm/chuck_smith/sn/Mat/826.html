"LORD, IS IT I?"
I. SELF EXAMINATION.
A. If they were like us.
1. James and John, certain it was Peter.
2. Peter knew not Him.
3. ("50 let a man examine Himself").
B. Read so many scriptures that fit the other fellow.
1. Bible should be a mirror. Paint critic.
II. BETRAYING CHRIST.
A. By our actions.
1. Hypocrite.
B. By our words.
1. Profanity.
C. By what we don't do.
1. Letting men blaspheme Him in our presence.
III. SELLING CHRIST.
A. Look with scorn upon Judas...30 pieces of silver.
B. People selling Christ today, for much less.
1. Worldly pleasures (Moses chose to suffer).
2. Pride.
3. Popularity.
