I. ALL OBSERVING FATHER.
A. He sees our secret acts.
1. Emphasis on secret service.
2. Prayer, alms, fasting.
3. Not to spy out, but to reward.
II. ALL KNOWING FATHER vs. 8:32.
A. Knows our physical needs.
1. Ask and ye shall receive.
a. Alexander the Great.
2. Result - our prayers to differ from heathen.
a. Baal.
1. Cry louder.
b. Heard for much speaking.
III. ALL GIVING FATHER CHAP. 7:11.
A. One who delights in doing so.
1. Fathers and giving.
a. Delight in doing so.
