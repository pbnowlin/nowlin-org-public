I. MOST IMPORTANT QUESTIONS IN LIFE.
A. Personal Question.
1. Everyone must answer it.
2. No one can evade it.
3. Choose yet this day whom.
B. It is important because.
1. How you answer it determines the course of your life.
2. How you answer it determines how you will spend eternity.
3. Christ is waiting to record your answer ye shall serve.
II. RESULTS IN THE LIVES OF OTHERS WHO HAVE ANSWERED.
A. Pilate "I wash my hands."
1. Impossible!
B. Jews "Crucify him."
1. 40 years later Titus with Roman troops.
C. Voltaire "Curse the wretch."
1. Doctor and nurse testify died in agony of Hell.
D. Paine "Impostor!"
1. Died screaming for God's mercy.
E. Hobbs.
1. Dying words "I am about to take my last journey, a great leap into the dark."
F. Paul the Apostle "For me to live is Christ."
1. "O grave, where is thy victory."
2. "I have fought a good fight."
G. Stephen.
1. "Lord receive my Spirit." "Lay not this sin to their charge."
III. HOW CAN YOU ANSWER THE QUESTION.
A. Receive or Reject:
1. Receive.
a. John 1:12.
2. Reject.
B. Believe it or not:
1. Believe on Him.
a. John 3:16.
2. Disbelieved.
a. John 3:36.
b. John 3:18 "He."
C. Confess or Deny:
1. Confess.
a. Rom 10:9.
b. Matt. 10:32.
2. Deny.
a. Matt. 10:33.
