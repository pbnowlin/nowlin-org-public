II. THE CHURCH AT PERGAMOS

A. Unlike Ephesus and Smyrna, Pergamos was not a port city and it was not known for it's commerce!

1. Pergamos was known for it's religion!!!!

a) You could find some of the world's finest temples built in honor of their many gods.

b) Some of the biggies were Zeus, Aphrodite, and Aesculapius.

(1) The people of Pergamos love to worship the "scalpel"!

(a) Well, not exactly, they worshipped the god of medicine, Aesculapius!

(b) It is from this name that we get our word scalpel!

(c) It is ironic that this god of medicine was worshipped in the form of a serpent.

(d) This serpent was usually wrapped around a staff!

(e) This "symbol" of medicine has become common in every hospital and Dr.'s office around theworld!!
