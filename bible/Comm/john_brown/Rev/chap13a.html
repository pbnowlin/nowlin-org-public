The Beastly Trinity

Revelation 13:1-18

I. INTRODUCTION

A. Last week we studied the war that will take place in Heaven as Michael the Archangel heads up an angelic army against "the prince of the power of the air" (Eph 2:2) and his demons.

1. Michael wins of course, and satan is kicked out of the heavenlies and confined to the earth.

2. That's good news for those in heaven, but satan pulls out all the stops on earth knowing "that he has a short time." Rev 12:12

3. This morning's study is a result of satan's "pulling all the stops".

a) satan empowers two great warriors in this battle and one's mission is to cause the other to be worshipped as god!

b) They are referred to as, "beasts" here in chap. 13.

c) But later, in chaps. 16, 19, and 20 the latter beast is referenced as "the false prophet."

d) So we have before us "the beastly trinity" of the "dragon (which is satan Rev 12:9, the "beast", and the "false prophet"!

(1) As we study them and their "ministries", you will see their "copycat" resemblence to the Holy Trinity of God!

(a) satan has always wanted to be worshipped as god!

(b) The "beast" is a "logos" of satan! He is a physical representative of his!

(c) And lastly, the "false prophet" glorifies the "beast".

II. TEXT

A. Rev 13:1-2 1 Then I stood on the sand of the sea. And I saw a beast rising up out of the sea, having seven heads and ten horns, and on his horns ten crowns, and on his heads a blasphemous name. 2 Now the beast which I saw was like a leopard, his feet were like the feet of a bear, and his mouth like the mouth of a lion. The dragon gave him his power, his throne, and great authority. (NKJV)

1. The two witnesses have already been slain by the beast!

2. Daniel calls him "the prince that shall come" (Dan 9:26).

3. Isaiah calls him "the wicked!" (Isa 11:4)

4. Chrst called him "the abomination of desolation" (Mat 24:16)

5. Paul labeled him as "the man of sin, the son of perdition" in 2Th 2:3

6. Just prior to this, John had been watching the "woman" and the "dragon" when all of a sudden, he is standing on a seashore observing a beast rising out of the sea similar in appearance to the dragon!

a) But it is not the dragon! Rev 12:9 told us that the dragon was satan himself.

b) This beast "rising up out of the sea" is symbolic! It represents a man, as v.8 tells us the people of earth will physically worship him!

c) He get his power from the dragon Rev 13:2.

7. Usually the sea referred to in scripture is the Mediterranean.

a) Therefore it is commonly assumed that this beast-man will come from one of the lands surrounding this great sea.

b) The seven heads and ten horns represent power and authority and in this case: a league of nations and leaders that are ultimately under the power of satan!

(1) Daniel saw the same vision and interpreted the lion as the Babylonian empire, the bear as the Medo-Persian empire, and the leopard as the Grecian empire!

(a) All of which were yet future, but history has proven this prophecy correct!

(b) All three of these "empires" have ruled or dominated God's people in some way!

(c) Here John sees them in reverse order since he is "looking back" over history.

c) Please keep in mind that out of this "league" will rise a very charismatic leader. This man is the beast. And he is head of this league!

B. Rev 13:3-4 3 And I saw one of his heads as if it had been mortally wounded, and his deadly wound was healed. And all the world marveled and followed the beast. 4 So they worshiped the dragon who gave authority to the beast; and they worshiped the beast, saying, "Who is like the beast? Who is able to make war with him?" (NKJV)

1. Scripture doesn't tell us from which nation this "head" will be!

2. We do know however that his nation will best suit the nature and purposes of the dragon which empowers him!

a) It is interesting to note that Sadam Hussein has spent millions of dollars rebuilding Babylon!

b) Babylon in modern Iraq is also the geographical "center" of all the world's land masses!

(1) Wherever and whichever this nation is, it will also become the "center" of worship.

(2) All the nations as well as their respective religions will unite as they see this "suedo" death and resurrection of the beast!

(3) With all the flying angels and demons and the cosmic disturbances, no one will any longer doubt the existance of God!

(4) It is just that many will choose satan as god since he appears to be the most powerful!

(a) After all, he had power to overcome God's two witnesses, and now seemingly death!!

C. Rev 13:5-6 5 And he was given a mouth speaking great things and blasphemies, and he was given authority to continue for forty-two months. 6 Then he opened his mouth in blasphemy against God, to blaspheme His name, His tabernacle, and those who dwell in heaven. (NKJV)

1. During this last 3 1/3 years, the beast will curse and defame the Lord, His holiness, His love, His law, His grace, and His people!!

a) He has but a short time and can no longer stand before the Lord and accuse the brethren, so he turns it loose on those remaining on the earth.

b) No doubt, to prevent any who might be thinking of giving their heart to the Lord!

D. Rev 13:7-10 7 It was granted to him to make war with the saints and to overcome them. And authority was given him over every tribe, tongue, and nation. 8 All who dwell on the earth will worship him, whose names have not been written in the Book of Life of the Lamb slain from the foundation of the world. 9 If anyone has an ear, let him hear. 10 He who leads into captivity shall go into captivity; he who kills with the sword must be killed with the sword. Here is the patience and the faith of the saints. (NKJV)

a) Rom 8:29-30 29 For whom He foreknew, He also predestined to be conformed to the image of His Son, that He might be the firstborn among many brethren. 30 Moreover whom He predestined, these He also called; whom He called, these He also justified; and whom He justified, these He also glorified. (NKJV)

b) The Lord knows everyone who will accept Him, and their names are written in the book of life! These saints from the tribulation will not bow, at the cost of their own lives (he "overcame them")!

c) All the rest of the earth dwellers will bow and worship the beast.

d) John begs, "Listen with your heart! (he who has ears)"

(1) If any man has had a difficult time making a decision up 'til now, the beast is about to force it!

(2) John reassures the saints in v.10 that those responsible for the death of the saints will likewise be put to death! You see, the dispensation of grace has ended!

III. CONCLUSION

A. Rev 13:18 Here is wisdom. Let him who has understanding calculate the number of the beast, for it is the number of a man: His number is 666. (NKJV)

1. The beast is a man!

2. That man has a name!

3. His name has a numerical equivalent!

4. We are expected to count the number of this man's name!

a) In English this seems weird, but in the Hebrew or the Greek language and in some cases Latin, it is not strange at all!

b) You see, each symbol used for the alphabet was also used for a number!

c) For example, in Latin V=five, X=10, L=50, and so on!

d) In Greek, the language of the New Testament, alpha = 1, beta = 2, iota = 10, rho = 100!

e) To "count" the number of the beast one would simply add up the numbers attatched to the letters in his name!

5. Who is this man? No one knows!

a) Many people could have names that the letters equal 666, but only one will rule the world!
