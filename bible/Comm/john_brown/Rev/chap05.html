I. INTRODUCTION

A. Dan 12:8-9 8 Although I heard, I did not understand. (His vision of future events) Then I said, "My lord, what shall be the end of these things?" 9 And he said, "Go your way, Daniel, for the words are closed up and sealed till the time of the end. (NKJV)

1. The meaning of the sealed scroll is in the Old Testament book of Jeremiah:

a) Jeremiah's cousin Hanameel, decided to sell his land to Jeremiah because he figured that when Nebucadnezzar conqured them the land would be confiscated anyway.

b) While Jeremiah was in jail for giving this prophecy, the Lord told him to accept his cousin's offer and buy the land because one day they would return and that way it would be preserved for his heirs.

(1) The court officials were called and the title deed was made out and the transaction was completed.

(2) The deed was rolled up into a scroll and sealed and could only be opened by the rightful owner!

(3) The deal was not fully consumated until the owner came forth to break the seals and claim his land.

(a) It took Jeremiah 70 years!

(i) Psa 24:1...The earth is the LORD's, and all its fullness, The world and those who dwell therein. (NKJV)

(b) The church has been raptured, now it is time to redeem all that was His from the beginning.

(i) satan is called the "god of this world" in Corinthians but he is a usurper. He doesn't hold the title deed!

(ii) This "scroll" is the title deed to the earth!!

(iii) And the rightful owner is here to break the seals and to take possession!
