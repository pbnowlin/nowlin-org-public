On a crowded street in one of our large cities, a young man was snatched from the path of a speeding truck, his life saved by a stately looking man. Still breathless from fright, the youth thanked the man for saving his life, and soon the man was lost in the crowd.

Two weeks later in a crowded courtroom, an anxious young man stood before the bench to be sentenced for murder. The Judge asked, "Young man, have you anything to say before the sentence of death is passed upon you?"

"Why, Yes! Yes, Judge," the youth responded, "You know me!"

A silence moved like a shock wave over the court room.

"I'm sorry, I cannot place you," said the Judge.

But the boy persisted, "You remember two weeks ago. At 7th Ave. and Camelback, you saved my life. Surely, Judge, you can do something to save me now."

The Judge said, "Young man, I do now remember you. But that day I was your savior. Today I am your judge."
