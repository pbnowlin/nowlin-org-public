The Prisoner of Rome – Act 21:18-28:31

The events in this section cover a period of about four years (58-62). During this time the apostle was a prisoner of the Roman government. The sovereignty of God prevailed in that the Gospel of Christ was given with power and authority.

  • Paul’s Witness in Jerusalem – Act 21:18-40; Act 22; Act 23:10
    • The Judaistic Christians – Act 21:18-26
      • They charged Paul with turning believers to forsake Moses and Jewish traditions
        • Paul had rejected for himself the Law of Moses and the customs of Judaism
        • He had not demanded that others do the same
        • He concurred with the conference (Act 15) that rites and ceremonies did not bring salvation
        • Paul was convinced that under the teachings of grace these observances would disappear
      • The request of the elders – Act 21:22-26
        • Submit to the vow of purification
        • Paul complied with their request
    • The non-Christian Jews – Act 21:27-31
      • The Jews from Asia stirred up hatred – Act 21:27
      • They brought false accusations – Act 21:28-29
      • They were intent on killing him – Act 21:30-31
    • The Roman authorities – Act 21:31-40
      • They rescued Paul from death
      • They discovered his identity
      • They permitted him to speak to the mob – Act 21:40
    • Paul’s message – Act 22:1-21
      • His defense against the charges
        • That he spoke against the Jewish traditions
        • That he spoke against the law of Moses
        • That he spoke against the temple
      • The sketch of his early religious life – Act 22:3-5
        • The conversion
        • The calling
          • Chosen
          • To know His will
          • To see Christ as righteous
          • To hear His word
          • To be His witness
        • The conversion, the calling and the message were all of divine origin
    • The effect of Paul’s message – Act 22:22-29
      • The rage of the Jews boiled over
      • The Romans took him into custody
    • Citizenship
      • Roman
      • Heavenly
        • Acquired through the new birth
        • It is the gift of God
        • It is sealed by the Spirit of God
  • Applications
    • What are the fixed points on which we must not give way?
    • What are the points which may be yielded?
