• The Background or Stephen’s Witness
    • The problem that developed
      • The problem - murmuring
        • The Helenists - Jews who spoke Greek
        • The Hebrews - Jews who spoke Aramaic
        • Murmuring
          • Complaining which spreads discontent
          • Complaining which spreads dissension
          • Complaining to the wrong people
          • Murmuring brought the judgment of God in the wilderness – Exd 15; Exd 16; Exd 17
      • The occasion: distribution of aid
        • The support of the widows
        • The charge of neglect by the Hellenists
    • The solution to the problem
      • The Apostles leadership – Act 6:2-4
        • It was direct - they acted
        • It was wise
          • They used other men
          • They allowed the gifts in the Body
          • They continued in their own calling - tithe, ministry of the Word, and prayer
      • The appointment of seven men
        • To take care of the needy
        • To free the Apostles from unnecessary duties
    • The qualifications of those appointed – Act 6:3
      • They were to be men, not women
      • They were to be from the group, that is, believers
      • They were to be of honest reputation
      • They were to be spiritual men
        • Carnal - a Christian who counts on something in himself
        • Spiritual - a Christian who is resting in Christ
      • They were to be men of wisdom
        • Able to apply Scriptural truth to practical situations
        • Wisdom is the use of common sense - Scriptural truth
      • The men selected were Hellenists
    • The appointment of the men – Act 6:6
      • They prayed together
      • The Apostles laid their hands on them - the act / of identification
    • The results – Act 6:7
      • The Word of God increased
      • The number of disciples multiplied greatly
      • A great many priests were obedient to the faith
      • Stephen did great miracles
        • The miracles were to confirm association with the Apostles
        • To confirm new things - the extension of ministry
        • The miracles did not continue after the Apostles and completion of Scripture
