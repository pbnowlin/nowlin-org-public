• The Ministry in Iconium – Act 14:1-6
    • They went together into the synagogue
    • They spoke boldly in the Lord
      • With a clear message
      • With a direct testimony
      • With a persuasive manner
    • They remained (6 months)
    • The results
      • The Lord confirmed the message
        • The physical signs
        • The spiritual miracles
      • Many believed
      • Many opposed
        • Subtle opposition
          • Questioning
          • Negative
          • Critical
          • Poisonous propaganda
        • Viscious-violent opposition
      • Departure to Lyconia
  • The Ministry at Lystra – Act 14:6-20
    • They preached the Gospel
    • The lame man was healed – Act 14:8-10
      • The man
        • He was crippled from birth
        • He was helpless
        • He heard Paul’s message and believed
      • Paul
        • He looked directly at the man
        • He discerned that the man was responding
        • He spoke with authority
      • The healing
        • The man obeyed the Gospel
        • The man leaped and walked
    • The results
      • The people begin to worship Paul and Barnabas as gods – Act 14:14
      • The response of Paul and Barnabas
        • They stopped them – Act 14:14
        • They corrected them – Act 14:15
        • They taught them – Act 14:15-18
          • Declared that they were human and refused worship
          • Exhorted them to turn from empty rituals and vain ceremonies
          • Referred to the living God
            • He has revealed Himself through creation
            • He has provided a source of joy
  • The Return Home to Antioch, Syria
    • They returned through Lystra, Iconium, and Antioch; places of persecution
    • Their ministry
      • To strengthen believers in their faith – by teaching the Word of God
      • To encourage believers in their faith – exhorting them to continue
      • To fortify them for suffering
      • To organize them for effective service – they appointed elders in every church
      • To pray with them and for them
    • The arrival at home – Act 14:27-28
      • The rehearsal of what God had done through them
      • The fulfillment through grace
