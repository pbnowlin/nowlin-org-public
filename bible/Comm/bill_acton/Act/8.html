• The Expansion of the Church – Act 8:1-5
    • The paragraph begins with persecution
    • The paragraph ends with proclamation
    • God uses opposition
      • The murder of Stephen by the Council
      • The rage of Saul

      • Christ rules in the midst of enemies
  • Philip in Samaria – Act 8:4-24
    • It was a transitional witness
      • Samaria is between Judea and Galilee
      • Samaritans are a bridge between Jews and Gentiles
    • It was a genuine ministry of the Spirit
      • There was response to the truth (Christ) – Act 8:5-6
      • There was the accompaniment of power
        • There were miracles of healing
        • The physical demonstrated the power of the spiritual
      • There was great joy in that city – Act 8:8

            • A special channel that others don’t have
          • Counterfeit power

            • The demonic produces miracles
