• The Ministry of Ananias – Act 9:10-19 – “Body Life”
    • His identity - “A disciple in Damascus”
    • His call and commission – Act 9:10-11
      • He was selected by the Lord
      • He was to communicate God’s will to Paul
      • His question to the Lord – Act 9:13-14
      • The Lord’s reply
        • “Go”
        • The message
          • Concerning Paul’s election – Act 9:15
          • Concerning Paul’s mission – Act 9:15-16
            • To speak to the world
              • Gentiles
              • Jews
              • Kings
            • To suffer in His name
      • The obedience of Ananias – Act 9:17
  • The Lord’s Working in Paul
    • “Behold he is praying” – Act 9:11
      • Prayer is a convincing evidence of spiritual resurrection
      • Prayer reveals a change in Saul
      • His praying
        • It was real
        • It was right
        • It was in Jesus’ name
    • Paul received his sight – Act 9:18
    • Paul was baptized
    • Paul was controlled by the Holy Spirit – Act 9:17
    • Paul was strengthened
    • Paul witnessed in Damascus – Act 9:20
      • It was immediate
      • It was faithful - proclaimed that Jesus is the Son of God
        • The Lordship of Christ
        • When you accept His authority, He becomes Saviour – Rom 10:9
      • It was effective – Act 9:21-22
    • Paul’s time in the Arabian desert “D.D. degree”
      • His time there could have taken place between Act 9:22-23
      • Gal 1:15-18 reveals that Paul was in Arabia before he conferred with any of the Apostles in Jerusalem
      • In the stillness of the Arabian desert, a Jewish Pharisee was transformed into the Apostle of Christ to the Gentiles
    • Paul back in Damascus – Act 9:23-25
      • The hatred of the Jews
      • The help of the disciples
      • The escape to Jerusalem
    • Paul in Jerusalem – Act 9:26-29
      • His first action - to join with the disciples
      • His cold reception
        • They were afraid of him
        • They did not believe that He was a disciple
      • His gracious friend, Barnabas
      • Paul’s bold witness – Act 9:29
    • Paul sent to Tarsus – Act 9:30 - He stayed there 7 or 8 years
  • The Churches of Judea, Samaria and Galilee – Act 9:31
    • They had rest and peace
    • They were increasing in number
    • They were being edified
