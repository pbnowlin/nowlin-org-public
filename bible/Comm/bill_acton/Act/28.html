• The Signs of an Apostle – 2Co 12:12
    • The poisonous snake miracle – Act 28:1-6
      • The people
        • Described as “barbarous”
          • They did not speak Greek
          • They were not savages
        • They treated the shipwrecked people with kindness
      • The snake bite – Act 28:3-5
        • Paul was gathering firewood
        • The poisonous snake clung to his arm
        • The people thought Paul was being punished for sin
          • Human ideas of justice
          • Human ideas of judgment
          • The explanation of Christ – Jhn 9
        • Paul suffered no harm
        • The people now thought Paul was “a god”
    • The healing of the father of Publius – Act 28:7-8
      • The man was sick
      • The healing was instantaneous and miraculous
    • The healing of the people – Act 28:9
      • The rest of the sick came to Paul
      • They were all healed
    • The principles involved
      • The gift of healing was an apostolic sign
      • The gift of miracles was an apostolic sign
      • The sign gifts ended with the apostles
      • No one today has the sign gifts
      • The sign confirmed the message of resurrection
    • The people honored the apostolic party - Act 28:10
      • Gratitude produces generosity
      • They loaded the ship with supplies
  • The Trip from Malta to Rome – Act 28:11-16
    • Three days on the Island of Sicily – Act 28:11-12
    • Landing at the port of Puteoli – Act 28:13-14
      • 130 miles from Rome near the city of Naples
      • Paul was met by Christians – Act 28:14
        • The miraculous witness of the Gospel
        • The wide spread of Christianity
    • On to Rome – Act 28:15-16
      • Two groups of Christians meet up with Paul
        • Some at the forum of Appius (40 miles from Rome)
        • Some from the three taverns (30 miles from Rome)
      • They welcomed him
      • They prayed with him
      • They encouraged him
  • In Rome – Act 28:17-31
    • Paul invites the local Jewish leader to meet with him
      • He explained his predicament – Act 28:17-23
      • He points out the real reason for Jewish opposition: for the hope of Israel
    • The next meeting – Act 28:23
      • Paul led in Bible study
      • The subject: The Lord Jesus Christ
      • The study of Moses and the prophecies
      • The result – Act 28:24
        • Some believed
        • Some did not believe
        • Paul’s observation of Isaiah’s word – Act 28:25-28
    • Two years in Rome – Act 28:30-31
      • Paul was preaching and teaching - The Lord Jesus Christ
      • Paul was “unhindered”
      • Paul wrote Philippians, Ephesians, Colossians and Philemon
