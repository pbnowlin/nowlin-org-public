• The Occasion – Act 3:1-10
    • The circumstances
      • Peter and John are going together to the Temple to pray
      • At the Temple gate there is a lame beggar
      • Peter and John were used by the Lord to give this man a miraculous healing
      • The result
        • The man was healed
        • The people were amazed
    • The spiritual principles taught by this miracle
      • A picture of the Church
        • Peter and John are together
        • They are going to pray
        • They stopped to help
        • They trusted the Lord
      • A picture of the world
        • The lame man reveals the helplessness of every man out of Christ
          • The lost condition of society
          • The total depravity of man
          • Lost in sin
        • The lame man reveals the Church’s opportunity
      • The Church’s gift
        • What Peter and John did not do
          • They did not give “silver and gold”
          • They did not give a scientific philosophy of feet and ankles
          • Did not build a school to teach why the man is lame
        • The transforming power of the Lord Jesus Christ
      • The healing transaction
        • The direct eye contact
        • The Church must make personal, immediate, direct contact with lame humanity
        • The Church must go to the man outside the gate - and take him by the hand
        • The Lord Jesus Christ through His Church was making contact with a human need
        • The miraculous power
          • “In the name of Jesus Christ rise up and walk”
          • He took Him by the hand, He lifted Him up and immediately the man received strength
        • The completeness of conversion
          • Christ takes a man who is lame and puts him on his feet
          • The man: arose, stood, walked, leaped and praised God
          • Christ cancels disability and communicates ability
          • Christ makes the person useful
          • Christ makes the man whole and complete – Col 2:9
    • The purpose of the miracle
      • To help the man directly
      • To open up a witness to the people – Act 3:12-4:4
  • The Application
    • We are the Church
    • We have the name of Jesus Christ
    • We are to exercise faith in His name
    • His name is working today
