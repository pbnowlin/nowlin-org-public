• Summary of Paul’s Ministry
    • He preached the Kingdom – Act 20:25
    • He served with a clear conscience – Act 20:26
    • He faithfully declared the whole “counsel” of God – Act 20:27
    • He earnestly warned the church of peril – Act 20:31
    • He did not live for material gain – Act 20:33
    • He worked with his hands – Act 20:34
    • He taught them to give – Act 20:35
  • The Exhortation to the Elders – Act 20:28
    • The concern about your own lives
      • They must obey the truth they teach
      • Genuine obedience brings authority
    • Take care of the Church
      • The titles: 3 titles are used interchangeably for the same men – Elder, Bishop, and Pastor
      • To take care of means to “shepherd”
      • The shepherd ministry
        • Feeding
          • Sheep are not self-feeders
          • Sheep need help
        • Guarding
          • Sheep must be protected
          • Sheep must be defended
        • Healing
          • Sheep get sick
          • Sheep get hurt
        • Watching
          • Close observance
          • Alert evaluation
          • Correct interpretation
        • Guiding
          • Sheep are not independent travelers
          • They must be led
        • Rescuing the lost
        • Loving
          • “He calls His sheep by name” – Jhn 10:3
          • Love always individualizes
      • It is the Church of God
        • Christ loves the Church
        • Christ bought the Church with His own blood
          • Redemption
          • Atonement
          • Ownership
      • Watch for perils – Act 20:29-30
        • Imposters - Wolves in sheep’s clothing
          • They talk like
          • They act like
        • Outsiders who persecute
        • They shall not spare the flock
        • They are from the inside
          • They divide and separate
          • They seek a following
          • They draw people to themselves
      • Follow Paul’s example – Act 20:31-35
        • Admonish with compassion – Act 20:31
        • Use the word of grace – Act 20:32
        • Be selfless in serving – Act 20:34-35
        • Work hard in order to give
      • The commitment – Act 20:32
        • I commend you to God
          • Acknowledge sovereignty
          • Release them
        • I commend you to the word of His grace – Rom 5:2, 20; 2Ti 2:1; Tit 3:7; 2Co 9:8; 2Co 12:9
          • It builds up
          • It secures inheritance
        • The word of His grace
          • Electing love
          • Effective atonement
          • Effective calling
          • Eternal security
  • The Farewell – Act 20:36-38
    • Prayer was real
    • Love was real
    • Sorrow was real
    • Separation was real
