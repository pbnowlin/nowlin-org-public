    • The conversion of Lydia, the first believer on European soil
      • The setting – Act 16:14
        • It was the Sabbath
        • There was a prayer meeting at the riverside
      • Lydia
        • A seller of purple cloth
        • From the City of Thyatira
        • She worshipped God
      • The manner of her conversion
        • It was brought about by providential circumstances
          • She was from Thyatrira
          • Paul is forbidden to go there
          • Paul and Lydia must meet at Philippi
            • Paul must be directed
            • He must be brought to Troas
            • On a ship going to Samothrace
            • Landing at Neapolis
            • He must go down by the river
            • Lydia must be present
            • God brought them together
            • God’s providence is working everything together for the salvation of His elect
            • God will not allow one elect soul to miss the predestined moment!
            • God is in control of salvation!
        • The grace in preparing the heart
          • She knew something of the Word of God
          • She worshipped
          • She did not know Christ
          • God was preparing her heart
          • Grace opened her heart
            • She did not open her own heart
            • Her prayers did not do it
            • Paul did not do it
            • Nothing short of divine power can do it – Jhn 1:13
            • Faith is the gift of God – Eph 2:8-9
          • God uses “means”
            • She went to a prayer meeting
            • She heard the message
            • God used the witness of Paul
            • The truth entered the open heart
              • Her desires were awakened
              • Her understanding was enlightened
              • Her heart was influenced to believe
              • Her life was exercised in faith
      • The evidence of her conversion – Act 16:15
        • She is obedient to baptism
        • She communicates to her household
        • She is gracious in hospitality
      • Our conversion
        • Same natural condition
        • Same providential work
        • Same marvelous grace
        • Same miraculous believing
      • Our response
        • Thankful hearts for the providences which brought us to the place where Christ was revealed to us
        • Appreciation that results in devotion, obedience, and service
        • Faith in continuing grace to provide and empower
