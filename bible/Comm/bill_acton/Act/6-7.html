• The Occasion of Stephen’s Witness – Act 6:8-15
    • Stephen’s ministry
      • The basis of it
        • A man full of faith – Act 6:5
        • A man full of the Holy Spirit – Act 6:5
        • A man full of grace (power) – Act 6:8
        • A man full of wisdom – Act 6:10
      • The fruit of it – Act 6:8, 10
        • Great wonders and signs
        • Powerful conviction
    • The bitter opposition – Act 6:9, 11-15
      • The identity of the opponents – Act 6:9
        • Jews freed from Roman slavery
        • Greek speaking synagogue
      • The method they employed – Act 6:11-15
        • They resorted to treachery and lying
        • They set up false witnesses
      • The accusation they made
        • That he had spoken against God and Moses
        • That he had spoken against the Temple and the Law – Act 6:13-14
  • The Content of Stephen’s Witness – Act 7:2-53
    • The general character
      • It is historical
      • It is doctrinal
        • Reveals the sovereignty of God
        • Reveals the depravity of man
    • The contrast between failure and faith
      • The men of faith: Abraham, Joseph, and Moses
      • The failures
        • Joseph’s brethren – Act 7:9
        • The people in Egypt – Act 7:25,35
        • The nation
          • In the wilderness – Act 7:38-40
          • In idol worship – Act 7:44-50
          • In rejecting the prophets – Act 7:52
        • The people to whom Stephen witnessed failed – Act 7:51-53
          • They rejected the Prophet
          • They crucified the Just One
  • The Result of Stephen’s Witness – Act 7:54-8:3
    • The madness of the mob
      • They were cut to the heart
      • They were filled with rage
    • The vision of Stephen – Act 7:55-56
      • He saw the glory of God
      • He saw the Lord Jesus Christ – Mar 14:62 (This is what every believer sees at death)
      • He told what He saw
    • The stoning – Act 7:57-8:1
      • They would not listen
      • They put him to death
    • The triumph – Act 7:59-60
      • He died in prayer
      • He died in peace
      • He died in grace
      • He was “faithful unto death” – Rev 2:10
    • The extension of the Church – Act 8:1
  • Observation about the Death
    • It happened at the high point in his life and ministry
    • It was painful and filled with what would be dreaded
    • His departing moments were calm, peaceful, and joyous
      • He rose above all fear of man
      • He kept his mind on Christ
    • He had no resentments
    • He died as a conqueror
    • His death was useful
      • Conversion of Saul
      • Spread of the Church
