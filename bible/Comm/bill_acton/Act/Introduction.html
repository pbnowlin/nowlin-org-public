• The Title
    • The acts of the risen Christ
    • By the Spirit of Christ
    • Through the apostles and other believers
  • The Author: Luke
    • A physician and historian – Col 4:14
    • A companion of Paul – 2Ti 4:11; Phm 24
    • Act 1:1; Act 16:10-17; Act 20:5-38; Act 21:1-18
  • Historical Coverage from 33-64 A.D.
  • Its Place in the New Testament
    • It is the bridge
    • The New Testament
      • The Gospels: revealing the person of Christ (biographical)
      • The Acts: record of the preaching of Christ (historical)
      • The Epistles: instructing the people of Christ (practical)
      • Revelation: displaying the glory of Christ (prophetical)
  • The Importance of the Book
    • Doctrinally
      • Concerning Christ
        • His Lordship
          • Resurrection
          • Ascension
          • Head of the Church
      • Concerning the Holy Spirit
        • He is the Spirit of Jesus Christ
        • He does the work of Christ
        • He is predominate over the human in the Church
        • He indwells believers
      • Concerning the Church
        • The beginning of the Church
        • The growth of the Church
          • Numerically
          • Geographically
          • Culturally
          • Racially
          • Religiously
          • Jew and Gentile
        • The message of the Church
          • Forgiveness of sins
          • The resurrection of Christ
        • The preservation of the Church
        • The life of the Church
          • The teaching
          • The fellowship
          • The sharing
          • The witnessing
          • The danger
    • Practically
      • Enables us to see how God can accomplish His purpose in spite of opposition
      • Enables us to see how God can use the least and weakest of all humans for His purpose
      • Enables us to see the devotion of those who respond to the grace of God
  • Summary and Application
    • The book of Acts is the continuation of the Gospels about the “Acts” of the risen Lord Jesus Christ
    • The resurrection is the pivot around which all New Testament theology proceeds
    • The witness is not centered on an attempt to systematically prove the resurrection
    • The most convincing evidence is that of changed lives through contact with the risen Christ
    • Acts is the record of those who lived by what they were convinced was true
    • The resurrection dominated their life
    • What dominates our life?
