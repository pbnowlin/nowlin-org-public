• The Introduction to Paul’s Witness – Act 19:1-7
    • The disciples of John the Baptist – Act 19:1-3
    • Paul’s question to them – Act 19:2
      • He discerned a problem
        • There was something missing
        • The similar problem of Apollos
      • “Did you receive the Holy Spirit when you believed?”
    • Their answer: “No, we have not even heard about the Holy Spirit.” – Act 19:2
    • Paul’s second question: “What baptism did you receive?” – Act 19:3
    • Their answer: “The baptism of John the Baptist.”
    • Paul’s reply
      • The baptism of John was a baptism of repentance – Act 19:4
      • He taught them about Christ
    • The result
      • They were baptized in the name of Christ – Act 19:5
      • The Holy Spirit came to indwell them – Act 19:6
      • They received two gifts
        • The gift of languages
        • The gift of prophecy
  • The Lessons Learned
    • The condition of the men
      • They were not saved
      • They did not know the grace of God
      • They did not have faith in Christ
      • John’s baptism did not bring salvation
    • The ministry of Paul
      • What he did
        • He taught them about Christ
        • They were baptized in the name of Christ
      • What he did not do
        • He did not teach about the Holy Spirit
        • He did not teach about the baptism of the Holy Spirit
        • He did not teach two baptisms
        • He did not press for a second experience
        • He did not teach second blessing, deeper commitment, more faith, greater yieldedness or agonizing waiting
    • The effect of that ministry
      • Through faith in Christ the Holy Spirit came to indwell
      • Two spiritual gifts were given
        • The gift of languages
          • A sign gift to unbelieving Jews
          • An announcement that God was turning from the Jews to the Gentiles
          • The gift was associated with the Apostles
          • The gift ceased with the Apostles
        • The gift of prophecy
          • The proclamation of God’s Truth
          • The purpose of edification
  • The Holy Spirit in Our Life
    • Believe in the Son - the Holy Spirit lives within – Jhn 7:37-39; Rom 8:9; 1Co 3:16; 1Co 6:19; 1Jo 3:24
    • Live and walk in the Spirit
      • Accept His Person
      • Acknowledge His Presence
      • Agree with His Purpose
      • Appropriate His Power
        • Fresh Faith
        • Fresh Fruit
