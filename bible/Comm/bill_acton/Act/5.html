• The Occasion of the Witness
    • The Apostles imprisoned
    • The Lord sets them free
    • The Angel charges them to teach the people
    • The Apostles obey the Lord
    • The Jewish leaders apprehend them again – Act 5:20-27
  • The Content of the Witness
    • The charge – Act 5:28
      • You have filled Jerusalem with your teaching
      • You are accusing us
      • You have disobeyed our command
    • The reply – Act 5:29
    • The message
      • Loyalty to the Lord
      • Loyalty to the facts of the Gospel
        • He maintained the death and resurrection of Christ – Act 5:30
        • He maintained the lordship of Christ – Act 5:31
          • He is a Prince
            • To be honored and obeyed
            • He has power and dominion
          • He is a Saviour
            • He has accomplished the saving work
            • He is approachable
          • Approach Him as a Prince
            • With a confession of sin (past rebellion)
            • Accept His great purpose
              • To love God
              • To be like God
            • Submit to His rule
            • Surrender everything to Him
              • You are not your own
              • You belong to Him
            • Give Him your loving loyalty
          • Approach Him as a Saviour
            • Confess your need of a Saviour
            • Believe that He is able to save you
            • Submit yourself entirely to His salvation
          • Receive His gifts
            • Repentance
              • Change of mind
              • Change of living
            • Forgiveness
              • As though your sin had never been
              • Eternal removal of the penalty
            • Ask Him for them
              • Humbly
              • Believingly
        • He maintains the redemptive work of Christ – Act 5:31
      • Loyalty to the Holy Spirit – Act 5:32
  • The Result of the Witness – Act 5:33-42
    • On the Council – Act 5:33
      • They were “cut to the heart”
      • The convicting work of the Holy Spirit
      • Thrown into an uncontrollable rage with intent to kill
      • They hated the truth – Eph 6:12
    • On Gamaliel – Act 5:34-39
      • An appeal to common sense
      • An appeal to the sovereignty of God
    • On the Disciples – Act 5:40-42
      • Suffering - “They beat them”
      • Joy – Act 5:41
        • They were rejoicing
        • Suffering in His name is a privilege – Mat 5:11
      • Continuing witness – Act 5:42
        • When? Every day
        • Where? Temple and home
        • How? They ceased not
        • What? To teach and preach Jesus as the Christ
