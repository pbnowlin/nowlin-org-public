• Introduction: Author is Paul, in 58 AD, from Corinth during his third missionary trip
  • Theme: God saves sinners completely
  • Subject Matter: God declares sinners righteous through the merit of Christ, and calls them, and enables them to pursue righteous living

The Salutation

  • About Paul Himself
    • As to his ministry
      • The is a servant of Christ Jesus
      • The is an apostle
    • As to his life
      • He is called
      • He is separated
        • To a relationship
        • To a purpose
  • About His Message
    • It is the Gospel of God
    • It was prophesied through the prophets
    • It was personified in Christ
      • His humanity — the seed of David
      • His deity — the Son of God
      • His authority
        • The resurrection
        • The Spirit of holiness
      • His grace
        • The gives apostleship
        • The gives effectual calling
  • About His Readers
    • They are the called of God
    • They are the saints of God
    • Their testimony — their faith is known
