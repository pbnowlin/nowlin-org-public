• Definition of Dominion
    • To rule over
    • To control
  • Sin in the Unbeliever
    • It is in the nature
    • It is the desire
    • It is the pleasure
    • There is no desire for holiness
    • Sin is king
  • Sin in the Believer
    • Believers do sin
    • Sin is no longer king
    • Sin is our enemy
  • Our New Position
    • We have imputed righteousness
    • We love holiness
    • Sin cannot condemn us
      • "Sinners though we be in ourselves, we are righteous before God; damnable though our state may be, in Christ we are justified; the handwriting of God through the blood of Christ, inscribed in our conscience, declares us as righteous as if we never had committed any sin, yea, as if we personally had paid the penalty for our sins, and kept all God's commandments ever since!"
    • Our works cannot add to our righteousness
  • Grace Is Now Our King
