• God's Righteousness Demands Perfection
    • In regard to holiness — "Be ye holy for I am holy"
    • Through conscience — Conscience gives us the fact of morality
    • Through law — Law gives us the content of morality
    • The law cannot save because:
      • We have a fallen nature opposed to God's law — Rom 8:5-7
      • The nature of the law
        • The law is a unity
        • One violation breaks the whole law — Jas 2:10
        • Righteousness by law (works) means total obedience and perfect goodness.
        • Righteousness by law means permanent obedience, not temporary — we must keep the whole law all of our lives
      • We are already sinners
        • We are already condemned — Jhn 3:18
        • If we lived perfectly from now on it would not take care of our past
    • Self-righteousness is not acceptable to God's Tit 3:5
  • God's Righteousness Demands Punishment
    • The nature of sin
      • Sin is selfishness
      • Sin is rebellion
    • The punishment for sin
      • Physical death — eternal
      • Spiritual death — eternal
    • The results of sin
      • Separates us from God
      • Renders us spiritually dead
      • Opens our life to evil
  • God's Righteousness Is Revealed
    • Through His law
    • Through His Son — Rom 1:17
      • The sinless life
      • The sacrificial death
