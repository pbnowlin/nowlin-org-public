• How Christians Live Together — Rom 15:1-2
    • The strong sustain the weak
      • Bearing weakness together
        • We all have areas of strength
        • We all have areas of weakness
      • Not living just to "please" self
        • The sacrifice of personal desires
        • The desire for growth for others
    • The strong encourage the weak
      • By sharing how God helps
      • By patient loving kindness
      • By remaining firm to convictions
    • The weak encourage the strong
      • By willingness to learn
      • By response to God's Word
  • The Example of Christ — Rom 15:3
    • The did not live for Himself
    • The took all the hatred of the world for God upon Himself
    • The gave His life completely
  • The Encouragement of the Scripture
    • The Old Testament is for our learning
      • Through the historical experiences we learn about the nature of God
      • Through the historical experiences we learn how we should respond — 1Co 10:1-15
    • All of God's Word builds comfort and patience
    • God's Word is given for the purpose of building hope
  • The Enabling Power of God
    • God gives strength
      • Strength to do beyond our own abilities
      • Strength to submerge our own interests for the interests of the entire Body of Christ
    • God gives unity of purpose
      • One voice
      • Praise to God
      • The Father of our Lord Jesus Christ
  • The Relationship Desired
    • Accept one another
    • As Christ accepts us
      • In our unworthiness
      • In our poor response
      • In our failing performance
  • Our Relationship to Adam or to Christ Determines Our Position Before God
  • Our Fellowship with Christ Determines Our Relationships to Each Other
