• Introduction:
  • What Is God's Purpose?
    • To save the elect
    • To punish the rest
  • What Is God's Purpose for Israel?
    • There is no special place for the nation in the future
    • Some Jews and some Gentiles are being saved to form one body — the Church

II. God's Purpose Fulfilled

  • God's Purpose Is Fulfilled Through Christ
    • The illustration of the Chief Cornerstone " Isa 8:14; Mat 21:42-44; 1Pe 2:6-8
      • Christ is the Cornerstone of the Church
      • Christ is rejected by the Jews and the world
      • Through the Stone — He saves
      • He hardens
  • God's Purpose Is Realized in a Just and Righteous Way Through His Elect
    • The Gentiles have righteousness by faith
    • This righteousness is the gift of grace
    • This righteousness is in Christ — His merit
      • It does not mean we are sinless
      • It is not based on our merit or performance
  • God's Purpose Is Realized in a Just and Righteous Way Toward the Reprobate
    • The condemnation is through unbelief and rejection
    • The condemnation is through the attempt to gain righteousness by the law — works
  • God's Purpose Is Fulfilled in Jews and Gentiles
    • The Jews
      • Some Jews are saved — the remnant
      • Some Jews are lost — rejected
    • The Gentiles
      • Some Gentiles are saved
      • Some Gentiles are lost
    • The issue involved — who determines who is saved and who is lost?
  • The Conclusion
    • All boasting is excluded
    • Only God deserves glory
