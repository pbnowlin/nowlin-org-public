• The Nature of Grace
    • Grace is God acting freely
      • According to His nature
      • According to His love
      • Without obligation
    • Grace is God acting righteously through the cross of Christ
      • Sin is punished
      • His justice is satisfied
        • Not because God is big-hearted
        • It is because His judgment has been fulfilled
    • Grace has no cause in the receiver
      • Its cause lies wholly in God
      • Its cause is totally in the giver
        • Grace does not act where there is desert
        • Grace does not act where there is ability
        • Grace does not help — it does all
    • Grace is sovereign
      • It has no debts to pay
        • It is a pure gift
        • It cannot be repaid
      • Has no promises to fulfill
      • Can act toward whomever it pleases
      • Can choose the least worthy for its highest favor
        • It is not withheld because of demerit
        • It cannot be lessened because of demerit
      • The receiver must stop trying to become worthy
      • The receiver must acknowledge complete unworthiness
      • The receiver must acknowledge complete inability to attain worthiness
      • The receiver must find worthiness in Christ alone
      • Human pride has no place in grace
        • The natural man "hates" grace
        • The receiver "loves" grace
  • The Place of Man in Grace
    • He is totally, fully and completely acceptable and worthy in Christ
    • He is not "on probation"
    • His past life and old position does not exist before God — he died at the cross
    • His present life is Christ
    • Grace is bestowed, it is never withdrawn
  • The Proper Attitude of Man under Grace
    • To believe and receive
    • To consent to be loved while unworthy
    • To refuse to make resolutions or vows — that is trust in self (flesh)
    • To expect to be blessed (receive love) though realizing more and more lack of worth
    • To count on God's goodness
    • To be tender in conscience toward Him
    • To be thoroughly grateful
    • To continually tell of His goodness and mercy
  • The Discoveries of Man under Grace
    • To "hope to be (do) better" is to fail to see ourselves in Christ
    • To be disappointed with yourself is to have believed in yourself rather than in Christ
    • To remain in discouragement (depression) is to be self-centered rather than grace (Christ) centered
    • Devotion comes not from our will to show it but from the gratitude for grace received while unworthy
    • To put devotion first and blessing second is reversal of God's order; that is law not grace; blessing does not depend upon devotion; grace bestows undeserved, unconditional blessing — devotion follows
      • Salvation is the work of God for man — it is not the work of man for God
      • Salvation is the bestowal and actual impartation of eternal life — it is not the beauties and artificial imitations of ethical living
      • Salvation is the imputed righteousness of God — it is not the imperfect righteousness of man
      • Salvation is according to the faithful calling of God — it is not according to the fitful carefulness of man
      • Salvation is a divine reconciliation — it is not a human regulation
      • Salvation is the canceling of all sin — it is not the cessation from some sin
      • Salvation is being delivered from, and dead to, the law — it is not delighting in or doing the law
      • Salvation is divine regeneration — it is not human reformation
      • Salvation is being acceptable to God — it is not becoming exceptionally good
      • Salvation is completeness in Christ — it is not competency in character
      • Salvation is possessing every spiritual blessing — it is not professing any special betterment
      • Salvation is always and only of God — it is never of man. It is the unsearchable riches of Christ. It is unto good works which God hath before ordained that we should walk in them.
