• The Fact of Abraham's Justification by Faith
    • Proved by Paul's previous statement Rom 4:1-2
      • The Jew is justified by faith apart from the law — Rom 3:20-31
      • The Gentile is justified by faith apart from the law — Rom 3:20-31
    • Paul's statement is absolutely necessary
      • For converted Jews who found it difficult in breaking with Judaism
      • To clear Jewish misunderstanding of the O.T.
      • To show that God is one — His manner of giving salvation is always the same
      • This way of salvation does away with all forms of boasting
        • Even Abraham has nothing
        • No man can boast
    • The question of the text
      • "What then shall we say that Abraham our father has attained by the flesh? What is the Flesh?
        • External — not spiritual
        • The sin nature — Jhn 3:6; Rom 8:7
        • The natural human life — Phl 3:3
        • Anything that a person counts on for self-righteousness — works of any kind
      • Was Abraham justified on the grounds of his good character and good deeds?
        • If so — he can boast
        • That is impossible
      • What saith the Scripture?
        • Paul's respect for the authority of the O.T.
        • The clear statement of Gen 15:6 — Abraham is righteous because he believed God
    • What Abraham believed
      • Not in his own merit
      • He trusted God
        • God's Covenant
          • Land
          • People
          • Savior
      • Abraham believed in the plan of redemption thru Christ
      • Abraham was justified in exactly the same way we are justified now
        • Without works
        • Without merit
        • By faith alone
    • What Abraham received — "It was counted to him for righteousness"
      • The word is translated — count, reckon and impute
      • The basic meaning — to put to an account — Phl 1:8 — Our account — when we have nothing at all, God puts in the righteousness of Christ
  • The Explanation of Abraham's Justification by Faith
    • A general principle of grace
      • He that works deserves payment
      • The payment is not of grace, but of debt
      • He that does no work is justified by believing
        • Description of the man
          • He has no work
          • He is ungodly
          • Abraham is ungodly
        • Description of grace
          • The man believes
          • The man is justified
    • The explanation of justification
      • Godward
        • A declaration by God that He is granting pardon and granting the righteousness of Christ to the account and declaring the person as righteous
      • Manward
        • Justification does not change the man
        • Justification does not make the man any better
