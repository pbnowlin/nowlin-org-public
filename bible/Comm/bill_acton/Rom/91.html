• The Only Way to Live in Relationship to God
    • The surrender of the life — the body
      • The urgent request
      • Paul wants action
      • The basis of this appeal — "Therefore"
      • All that has been written before
        • Because you are forgiven
        • Because you are in Christ free from condemnation
        • Because you are indwelt
        • Because you are eternally united with Christ
        • Because you are the elect of God
        • Because there is no separation from love"
      • Love so amazing, so divine demands my"
        • Furthermore I beseech you"
    • The nature of true surrender to God
      • A voluntary act "Ye present"
        • Complete resignation
      • A personal and specific act
        • "Your bodies" a symbol that our whole life is to be consumed in the fire of God —sovereign
        • The sacred obligation of the Christian
        • We are the priests
        • We are an offering that lives
    • This is a useful act "Living"
      • God has no need for dead bodies
    • It is a reasonable act — "Your reasonable service"
      • An act of reason — done intelligently
      • This is the only reasonable relationship to God
      • Surrender now — don"t live in rebellion
      • This is peace, joy, relief from tension
    • Second appeal, based on the first — "Be not conformed to the world"
      • Don"t live by the standard of the world
      • Be subject to the Spirit of God
    • Be ye transformed
      • By the renewing of your mind
      • The constant exercise of the mind on spiritual themes
      • Transformation — inner change
      • As our mind is occupied with Christ we become like Him
    • The purpose of transformation
      • "That ye may prove"
      • To discover personally the fact and the excellent character of the will of God
    • God's will for the believer is good
      • It will not always be easy
      • Character building requires discipline and testing
    • God's will is acceptable
    • God's will is perfect — regardless of circumstance
      • There was a cross in the life of Christ
      • There will be crosses in our lives
      • But it will be God —perfect will for us
  • The Only Reasonable Attitude in the Church — Rom 12:3-5

    • Guard against conceit and boasting
    • Soberly — evaluate your life properly
      • Face reality
    • Appreciation — God hath dealt to every man

      • Just a small part
      • We are not the big wheel, but a spoke
      • There are responsibilities in the church
    • How to get along — Rom 12:9-13
      • Sincere love — "without hypocrisy"
        • No ulterior motive
          • Only for the sake of Christ
          • Only for the sake of others
      • Diligence in service — not slothful
      • Zeal — fervent in spirit — divine energy
      • Optimism — rejoicing in hope
        • Christians need this — don"t mope around
        • Don"t look on the gloomy side
      • Patience in tribulation
        • Steady thru every experience
        • Prayer
        • Generosity
  • In Relationship to the World — Rom 12:17-21
    • Peaceful
    • Unavenging
      • Belongs to God
