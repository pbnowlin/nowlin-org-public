        • Sensitive to needs
        • Willing to share
      • Hospitality — Rom 12:13

        • Open home
        • Willing to accept personal inconvenience
      • Without vengeance — Rom 12:14
        • No grudges
        • Bless them that curse
      • Sympathetic — Rom 12:15
        • Entering into the experience of others
        • Willing to be involved
        • Becoming vulnerable

        • Beware of pride

          • Being wise in our own eyes
