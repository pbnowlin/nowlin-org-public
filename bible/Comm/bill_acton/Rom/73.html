    • The illustration of the potter and the clay
      • The potter has power over the clay
        • The power is not just strength
        • The power is the authority, the right to do as the potter pleases
      • The vessels cannot question or protest the potter —purpose
      • The difference in the vessels
        • Some are unto honor
        • Some are unto dishonor
      • The purpose is to reveal God's sovereignty to determine the final destiny
        • to honor / to dishonor
        • to salvation / to damnation
        • without regard to works
