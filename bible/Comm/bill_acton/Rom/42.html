• Review
    • Know our position — Rom 6:1-10
      • Justified by faith
        • By grace
        • Through Christ
      • United with Christ
        • In His death
          • Our old man is crucified
          • Our old position in Adam is dead
        • In His resurrection
          • We are risen with Him
          • We live a new life
            • Dead to the rule of sin
            • Alive to the reign of grace
    • Reckon our position to be true
      • Believe God's Word as truth
      • Live accordingly
  • Do Not Yield to Sin, but Yield to God — Rom 6:12
    • The believer's relationship to sin
      • Sin will rule our — body mortal
        • Sin remains in the body
        • Sin is not eradicated
        • Sin will rule
          • Sin seeks to dominate
          • Sin turns natural inclinations into selfish lusts
      • We are not to allow sin to rule
    • The believer and sanctification
      • This is a doctrine we practice
      • Sanctification is not a deliverance once and forever
      • Sanctification requires active participation
      • Sanctification is an exhortation
        • Do not allow sin to rule — Rom 6:12
        • Do not steal — Eph 4
        • Do not lie — Eph 4
        • Et cetera, et cetera, et cetera
      • Parenthetical question: How does this reconcile grace apart from works?
        • It is God doing it through us — Phl 2:13
        • God's way of working is through our participation, according to Rom 6:12
        • We do it — but God enables us
    • The practical outworking
      • Understand — know the doctrine
      • Realize — reckon the truth of it
      • Yield to it — the application
        • "In the light of this truth what kind of person must I be?"
        • What is my position now?
        • What is my motivation to apply Rom 6:12
          • Not a reaction against my guilt
          • Not a desire to be happy
          • Not to try to be a Christian
          • Not because we are afraid of Hell
          • The motivation is positive
            • I know what Christ has done for me
            • I know my new position
            • I know God's purpose in me — 1Th 4:3
              • To undo the work of sin
              • To conform me to Christ
            • I know God's honor is involved in the matter of my behavior
            • I do not want to disgrace my Lord — 2Ti 2:19
            • Therefore, I do not allow sin to rule
            • If I allow sin to rule in my body it is a denial of everything I claim in Christ
      • The exhortation is possible for us to fulfill
        • God is not saying this to a natural man
        • This is written to us who believe
          • We are dead to Adam
          • We are alive to God
            • We are not alone
            • We have adequate resources
          • We are to exercise resistance
        • This is addressed to our will
