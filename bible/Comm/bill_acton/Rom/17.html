• The Object of Justification — Rom 3:25-26
    • Christ's sacrifice displays God's righteousness in His dealings with men in Old Testament dispensations
      • How can a holy God forgive sin and still be righteous?
        • Through the cross
        • Through forbearance
      • God's righteousness is declared
    • Christ's sacrifice displays God's righteousness in His dealings with men in this present time
      • God is just — sin has been punished
      • God is justifier — He took the punishment
  • The General Consequences of Justification by Faith — Rom 3:22-31
    • It humbles man
      • Man is unable to merit justification
      • Salvation is available to all on exactly the same condition
      • Boasting is excluded — Eph 2:8-9; 2Co 11:16-18; Phl 3:4-7; 1Co 1:26-31
    • It honors God — Rom 3:28
    • It eliminates any national aspect to Christianity — Rom 3:29-30
      • Not solely the God of the Jews
      • Not solely the God of the Gentiles
    • It eliminates salvation through ordinance — Rom 3:30
    • It confirms the law — Rom 3:31
      • Faith in the atoning work of Christ is a confession —
        • That the law is righteous
        • That the law is just in its" demand
      • Faith in the atoning work of Christ is the confession that Christ alone could satisfy the demands of the law
  • The Condition of Justification — Rom 3:28
    • It is by faith "alone"
    • Faith is not a work which merits justification
    • Faith has an object — a Person
    • Faith is a commitment to a Person of trust — 2Ti 1:12
  • Faith Is Sufficient for Complete Justification
    • Justification is immediate
    • We can never be more justified
  • Faith Is Sufficient for Certain Justification
    • The "works" system cannot produce assurance
    • The "experience" system cannot produce assurance
  • Faith Is Sufficient for Permanent Justification
    • Cannot be lost by lack of works
    • Not based on our faithfulness
    • Resident in God's righteous character and activity
  • Faith Is Sufficient to Demonstrate Justification
    • Empties of boasting — fills with praise
    • Change toward Christ
    • Change toward sin

Practical Observations

  • Justification is an objective reality based on concrete historical facts — 1Co 15:3-4
  • Justification is not experience-centered, and the church is drowning in the sea of experience today.
    Eastern Religion: "You go in and in and in and then you go in and in and in and after that you go in and in and in."
  • There is no deeper life — there is no higher life — there is nothing greater than the revelation of justification by faith in the Gospel of Christ
