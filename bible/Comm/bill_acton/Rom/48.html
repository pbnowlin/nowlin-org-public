• Rom 7:1-6
    • Those under the Law are released from that position by sharing in the death of Christ
    • Joined to a risen Christ — they bear fruit
    • Released from Law — they give glad and willing service
  • Rom 7:7-25
    • Description of the struggle of seeking to compel the flesh to obey God —Law — the result is utter despair
    • The new view — victory in Christ!

Purpose:

To help the willing believer cast himself into the depths of grace

Analysis:

  • Know Ye Not? The Law Dominates a Man as Long as He Is Living
  • The Law — of Moses
    • Attitudes toward the Law
      • The Legalist — is under the Law and fears
      • The Libertine — rejects the Law and hates
      • The Believer — preserves the balance and loves
        • Recognizes inability to keep Law — Law cannot save
        • Seeks to obey God —will by indwelling power of Christ
    • The Marriage Law
      • The binding character of the Marriage Law — Rom 7:2a
      • The duration of bond — "So long as he lives" — Rom 7:2b
      • The dissolution of Marriage Law, "If the husband dies" — Rom 7:2b
    • The Application
      • The first husband is the Law
      • When Christ died the Law died with Him — Col 2:13-17
      • We became free to live in eternal union with Christ
    • The principles applied to the spiritual life — Rom 7:4-6
      • Death experienced — Rom 7:4a
      • Relationship expected, "Joined to Christ" — Rom 7:4b
      • New life begun, "Bring forth fruit unto God" — Rom 7:4c
      • New freedom experienced — Rom 7:6
        • The time — "now"
        • The extent — "delivered from the law"
        • The method — "being dead"
        • The purpose — "that we should serve"
          • Negative — "not in oldness of law" — Rom 7:6
          • Positive — "but in newness of spirit" — Rom 7:6
