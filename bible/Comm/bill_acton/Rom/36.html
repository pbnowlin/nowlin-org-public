• Does the Teaching of Grace Encourage People to Sin? — Rom 5:20-21; 6:1-2
    • Our union with Christ — Doctrine — Rom 6:1-14
    • Our union with Christ — Practical — Rom 6:15-23
  • The Question — Rom 6:1
    • The true teaching of grace does not provoke this question
    • The question indicates a misunderstanding of the grace principle
  • The Answer — Rom 6:2
    • God forbid
      • By no means
      • Let it not be
      • It is unthinkable
    • Grace is to deliver us from the bondage and rule of sin
    • The answer in a question — "How shall we who died to sin, live in sin?"
  • Our Death to Sin
    • It happened once and forever
    • A definite fact that belongs to the past. One unique point in history
    • The believer died to sin — Rom 6:3-6, 11
  • Analysis ofRom 6:2
    • "We" — being what we are
      • Justified by faith
      • Our special position
    • "We who died to sin"
      • An event that has happened
      • Not a process
    • "How shall we live any longer therein?"
      • Does not mean "perfection"
      • Does not say we "ought" to be dead
      • Does not say we "are dying to sin"
      • Does not say we "are renouncing sin"
    • In what sense has the believer died to sin? — Rom 5:21
      • We have died to the reign of sin
      • The death and resurrection of Christ brought an end to the reign of sin in the believer
  • Analysis ofRom 6:3-5
    • Question: How did we die to sin?
    • Answer: In the doctrine of our union with Christ
    • The meaning of baptism in Romans 6
      • Sacramental — the act or rite or performance of baptism joins us to Christ — the act itself
      • Refers to baptismal vows — our vows put us into union with Christ
      • This is not water baptism
      • It is the baptism of the Holy Spirit — 1Co 12:13
        • He joins us to Christ
        • He puts us in the body
    • "Know ye not?" Do you understand?
    • "All who have been baptized into Christ"
      • All believers have union with Christ
      • Something that has been done for us
    • "Into Jesus Christ"
      • Totally, fully, completely
    • Being baptized into Christ means —
      • That we are joined to Him
      • That we are participators in what has happened to Him
        • We are joined with Him in His death
          • It is an objective fact
          • It is not a subjective experience
          • It has happened
        • Death to the reign of sin
        • Burial is the proof of death
        • We are joined to Christ in His resurrection
          • Christ was raised by the glorious power of the Father
          • The same glorious power that raised Christ has done the same to us — Eph 1:19-20
          • We have "newness of life"
            • A new realm
            • A new "walk" — 2Co 5:17
