The Response — The Realization — The Actualization
The Holy Spirit living in us produces the life that pleases and honors God

  • Review Rom 8:1-11
    • No condemnation through Christ
    • The sinful nature: Death
    • The Spirit in us: Life and peace
  • Consideration — Rom 8:12-14
    • The exhortation — Rom 8:12
      • "Since the Holy Spirit lives in you, do not live as if He does not"
    • The explanation
      • No obligation to our sinful nature
        • The cause of our troubles
        • We owe it — Nothing
        • No claim — Free
        • No slavery
        • No indebtedness
      • Our obligation is to the Lord
        • To the Holy Spirit
        • This is how we now live
        • Set free to live
    • The practical outworking
      • It is not perfectionism
        • Early 1900's — holiness
        • Pentecostal
        • A sinless perfection thru an experience
        • A second blessing beyond justification
        • Must seek
        • Must qualify
        • Devastation — not biblical
          • Must adjust God and His Law
          • Must lie to self and others
          • Must deny grace
      • It is not crisis victory
        • Not instant victory that lasts forever
        • The deeper life higher etc.
        • Not an experience of total surrender
        • Not something new
      • It is a growth process
        • It is gradual
        • It is maturing
        • Not instant arrival
      • It is a life to life
        • The truth of Rom 8:1-13
        • Now live it out
        • Put it into practice and keep on and on
        • Do not wait for or seek an experience
        • Respond now!
    • The precise approach
      • The realization and acknowledgement of biblical authority
        • The Holy Spirit lives in you and me
        • God lives in
        • The indwelling — 1Co 6:19-20; 1Co 3:16
        • A fact!
        • For all believers — even Corinthians
      • The actualization
        • Since I am a Christian I live like it
        • Since the Holy Spirit lives in me — I live accordingly
        • Not as though it is not true
