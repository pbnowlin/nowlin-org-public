• The Jew Had Many Advantages — Rom 2:17-18
    • He had the name of Jew — He was identified with the people of God's Covenant — Rom 2:17
      • They were chosen by God — election
      • They were to reveal God
    • He possessed the Law — He rested in the Law as though possession brought acceptance — Rom 2:13,17
      • They had exclusive possession of the Law
      • They thought they had exclusive possession of God
    • He glories in God — a vain consideration of himself while ignoring God's Word and will — Rom 2:18
    • He accepted the truth intellectually but his will was not changed
  • The Jew Knew His Position in the World
    • He had God's prophets
    • He had God's Word
    • He was the teacher
    • The description of the untaught — Rom 2:19-20
      • Blind
      • In darkness
      • Foolish
      • Babes
