• Witness of the Gospel
    • Formerly limited to the Jews
    • Under the New Covenant it is worldwide
    • This declaration now requires witnesses — preachers
    • The Word of God must be heard
  • The Witnesses
    • In the general sense all believers are preachers — proclaimers
    • In another sense there are men who are called by God to preach
    • Biblical texts — Jhn 8:32; Jhn 10:22-30
  • The Content of the Message
    • It must never be anything but the Word of God
    • It must keep Christ central
      • Who He is
      • What He has done
      • His return
    • The result depends upon God
  • Lord of the Witness
    • He sends those whom He chooses
    • He directs those whom He sends
    • No one should try to make himself a preacher or decide where he will work
    • The Church must determine the call
    • The Church must confirm the man
  • Necessity of the Witness — Preaching
    • Salvation depends on it
      • "How shall they call on Him in whom they have not believed?"
      • "How shall they believe in Him whom they have not heard?"
      • "How shall they hear without a witness?"
    • The result of "hearing" is faith
      • The inevitable effect is believing
      • When the Gospel is "heard" faith is the result —" Act 13:48
