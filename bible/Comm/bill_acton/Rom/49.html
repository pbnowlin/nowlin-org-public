• The Relationship of the Law to the Believer
    • The marriage illustration
      • The binding character of the marriage law
      • The duration of the bond
      • The dissolution of the bond
    • The doctrinal truth
      • The first husband is the law
      • When Christ died the Law died with Him — Col 2:13-17
      • The believer is married to a new husband — Christ
    • The principles applied
      • Death experienced — Rom 7:4a
      • Relationship established — Rom 7:4b
      • New life begun — Rom 7:4c
      • New freedom experienced — Rom 7:6
        • Negative — not by the old letter
        • Positive — but by the new spirit
  • The Law and Sin
    • Law reveals the fact of sin — Rom 7:7
      • The Law makes it apparent
      • The Law did not create sin
      • The Law exposes sin
      • The exposure to Paul
      • The evil desires revealed, 10th Commandment — Exo 20:17
    • The Law reveals the awareness of sin
      • It affects the conscience
      • It increases the sense of sin
      • It provokes sin
    • The Law condemns sin — Rom 7:8b
      • The judgment of the Law
      • The fact of spiritual death
    • The Law reveals the power of sin — Rom 7:9
      • The Law has legal power to convict
      • The Law has no life-power to deliver
      • Sin over-powers us and holds us helpless
      • We must die to self-reliance
    • The Law reveals the effect of sin — Rom 7:10
      • Sin brings death
      • Law legalizes
      • Paul —discovery of indwelling sin came after conversion
    • The Law reveals the deception of sin — Rom 7:11
      • It deceives us into thinking wrongly about God — that He will condone sin
      • It deceives us into thinking wrongly about ourselves — that we are not really responsible
        • By making us think that we can stand in our own strength
        • By producing the illusion that we can meet any situation
    • The Law reveals the sinfulness of sin — Rom 7:12-13
      • The Law imposes penalty upon the Law-breaker
      • The Law proves the sinfulness of human nature
        • Sin as leprosy
        • Sin as leaven
        • There is no defect in the Law
        • The defect is in us
    • "Under grace" means we are not "under Law"
      • We have been discharged from the Law
      • We have been cleared and will never face trial by the Law
        • The Law must be out of the way for the Lord Jesus Christ to live by faith in our hearts
