• Christ Is the End of the Law — Rom 10:1-4
    • The ceremonial law
    • The social law
    • The moral law
    • The Christian is not under law, but under grace
    • What is the standard for Christians?
    • Christ is the end of the law for righteousness
      • He fulfilled the law
      • He obeyed for the elect
  • Righteousness by Faith — Rom 10:5-8; Deu 30:12-14
    • Righteousness of the law: we work it out
    • Righteousness by faith, God works it out — gives it to us by grace and we respond through faith that He gives us
    • Righteousness is being right with God
      • The legal position — settled forever
      • The experiential condition — growing based on trust
    • Righteousness by faith
      • Faith is trust in Christ
      • We know Him
      • We love Him
      • We trust Him
    • Basis of our righteousness
      • We look at self — we find sin
      • We look at the law — we find the curse
      • We look at Christ — we find salvation
        • He died
        • He arose
        • He atoned
  • Conclusions
    • We must be careful not to drift back under the law
    • We must be careful not to rest on our works or performance
    • We must always trust in Christ only
    • We must always believe that His sacrifice is greater than my sin
    • We must always seek to follow Christ by enabling of Holy Spirit
    • Christ is now our life
