• Attitudes — Rom 14:1-6
    • Welcome each other
    • Do not judge each other
  • Personal Responsibility — Rom 14:5
  • God's Viewpoint — Rom 14:6
    • Observe every day in honor of God
    • Eat or do not eat in honor of God
    • Be thankful in doing and not doing
    • Do not make rules
  • We Do Not Have Proper Ability to Judge — Rom 14:7-12
    • There is only one Lord
    • We are not lords of one another
    • Christ is Lord of the dead
    • Christ is Lord of the living
    • We live in relationship to Him
    • We die in relationship to Him
    • We do not live or die alone
    • Christ will judge everyone
    • We are only free to judge ourselves
  • Another Word to the Strong, Free Christians — Rom 14:13-15
    • The law of liberty — Gal 5:1; 1Co 10:23-33
    • The limits of love
      • Love will not hinder others
      • Love will not cause others to stumble
      • We must not insist that others live according to our own conscience
      • "Do not let what you eat bring ruin on one whom Christ died"
        • Ruin — refers to a wasted life
  • Don't Major on the Minor — Rom 14:16-21
    • The important matter is not eating and drinking
    • The important matter is righteousness, peace, joy in the Holy Spirit
    • The spiritual is above the material
  • What Is Right and What Is Wrong
    • It is neither right or wrong to eat meat or drink wine
    • It is wrong if it is a hindrance to someone else
    • What is right — that which builds the Body of Christ
