• The Nature of Justification
    • Definition: "An act of God's free grace wherein He pardons all our sins, and declares us righteous through the righteousness of Christ given to us, and received by faith alone."
    • Justification is an act of God
      • An act of pure grace
      • An act of giving — imputation
    • Justification means that God declares — pronounces — regards us as righteous
    • Justice and grace contrasted
      • Justice demands God's best
      • Grace provides God's best
    • Justice condemns the best
      • Grace justifies the worst
      • Justification is an absolute righteousness
        • It is the righteousness of God — Rom 3:21
        • The justified sinner is declared to be as righteous as God — 2Co 5:21
      • It is more than forgiveness
    • Justification is an imputed righteousness — Rom 3:21
      • It is apart from the law
      • It is the gift of God received by faith
        • Faith — knowledge of the truth
        • Faith — agreement with the truth
        • Faith — commitment and trust in the truth
    • Justification is a manifested righteousness — Rom 3:21
      • In the person of Christ
      • In the work of Christ
    • Justice is an exclusive righteousness possessed only by believers — Rom 3:22
    • Justice is an inclusive righteousness available to all — Rom 3:22-23
    • Justice is a gratuitous righteousness — Rom 3:24
      • Given freely by His grace
      • Nothing in us to deserve it
      • Nothing to do with our behavior
      • It is entirely free — a free gift to those who deserve exactly the opposite
  • The Ground of Justification — Rom 3:24-25
    • The ground of free grace — the union of justice and love
      • Destroys guilt freely
      • Destroys guilt on condition of justice
    • The ground of Christ's redemptive work
      • Man was in debt
      • Christ paid our ransom in full
    • The ground of Christ's propitiatory work — Rom 3:25
      • Definition of propitiation — to render favorable
        • To conciliate
        • To render acceptable
      • The atonement
        • Covers sin
        • Bears away sin
      • Result
        • God is satisfied
        • God's justice is satisfied
  • Justification Is Our Present Position — "Being Justified"
