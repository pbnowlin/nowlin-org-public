  • The Time of Abraham's Justification by Faith — Rom 4:9-12
    • Abraham justified prior to the giving of the rite of circumcision — Rom 4:9-10
      • Abraham was justified fourteen years before circumcision
      • Faith was counted as righteousness
    • Why circumcision was given — Rom 4:11-12
      • It is an outward sign
        • Serves as a seal to authenticate the imputation of righteousness
        • Serves as a reminder of God's promise
      • That Abraham might be the Father of all who believe
        • In Abraham God makes clear the way of justification
        • Abraham is an example of "By Grace Through Faith" to all
        • Abraham is "Father" to all who believe — Jhn 8:36-44
      • The rite of circumcision
        • In itself it is nothing
        • It avails nothing — Gal 5:6; Rom 2:28-29
        • The principle of faith is vital!
  • The Relationship of Justification by Faith to the Law — Rom 4:13-16
    • The Promise from Genesis — Rom 4:12-15
      • Land
      • People
      • Savior
      • Heir of the World
    • The Promise was given to Abraham 430 years before the Law was given to Moses — Gal 3:17
    • The Promise was not made through the Law but through the Righteousness of Faith — Rom 4:13
    • The Seed
      • Christ is the Seed of Abraham — Gal 3:16
      • We are the seed by being in Christ
        • We were children of wrath
        • We are now children of faith
    • The Law and Faith do not mix — Rom 4:14
    • The Promise is unconditional — Rom 4:14
    • The Law brings wrath and punishment — Rom 4:15
    • The Promise brings righteousness and inheritance
    • The Law establishes transgression — Rom 4:15
      • Pin-points our sin
      • Makes guilt more evident and obvious
  • Justification by Faith Is Guaranteed by Sovereign Grace — Rom 4:16
    • The Promises of God are obtained by faith
      • Not by works
      • Not by law
      • Not by circumcision
      • Not by behavior
    • It is of faith — that it might be by grace
      • Grace is free unmerited favor
      • Grace is given to those who do not deserve it at all
    • Law and works go together
    • Grace and faith go together — Rom 11:6
    • Law is always a matter of merit
    • Grace is always the free gift of God received by faith
    • Salvation is God's glory
      • Anything but faith detracts from His glory
      • Anything but grace promotes boasting
    • Salvation by grace through faith includes all people — Rom 4:16
    • Salvation by grace through faith is complete and secure — Rom 4:16
      • Our salvation depends upon the Character of God and His grace
      • Our salvation never depends upon ourselves, our abilities, our understanding, our goodness or badness — Phl 1:6; Rom 8:38-39; Jhn 10:28
