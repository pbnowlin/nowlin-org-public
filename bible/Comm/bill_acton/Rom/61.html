• Foreknowledge
    • It means more than just knowing ahead
    • It includes determination and control
    • It is sometimes translated as "foreordained"
      • 1Pe 1:2, 20; Act 2:23
    • It is God's determination to carry out His plan to save His elect
  • Predestination
    • The fact of it
      • It is Biblical — Eph 1:4-5
      • It is necessary
    • The purpose of it
      • To save the elect
      • To conform the elect to Christ
  • Called
    • The necessity of the call — Eph 2:1-3; Rom 9:9-21
    • The irresistible call — Jhn 6:37, 44-45; 5:25; 1Co 4:7;
      Act 13:48, 16:14, 18; 9; 10; 11
  • Justification
    • The definition: The act of God whereby He declares righteous those whom He has chosen
    • The transaction: A change from guilt and condemnation to pardon and acceptance
    • The basis — Rom 3:24; 4:25; 5:9
    • The results — Rom 5:1; 8:33-35; 4:5; 3:28; 8:1
  • Glorification
    • The Glory of Christ — Heb 2:9-10; Phl 2:9-11; Rev 19:11
    • His Glory is our Glory — 1Pe 1:3-5; Rom 8:16-17
      • Phl 3:20-21; 1Co 15:51-53; Rom 8:29; Eph 2:7
