• The Background of the Passage
    • Doubtful things for Christians of the First Century
      • Eating meat that had been offered to idols
      • Drinking wine that had been offered to idols
      • Observance of certain "Holy" Days
    • Doubtful things for Christians of the Twentieth Century
      • Areas of life where Scripture does not speak specifically
      • Areas of life where conscience rules
      • Areas of life that are influenced by cultural background
      • The long list of "Christian taboos"
        • "Sanka" — the Christian coffee
        • "Basic H" — the Christian soap
  • The Exposition of the Text
    • The first attitude for all believers
      • The stronger believer is to receive or welcome the weaker
      • The "strong" believer has entered into the full freedom which is his birthright; He is strong in faith — Gal 5:1
      • The weaker believer is to receive or welcome the stronger — Rom 14:3
      • The "weaker" believer is the one who is governed by legalism and is ruled by negative restrictions that are not forbidden by Scripture
      • Believers are not to argue or dispute over these matters
        • We are not to make up the mind of the other person
        • We must not be judgmental where Scripture is silent
      • We are to welcome the other believer because God has — Rom 14:3
    • The second attitude for all believers is "judge not" — Rom 14:3-4; Col 2:16-17; 1Ti 4:3-5
      • Definition of "judging" — to condemn, label, categorize, criticize
      • Believers are to confront each other in the areas where Scripture speaks
      • Reasons for not judging one another
        • We do not have the right
          • Christ is Lord
          • The alone is their Master
        • It does not help us or them or the Body of Christ
          • Christ will uphold him
          • Christ alone is able
    • There is no merit in doing or abstaining — Rom 14:5-6
    • The Lordship of Christ demands individual freedom — Rom 14:5
      • Let every man
      • Be fully convinced
      • In his own mind
  • General Principles
    • Learn the "whole counsel of God"
    • Stay away from "all appearance of evil" — 1Th 5:22
    • Seek to build the faith of others
