• What is the Calling?
    • Paul's general use of the term
      • All the work of God is saving sinners
      • Changing the heart that hates God to love God
    • The specific use of the term
      • The work of the Holy Spirit in regeneration
      • The work of the Holy Spirit in producing faith for salvation — Rom 8:28-30
    • This Call of God is effective
      • It is not a mere invitation
      • It is a Divine summons
    • The Call comes with authority
      • The sinner on his own cannot obey the Call
      • The sinner is enabled or empowered with the special Grace of God
        • Common grace
        • Special Grace — Act 13:48; 16:14; 18:9-11
    • The Call is irresistible
      • The non-elect will refuse
      • The elect cannot refuse when it is God's time to save " Jhn 11
    • The content of the Call
      • It is in the Word of God
      • It is in the Gospel of Christ
  • What Is the Purpose of the Calling? — The quote from Hosea
    • Those who were not God's people are made the people of God
    • Those who were not beloved are made the beloved of God
    • Those who were not God's people are called into sonship with God
    • Jew and Gentile are now one " Gal 3; Eph 2;
    • The saved Jew and the saved Gentile are now the true Israel of God
  • Who Are the Called?
    • It is not all mankind
    • It is not the nation of Israel
    • It is not those who are deserving
    • It is those individuals who have been chosen by God to receive His salvation as the gift of His Grace
  • God Called Individuals Throughout History
    • Adam

    • Noah
    • Moses
    • David
    • Saul of Tarsus
    • Martin Luther
