    • The practical outworking — Rom 6:13
      • The negative — we must not yield our members unto sin as instruments of unrighteousness
        • "Members"
          • The parts of our body
          • The mind part of body because of the brain
          • The will as associated to the brain
          • The emotions as associated to the mind and chemical reactors in the body
        • "Instruments" are tools — servants
        • "Yield" — present
          • Do not present your "members" as "tools" of unrighteousness
          • Do not allow the body to be used in the service of sin
          • Do not allow individual members of the body to be used as a servant of sin
            • Appetites
            • Energies
            • Drives
            • Speech
            • Thoughts
            • Imaginations
            • Emotions
              • Fear
              • Worry
              • Love
              • Hate
              • Bitterness
              • Resentments
      • The positive
        • Present the members of your body unto God
        • As instruments — tools, servants of righteousness
        • As being alive unto God
          • The believer can do this
          • We are responsible to do this
      • The process
        • Initial prayerful presentation — giving, yielding
        • Continuing attitudes of giving
      • The problem
        • Ourselves

        • Performance oriented
          • Our struggle

      • The solution
        • "Stand" on our position
        • Present ourselves for service
        • Put ourselves at His disposal
          • We do not come to a clinic for illness
          • We come to the commander-in-chief for service
          • We must consider the military commands of the New Testament
            • Fight the good fight of faith
            • Be equipped as men
            • Put on the whole armor of God
            • Stand in the evil day
            • Endure hardness as a good soldier

            • Our failure
            • Our feeling
            • Our condition
            • Our success
          • The chief issue

            • But "How can I glorify God?"
