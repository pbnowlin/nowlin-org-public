  • The Example of Pharaoh
    • The meaning of God raising up Pharaoh — Exo 9:6
      • Usual interpretation — for natural purposes only
      • The point — God hates Pharaoh as He does Esau, and hardens Pharaoh for His own purpose
      • God raised up Pharaoh to rebel against Him
        • Pharaoh —rebellion is not contrary to God's purpose
        • Pharaoh and all the wicked are predestinated as vessels of wrath fitted for destruction
        • Pharaoh represents all the unsaved
          • Pharaoh does not mind being hard
          • All the unbelievers do not mind being hard
    • The realization of God's raising up Pharaoh
      • God worked on Pharaoh — He hardened Pharaoh —heart
        • Pharaoh and all the wicked are already unbelievers
        • God hardened an already hardened heart
      • How is this hardening accomplished
        • God gave His Word
        • God put His people in Pharaoh —presence
        • God gave him gifts
        • God used Pharaoh — Pharaoh did not use God
        • God is not struggling with Pharaoh
    • The purpose of God's raising up of Pharaoh
      • God's purpose is stated clearly
        • For God's power to be shown
          • Not just power over Pharaoh
          • But to show that He is God
        • God raised up Pharaoh to show that Pharaoh would rebel against God
