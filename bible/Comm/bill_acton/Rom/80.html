• The Difference Between the Jews and the Gentiles
    • Under the Old Covenant (O.T.) there was a difference — God dealt primarily with the Jews
    • Under the New Covenant (N.T.) the Gospel of Christ with its saving power — Go to all the nations
    • There is now no difference
  • The Unlimited Power of the Gospel
    • Whosoever shall call on Him shall be saved
      • God can save the "chief of sinners"
      • God can save from every race
      • God can save from every social, financial, education level
    • All who call upon Him shall be saved
      • All — means that no one who calls will be turned away — Jhn 6:37
      • All — does not mean that everyone will call on Him
    • Who is included in the "whosoevers" and the "alls"?
      • Only those whom God has chosen for salvation
      • Only those whom God first calls and regenerates
      • The "calling on Him" — faith is the gift of God's grace
    • The Gospel will go to the nations
      • God will save some Jews and some Gentiles
      • God will not save every Jew and every Gentile
      • There will be whosoever will and whosoever will not
    • The calling on Him
      • The confession of the need for salvation
    • The calling on Him is trusting in Christ for salvation
  • The Common Salvation
    • The same Lord
    • The same salvation
    • The same riches
    • The same Gospel
    • The same faith
    • The same Church Body
    • The same life-eternal
    • The same hope — no disappointment or shame
  • The Basis of this Salvation Is Christ — the Lord Over All
    • Over the Jews
    • Over the Gentiles — Act 2
  • The Riches He Gives to Us — Eph 1
