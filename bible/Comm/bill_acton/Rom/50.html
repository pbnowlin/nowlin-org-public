• Preliminary Observations
    • Romans chapter 7 is not a necessary experience
      • Paul —experience is highlighted by his background
      • Not every believer must go through Paul —anguish
    • This cannot be a continual experience
      • No Christian can live in constant defeat
      • Every Christian will experience some growth
    • This is the experience of a believer
      • Paul was hating sin
      • Paul was desiring holiness
  • The Purpose of the Passage
    • To demonstrate the inability of anyone to fulfill the demands of the Law — personal holiness
    • To demonstrate the necessity of the indwelling Spirit of Christ for the new position of justified to walk in holiness
    • To demonstrate that the same sovereign grace of God that justifies, gives a new position, and continues to operate in the life
    • To demonstrate that when the new nature "moved in," the old nature just "moved over" not "out"
    • To demonstrate that the Law is "good" but sin is "bad"
  • The Analysis ofRom 7:7-13
    • The Law and sin
      • Law reveals the fact of sin — Rom 7:7
        • To commit sin is to sin
        • The law makes it apparent
        • The Law did not create sin
        • The Law exposes sin
        • The exposure of Paul
        • The "evil desires" revealed — 10th commandment
      • The Law reveals the awareness of sin
        • It affects the conscience
        • It increases the sense of sin
        • It provokes sin
      • The Law condemns sin — Rom 7:8b
        • The judgment of the Law
        • The fact of spiritual death
      • The Law reveals the power of sin — Rom 7:9
        • The Law has legal power to convict
        • The Law has no life — power to deliver
        • Sin over-powers us and holds us helpless
        • We must die to self-reliance
      • The Law reveals the effect of sin — Rom 7:10
        • Sin brings death
        • Law legalizes
        • Paul —discovery of indwelling sin came after conversion
      • The Law reveals the deception of sin — Rom 7:11
        • It deceives us into thinking wrongly about God — that He will condone sin
        • It deceives us into thinking wrongly about ourselves that we are not really responsible
          • By making us think that we can stand in our own strength
          • By producing the illusion that we can meet any situation
      • The Law reveals the sinfulness of sin — Rom 7:12-13
        • The Law exposes sin
        • The Law imposes penalty upon the law-breaker
        • The Law protects the law-abiding person
        • The Law proves the sinfulness of human nature
          • Sin as leprosy
          • Sin as leaven
          • Sin as evil desire
          • Knowledge of right and the command to obey are incentives to evil
          • There is no defect in the Law
          • The defect is in us
          • "Search me, O God, and know my heart"
      • "Under grace" means we are not "under Law"
        • We have been discharged from the Law
        • We have been cleared and will never face trial by the Law
        • The Law must be out of the way for the Lord Jesus Christ to live by faith in our hearts
