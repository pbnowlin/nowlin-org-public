  • Analysis of Romans 9; 10; 11
    • The parenthesis
      • To reveal God's sovereignty
      • To reveal Israel's relationship to God
    • The message — Romans 9; 10; 11
      • Israel's election is according to God —Son
      • Israel's rejection is Israel —own fault
      • Israel's rejection is neither complete or final
  • God —Salvation for Man
    • The error of the Jews " Rom 9:30-33; 10:1-21
      • They tried to attain righteousness on their own
        • The Gentiles's saving faith — Rom 9:30
        • The Jews's no saving faith — Rom 9:31-33
          • They trusted in themselves
          • They trusted in their external ceremonies
          • They failed God
      • They did not respond to God's method of salvation " Rom 10:1-15
        • They were blind — Rom 10:1-5
          • Zeal without knowledge — Rom 10:1-2
          • Spiritually ignorant — Rom 10:3
            • Ignorant of God's righteousness
            • Attempted to establish their own righteousness
        • Their chief problem — "They have not submitted themselves unto the righteousness of God"
    • A clear statement of the only means of salvation — Rom 10:4-10
      • Christ is the complete fulfillment of the entire Law in behalf of every believer
        • Only Christ fulfilled the Law
        • His gift of imputed righteousness is sinful man —only hope
        • The Law is not a rule of life or a means of righteousness
      • Christ has come to man — Rom 10:6-8
      • Man responds to God
        • Confess with the mouth
          • Confession is the result of believing
          • Confession is an aid to assurance
        • Believe in the heart
          • Believe the Gospel personally
            • The death
            • The resurrection
      • The certain result — "Thou shalt be saved"
        • A new home in heaven
        • A new creature on earth
