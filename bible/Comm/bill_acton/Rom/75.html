• The Last Answer to the Questions Raised in this Text Regarding Predestination and Election
    • First — Is God unrighteous?
    • Second — Why does He blame?
    • The last answer Rom 9:22-23
  • God Deals Sovereignly with Everyone —
    • The vessels of wrath and the vessels of mercy
    • The meaning of the term, "vessels of wrath"
      • They are the ungodly reprobates, the wicked, the unbelievers whom God predestinated to serve the revelation and purpose of His wrath
      • They are the ones who live in time in disobedience to God and bear His wrath forever
      • The vessels of wrath are prepared for destruction
        • They are on their way to eternal damnation
        • They are prepared for that by God
        • They also want destruction
    • The vessels of mercy
      • They are predestined to glory by God
      • They are prepared for glory by God
  • The Way God Works Sovereignly in Everyone
    • The patience — endurance of God toward the "vessels of wrath" (the stranger in the home)
    • The longsuffering of God with the "vessels of mercy" (the surgeon operating on his son)
  • The Purpose God Has in His Sovereignty
    • To show His wrath and to make His power known enduring the vessels of wrath
      • God's wrath is His righteous hatred of sin, and His righteous punishment of sin
      • The vessels of wrath serve to carry out God's purpose in making His wrath known
      • God makes His power known
        • God is not in a power struggle to see who will win
        • All the powers of evil operate to serve God's purpose
    • God makes known the riches of His glory upon the vessels of mercy prepared for glory
      • His election
      • His grace
      • His salvation
      • His glory
