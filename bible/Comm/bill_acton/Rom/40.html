• Key Verse — Rom 6:5
    • Rom 6:6-7 present an exposition of the first half of Rom 6:5
    • The negative — Dead with Him
    • Rom 6:8-10 present an exposition of the last half of Rom 6:5
    • The positive — Alive with Him
  • Alive with Christ
    • Future resurrection
    • Present position — even now we walk in newness of life
    • The understanding of this truth comes as we learn what happened to Christ in death and resurrection
      • Death has no dominion over Him
        • The sting of death is sin
        • He died because He took our sin
        • He put Himself under the power of death
        • He destroyed death — Rom 5:21
        • He will never die again
      • Christ was raised from the dead
        • By the glory of the Father
        • An announcement that the work of redemption, atonement, justification, reconciliation, and salvation is absolutely completed
      • The resurrection is the proof; the guarantee, the point of certainly; our point of authority
        • He lives with God
          • Exclusively in the realm of God
          • No longer under sin and death — Phl 2:5-12
        • He is restored to previous position — Jhn 17:1-3
  • Summary — Rom 6:1-11
    • Look exclusively at the Lord Jesus Christ and our position in Him
  • The Turning Point — Rom 6:11
    • The realm of application —
      • Our practice
      • Our conduct
      • Our behavior
      • Our experience
    • The reckoning — realizing the truth about ourselves
      • What is true of Christ is true of us — we are joined to Him
      • Considering ourselves
      • A logical conclusion
      • It is not talking ourselves into something
      • It is the result of truth
      • It is accepting God's Word as true
      • It is counting on that truth
      • It is putting my life on that truth
    • We are to know and count on the fact that we are dead to the rule of sin
      • It does not mean that it is my duty to die to sin
      • It is not a command for me to die to sin
      • It does not mean that I believe sin to be eradicated
      • It does not mean that sin is dead
      • It does not mean "my reckoning" makes me dead to sin
      • We are to reckon something about ourselves as true because it is a fact through our union with Christ. He is dead to the realm of sin and alive to the realm of God
      • No longer under the dominion of death — Jhn 11:25-26
      • No longer under the dominion of sin
        • Sin can never make us its slave again
      • God's way was not to change the old man but to send it to the cross unto death to release us from it
      • No one who remains in Adam will be saved
      • We are released from Adam — New in Christ
    • We are to believe we are alive unto God — in the realm of the operation of the reign of grace
      • No longer under the wrath of God — Jhn 3:36
      • We are now reconciled to God
      • We are open to all the blessings of God — Rom 5:2
      • We are the special objects of His concern
      • We are in God's plan and purpose to be holy and without blame before Him in love — Eph 1:4
      • God's purpose is being worked in our life
        • Through our union with Christ we have a living, vital, organic attachment to Him
          • We receive life from Him — Jhn 15:5
          • We are His body — Eph 4:15
        • Thru the Spirit of Christ who indwells us
  • We Are to Believe Our Position — We Do Not Experience Our Position
    • We have the Word of God
      • We must believe it
      • We must submit to it
      • We must act upon it
    • We believe our position
      • We are in grace
      • God will bring us to perfection
    • We know we are not hopeless
      • Our relationship to sin is exactly the same as Christ"s
      • We are in Him
    • Our difficulty is in believing
      • It is too good
      • We are still in the body
    • By faith alone we reckon the truth
