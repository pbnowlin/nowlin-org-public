  • The Practical Application
    • Not happy to have them
    • Do not go out seeking them
    • Praise, thanksgiving, rejoicing, glorying — over time — Heb 12:11
    • How does it work?
      • Through the application of our faith
      • Our faith enables us to view them in proper perspective
        • Not as working against our hope
        • But as actually promoting hope — Rom 5:3-5
      • Our faith enables us to deal with our tribulations
        • Through knowledge — God's revelation "knowing that" — Rom 5:3
        • We know that tribulation worketh patience
          • The work is the process
          • Results: patience, endurance, steadfastness
      • What is the process?
        • Tribulations come
        • We realize our need
        • True faith turns to Christ
        • We receive a fresh supply of grace and strength
          • Increase of time — with the Lord
          • Increased dependence upon Him
          • Proper knowledge of ourselves
            • Not overestimating
            • Sense of dependence
