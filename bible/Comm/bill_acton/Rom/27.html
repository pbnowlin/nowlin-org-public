• Justification by Faith — Rom 5:1-4
    • We have been justified
    • By faith
    • Through our Lord Jesus Christ
    • We have peace with God
    • We have access to God
    • We stand in God's Grace
    • We rejoice in the hope of glory
    • We glory in our tribulation
  • The Anchor of Hope — Rom 5:5
    • Hope is based on God's love for us
    • God's love is "poured out" on us
    • God's love is dispensed by the Holy Spirit
    • The Holy Spirit is the gift of God to us
  • Rom 5:6-8, 10
    • Our condition before salvation
      • Helpless
      • Ungodly
      • Sinners
      • Enemies
    • What the Lord did for us
      • Christ died for the ungodly
      • Christ died for sinners
      • God demonstrated His love
      • We were reconciled to God through the death of His Son
  • "Much More" Assurance — Rom 5:9-10
    • We are saved from wrath
    • We are reconciled
    • We are maintained by His Life — His present ministry
      • Great High Priest
      • Advocate
      • Mediator
      • Intercessor
      • Chief Shepherd
      • Head of the Body
  • Reasons for Failure to Rejoice
    • Not believing the objective Truth
    • Looking too much at self — too introspective
    • Not doing hard work in Scripture
    • Lacking the concept and belief in God's sovereign grace
