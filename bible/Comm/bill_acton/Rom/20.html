• The Event of Abraham's Justification by Faith
    • Abraham was credited with righteousness
    • This righteousness is given by God
    • Justification is being credited with righteousness by God
    • David was credited with righteousness
      • His sons were covered
      • His transgressions were forgiven
      • His sin will never count against him
  • The Explanation of Justification by Faith
    • The issue is how a guilty sinful human can be accepted by a holy and righteous God?
    • The solution —
      • Righteousness is the gift of God
      • It is given apart from human merit
      • It is given through the merit of Christ
      • Righteousness is in Christ
  • The Experience of Justification by Faith
    • Abraham was justified, fourteen years before circumcision
    • Abraham believed God and was counted as righteous
    • Justification is realized by faith
      • Faith is believing the Word of God
      • Faith receives grace
        • Anything but grace detracts from God's glory
        • Anything but grace promotes boasting
    • Abraham and faith
      • By faith Abraham believed God's promises
        • About the land
        • About the nation
        • About the blessing
      • By faith Abraham believed God in spite of adverse circumstances
        • Abraham's age
        • Sarah's condition
      • By faith Abraham was faithful
        • He did not go back and forth
        • He did not go from side to side
      • By faith Abraham believed with assurance
        • Fully persuaded
        • Strongly convinced
      • By faith Abraham glorified God
        • He went out — Gen 12:1-3
        • His name changed — Gen 17:1-3
        • Rose up early — Gen 22:1-3
      • By faith Abraham trusted in God's sovereign power
        • God had promised
        • God would perform
    • Our justification — Rom 4:23-25
      • By grace
      • Through faith
