• Review
    • Question: Shall we sin so that grace might abound?
    • Answer: Absolutely not
    • We are united with Christ in His death
    • We are united with Christ in His resurrection
    • We have a new life
  • Analysis of Rom 6:5-6
    • "Since we have united together in the likeness of His death"
      • The union
        • Growing together
        • A joined growth becoming one
      • The likeness
        • Our physical death and resurrection are not identical with His
        • What has happened to Him literally happens to us spiritually
          • This does not primarily refer to our literal bodily resurrection
          • This refers to our present Christian life while we are still here in this world
    • The new life
      • We are no longer in the old life under the ruling power of sin
        • We died out of it
        • We have risen out of it
      • We are now under the rule of grace and the reign of grace is so powerful that sin has been defeated
      • There is a secondary application to our future resurrection — 2Ti 2:11-12, 19
    • The crucifixion of the "old man"
      • It has happened in the past
        • We should know this
        • We were crucified with Him
        • This is revealed knowledge
        • This knowledge is certain
        • It is not our trying
      • Our "old man"
        • The man that I used to be in Adam
          • I was a man in Adam
          • Now I am a man in Christ
          • This not the eradication of our sin nature
          • This is a positional change
          • This crucifixion is not a process
            • Christ died once
            • The man that I was in Adam died with Him — Gal 2:20
            • The man that was in Adam
              • Born under the law
              • Born in sin
              • Born under condemnation
              • That man is dead
            • The new man
              • In Christ
              • No condemnation
          • Relationship of Eph 4 "put off" and "put on"
            • Do not go on living as if you were still that old man — he has died
            • Be what you are — you are the new man
          • Illustration — Don't live like a baby when you are a man
          • Know this
            • Something to believe
            • Something to receive by faith
            • This is an action of the Holy Spirit which is non-experimental
            • I know because God said it Rom 4:18-21
            • I believe regardless of how I feel — Rom 8:1
            • We do not take on experience by faith — we are taking the objective truth by faith
            • Knowing it — believing it — being aware of it — I can now see proofs of the new life within me
    • The result of the crucifixion of the old man in Adam
      • In order that the body of sin might be rendered ineffective
        • Our physical body of which sin has taken possession
        • Sin as it dwells in us in our present embodied condition
        • The body itself is not sinful
        • Bodily instincts are not sinful
        • Sin influences and affects the body
        • Sin governs the body
          • Bodily instincts out of proportion
          • The body becomes our idol
        • The body of sin — being used and tyrannized by sin
      • In order that the body of sin might be rendered ineffective
        • The purpose of salvation is to deliver us from the tyranny of sin — to be set completely free including our body
        • The object of salvation is that while in this world we may approximate complete freedom
          • The future redemption of the body — Rom 8:23
          • The future glorification of the body — Rom 3:20-21
      • In order that we should no longer be slaves to sin
        • The man in Adam is a slave to sin — he has no freedom
        • The man in Christ is under the rule of grace — he has a new Master — Christ
