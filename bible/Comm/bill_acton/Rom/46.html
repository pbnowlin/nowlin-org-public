• A Summary Statement
    • It is a statement of fact — not exhortation
    • It is a declaration of our position
    • It is true of all believers
      • We have been freed from the rule of sin
        • Does not mean sinless perfection
        • Does not mean we are free from our sinful nature
        • Does not mean we are free from temptation
      • We are made servants of righteousness — enslaved
        • We are delivered over to it
        • Righteousness is our new principle of life
          • This principle involves our new nature
          • This principle involves divine power — Phl 2:13
          • This divine process will work in us until we are perfected — Col 1; Eph 4
  • Our New Service
    • A new Master — Christ
      • By redemption — price paid
      • By birth — spiritual
    • A new freedom
      • By truth — Jhn 8:32
      • By release — Gal 5:1
    • A voluntary service
    • A joyful service
    • A fulfilling service
    • A fruitful service
