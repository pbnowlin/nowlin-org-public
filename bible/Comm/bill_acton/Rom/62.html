  • The Process of God's Providence
    • Foreknowledge
      • It includes determination
      • It includes control
      • It is the set of His Will
      • It is not based on what we would do but what He does
      • Some other texts — 1Pe 1:2, 20; Act 2:23; 15:18
    • Predestination
      • Definition — The effective exercise of His will by which things determined by Him are accomplished
      • Fact — Eph 1:4, 11; Act 13:4
      • Predestined for conformation to the image of Christ
    • God's Call
      • The call is effective summons of elect to salvation — Jhn 6:37
      • The call is an eternal persistence of our Sovereign God
      • The effectual calling is necessary because of the condition of man in total depravity — Eph 1:1-3; Rom 3:9-20
      • Other texts
        • 2Th 2:13-14
        • 1Co 1:23-24
        • 2Ti 1:9
        • Act 16:13, 16
    • Justification
      • The means —Rom 3:24; 4:5
      • The meaning — The act of God by which He declares righteous those who believe in Christ
        • It includes full and complete pardon
        • The pardon is as if the sinner was never guilty
      • The proof — Rom 4:25; 5:1
        • The atonement is perfect
        • The resurrection is proof
      • The Grace — Rom 5:6, 8, 10
        • We were "without strength"
        • We were "yet sinners"
        • We were "enemies"
        • Christ died
    • Glorification
      • We share the Glory of Christ — 1Pe 1:4; Jhn 17:22; Rom 8:16-17; 1Jo 3:1; Phl 3:20-21; Rom 8:29
      • We are secure for eternity
