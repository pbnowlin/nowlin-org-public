• An Honest Confession — Rom 7:14
    • The Law is spiritual
      • Holy in character
      • Must be obeyed in spirit
    • I am of the flesh
      • Sold under sin
      • The old nature is still sinful
      • Carnal lifestyle
        • Living by material means
        • Living by self-effort
  • A Real Discovery — Rom 7:15-17
    • He did not want to sin — Rom 7:15a
      • His new nature
      • The new nature does not want to sin
    • He hated sin — Rom 7:15b
      • New nature hates sin
      • The unregenerate nature delights in sin
    • His heart approved the Law — Rom 7:16
      • "My heart consents to God's Law"
      • "But I am far from following it"
    • His old nature was "doing it"
      • "No longer I"
      • "It is sin dwelling in me"
      • Sin masters man
  • An Accurate Analysis of Himself — Rom 7:18-20
    • Spiritual bankruptcy of the flesh — Rom 7:18a
      • It is total bankruptcy — no good thing
      • It is total depravity — emotional, intellectual, volitional
    • The longing for holiness — "to will is present with me"
    • The fact of human helplessness — "how to do that which is good—I find not"
    • Summary
      • There is no good thing in our old nature
      • We cannot will to do good
      • We are totally dependent on the Holy Spirit for any goodness
  • The Bitter Conflict — Rom 7:21-24
    • Evil is present
      • It is ready to erupt
      • It is active
    • There is delight in holiness — Rom 7:22
    • The powerful principle of sin
      • It is warring in his members
        • Continuously offensive
        • Never at rest
      • Its goal is to take captive
        • Defeat
        • Immobilize
    • The hopeless condition, "O wretched man" — Rom 7:24
    • The right question — "Who shall deliver me from the body of death?
  • The Triumph of the Believer — Rom 7:25
    • There is Someone who can deliver!
    • A body of life
    • There is victory!
    • A thankful heart
