• Paul's Attitude about the Gospel
    • I am a debtor to share it — Rom 1:14
    • I am ready to declare it — Rom 1:15
    • I am not ashamed to live it — Rom 1:16
  • The Explanation of the Gospel
    • The Author — Christ
    • The Aim — Salvation
    • The Ability — Power of God
      • Takes away the penalty of sin
      • Destroys the power of sin
      • Creates new life
    • The appeal — Jew and Greek
      • The Biblical Gospel
        • To glorify God's proclamation of Divine Son
        • A summons to worship the Almighty, Sovereign Lord
        • God-centered
        • The Grace of God
        • Christ, a triumphant Savior accomplishing His purpose
        • Produces true conversions thru regeneration that results in submission to Christ
      • The "New Unbiblical" Gospel
        • To help man feel better and build self-esteem
        • Come to God to solve problems and get out of trouble
        • Man-centered
        • The ability of man
        • Man in control of destiny — God a helpless spectator and defeated Savior
        • Produces a mild interest in Christ as long as man can still live his way
  • The Righteousness that Is by Faith
    • The righteousness of God
      • It is His attribute of perfect holiness
      • It is revealed in His law
      • It is demanded of us
      • Disobedience demands punishment
    • God's righteousness satisfied
      • In the life of Christ
      • In the death of Christ
    • God's righteousness given
      • By grace
      • Through faith
    • The righteous live by faith
