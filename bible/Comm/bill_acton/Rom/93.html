• Response to God
    • Give your body as an offering to God
    • Do not follow the world in selfish living
    • Be transformed — be renewing your mind
  • Response to Self
    • Do not think too highly of yourself
    • Think with sober judgment
  • Response to the Body of Christ
    • There is one body
    • We are to recognize oneness — each one belongs to the other
    • We are to acknowledge the different members
    • Each member serves the body
    • The matter of spiritual gifts — graces
      • The abuses
      • The proper use
      • Prophesying — declaration of God"s Truth
      • Serving
      • Teaching
      • Exhortation
      • Giving
      • Leadership — management
      • Mercy
  • The Exercise of Your Spiritual Gifts
    • Use what you have
    • Do what you enjoy
    • Do what is obvious
    • Think simply and humbly
    • Begin now, do not wait for perfection
    • Count on God"s power and authority
    • Do not worry about naming your gifts
    • Rejoice over the gifts of others
    • Seek and accept the counsel of the other members
    • Do it all in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ
  • Conclusions
    • The Holy Spirit is the giver and the administrator of gifts
    • He is sovereign
    • Brings gifted people to the local Church
    • Church should be built around people, not programs
    • Each member to serve with humility in God-given place and way
    • No believer can have fullness of joy without exercising gifts
    • No Church can have the fullness of power and influence without the exercise of spiritual gifts — graces
    • The purpose — Eph 4
    • Strong local Churches are the only hope for a righteous nation
