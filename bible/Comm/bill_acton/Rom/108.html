• The Pattern of Paul's Life — 1Ti 1:15-16
    • Before conversion — a pattern of God"s goodness
      • Paul's depravity — Act 8:1-3
      • God's patience — Act 9:1-2
    • At conversion — a pattern of God's grace — Act 9
      • God took the initiative in Paul's salvation
      • Christ was revealed to Paul
      • Paul responded to God's grace — Act 9
    • After conversion — a pattern of God's greatness
      • God's power changed Paul's life — 2Co 5:17
      • God's power kept Paul through trials — 2Co 11:24
      • God's power gave Paul a ministry — Rom 15:15-16
  • The Character of Paul's Ministry — Rom 15:17-28
    • A Christ-centered ministry — Rom 15:17
    • An authoritative ministry — Rom 15:18-19
    • An outgoing ministry — Rom 15:19-20
    • A compassionate ministry — Rom 15:25-28; 2Co 8
