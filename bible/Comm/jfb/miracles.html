Chronological Table of the Miracles of Christ

by David Brown

On the order of some of our Lord's Miracles and Parables, the data being scanty, considerable difference obtains.
Miracles. Where Wrought. Where Recorded.
Water made wine Cana Joh 2:1-11.
Traders cast out of the temple Jerusalem Joh 2:13-17.
Nobleman's son healed Cana Joh 4:46-54.
First miraculous draught of fishes Sea of Galilee Luk 5:1-11.
Leper healed Capernaum Mat 8:2-4; Mark 1:40-45; Luk 5:12-15.
Centurion's servant healed Capernaum Mat 8:5-13; Luk 7:1-10.
Widow's son raised to life Nain Luk 7:11-17.
Demoniac healed Capernaum a 1:21-28; Luk 4:31-37.
Peter's mother-in-law healed Capernaum Mat 8:14-15; Mar 1:29-31; Luk 4:38-39.
Paralytic healed Capernaum Mat 9:2-8; Mar 2:1-12; Luk 5:17-26.
Impotent man healed Jerusalem Joh 5:1-16.
Man with withered hand healed Galilee Mat 12:10-14; Mar 3:1-6; Luk 6:6-11.
Blind and dumb demoniac healed Galilee Mat 12:22-24; Luk 11:14.
Tempest stilled Sea of Galilee Mat 8:23-27; Mar 4:35-41; Luk 8:22-25.
Demoniacs dispossessed Gadara Mat 8:28-34; Mar 5:1-20.
Jairus' daughter raised to life Capernaum Mat 9:18-26; Mar 5:22-24; Luk 8:41-56.
Issue of blood healed Near Capernaum Mat 9:18-26; Mar 5:22-24; Luk 8:41-56.
Two blind men restored to sight Capernaum Mat 9:27-31.
Dumb demoniac healed Capernaum Mat 9:32-34.
Five thousand miraculously fed Decapolis Mat 14:13-21; Mar 6:31-44; Luk 9:10-17; Joh 6:5-14.
Jesus walks on the sea Sea of Galilee Mat 14:22-33; Mar 6:45-52; Joh 6:15-21.
SyrophSnician's daughter healed Coasts of Tyre and Sidon Mat 15:21-28; Mar 7:24-30.
Deaf and dumb man healed Decapolis Mar 7:31-37.
Four thousand fed Decapolis Mat 15:32-39; Mar 8:1-9.
Blind man restored to sight Bethsaida Mr 8:22-26.
Demoniac and lunatic boy healed Near Caesarea Philippi Mat 17:14-21; Mar 9:14-29; Luk 9:37-43.
Miraculous provision of tribute Capernaum Mat 17:24-27.
The eyes of one born blind opened Jerusalem Joh 9:1-41.
Woman, of eighteen years' infirmity, cured [Perea.] Luk 13:10-17.
Dropsical man healed [Perea.] Luk 14:1-6.
Ten lepers cleansed Borders of Samaria Luk 17:11-19.
Lazarus raised to life Bethany Joh 11:1-46.
Two blind beggars restored to sight Jericho Mat 20:29-34; Mar 10:46-52; Luk 18:35-43.
Barren fig tree blighted Bethany Mat 21:12-13, 18-19; Mar 11:12-24.
Buyers and sellers again cast out Jerusalem Luk 19:45-46.
Malchus' ear healed Gethsemane Mat 26:51-54; Mar 14:47-49; Luk 22:50-51,; Joh 18:10-11.
Second draught of fishes Sea of Galilee Joh 21:1-14.
