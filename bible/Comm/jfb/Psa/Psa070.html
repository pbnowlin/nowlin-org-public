Commentary by A. R. FAUSSET
PSALM 70

Psa 70:1-5.

This corresponds to JFB for Ps 40:13-17 with a very few variations, as "turn back" (Psa 70:3) for "desolate," and "make haste unto me" (Psa 70:5) for "thinketh upon me." It forms a suitable appendix to the preceding, and is called "a Psalm to bring to remembrance," as the thirty-eighth [see on JFB for Psa 38:1, title].
