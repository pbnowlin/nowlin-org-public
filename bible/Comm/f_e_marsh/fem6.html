6. ABUNDANCE

1. Saved in abundant grace. "Where sin abounded" (Rom 5:20).

2. Blessed according to abundant mercy. "Which according to His abundant mercy hath begotten us again" (1Pe 1:3).

3. Energised with abundant life. "That they might have life, and that they might have it more abundantly" (Jhn 10:10).

4. Calmed in abundant peace. "Abundance of peace as long as the moon endured" (Psa 72:7).

5. Rejoiced with abundant joy. "Your joy may be more abundant in Christ Jesus" (Phl 1:26).

6. Satisfied with abundant goodness. "They shall be abundantly satisfied with the goodness of Thy house" (Psa 36:8).

7. Prayer answered in abundant manner. "Able to do exceeding abundantly" (Eph 3:20).
