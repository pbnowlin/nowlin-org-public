480. WHAT DOES IT MEAN TO BE SAVED

1. Submission to Christ. "The Church is subject unto Christ" (Eph 5:24).

2. Apprehended by Christ. "I am apprehended of Christ Jesus" (Phl 3:12).

3. Vitalized in Christ. "I can do all things through Christ" (Phl 4:13).

4. Enchanted with Christ. "Whom have I in heaven?" (Psa 73:25).

5· Decided for Christ. "I believe that Jesus Christ is the Son of God" (Act 8:37).
