266. LOVE OF GOD

Note the following seven points in relation to the forgiving love of God:

1. Unmerited in its bestowment (Col 1:12-14).

2. Divine, in its source (Psa 130:4).

3. Costly, in its purchase (Eph 1:7).

4. Free, in its manner (Rom 3:24-25).

5. Full, in its extent (Psa 103:3).

6. Practical, in its outcome (Luk 7:42).

7. Known, in the experience (1Jo 2:12).
