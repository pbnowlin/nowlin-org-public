464. UNDERSTANDING HEART

An understanding heart is a—

1. Christ-knowing Heart. "That we may know Him that is true" (1Jo 5:20).

2. Truth-enlightened Heart. "The eyes of your understanding being enlightened" (Eph 1:18).

3. Christ-instructed Heart. "Then opened He their understanding" (Luk 24:45).

4. Faith-influenced Heart. "Through faith we understand that the worlds were framed by the Word of God" (Hbr 11:3).

5. Bible-loving Heart. "Daniel understood by books" (Dan 9:2).

6. Word-keeping Heart. "That I may live, and keep Thy Word" (Psa 119:17).

7. Grace-taught Heart. "The Lord give thee understanding in all things" (2Ti 2:7).
