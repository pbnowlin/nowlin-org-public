341. SAVED

1. Saved by grace as to the source of salvation. "By grace ye are saved" (Eph 2:5).

2. Saved by Christ as to the means of salvation. "By Me if any man enter in, he shall be saved" (Jhn 10:9).

3. Saved by the Holy Spirit as to the regeneration and renewal of salvation. "Regeneration, and renewing of, the Holy Ghost" (Tts 3:5).

4. Saved by faith as to the reception of salvation. "Thy faith hath saved thee" (Luk 7:50).

5. Saved by the Gospel as to the knowledge of salvation. "By which also ye are saved…I preached unto you" (1Cr 15:2).

6. Saved by the risen life of Christ as to the keeping of salvation. "We shall be saved by His life" (Rom 5:10).

7. Saved by abiding in Christ as to the fruitfulness of salvation "Except these abide in the ship, ye cannot be saved" (Act 27:31).

8. Saved by baptism as to the confession of salvation. "Baptism doth also now save us" (1Pe 3:21).

9. Saved by hope as to the prospect of salvation. "We are saved by hope" (Rom 8:24).

10. Saved by endurance as to the reality of salvation. "He that endureth to the end shall be saved" (Mat 10:22).

11. Saved by fire as to service. "He himself shall be saved, yet so as by fire" (1Cr 3:15).
