10. ALL SAINTS

The late Henry Dyer was once asked where he worshipped. He replied "that he attended three churches, viz., 'Holy Trinity,' 'Christ's Church,' and 'All Saints.'"

1. In reference to the Lord's Coming. "The coming of our Lord Jesus Christ with all His saints" (1Th 3:13).

2. Refers to Paul when he says: "Who am less than the least of all saints" (Eph 3:8).

3. Paul's salutations. Salute…all the saints" (Rom 16:15; Hbr 13:24).

4. The saints' salutations. "All the saints salute you" (2Cr 13:13; Phl 4:22).

5. Paul's Epistle to the Philippians. "All the saints in Christ Jesus" (Phl 1:1).

6. Paul's Prayer for the Ephesians. "May be able to comprehend with all saints" (Eph 3:18).

7. Paul's Exhortation to the Ephesians. "Praying always …for all saints" (Eph 6:18).

8. Love to the saints. "Love unto all the saints" (Eph 1:15; Phm 1:5).
