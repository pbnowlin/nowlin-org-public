359. SEVEN RESURRECTIONS

1. The persons whom Christ raised as shewing forth His power (Mat 11:5).

2. Christ's resurrection as manifesting His pre-eminence (1Cr 15:20).

3. Believer's resurrection with Christ, declaring our oneness with Him (Eph 2:6).

4. Israel's national resurrection, or God's purpose accomplished concerning them (Eze 37:11-12).

5. The resurrection of Anti-Christ, or the person that Satan shall raise (Rev 17:8).

6. The resurrection of the bodies of believers who have died (Jud 1:9; Mat 27:53; 1Th 4:16; Rev 6:9-12; Rev 20:4).

7. Resurrection of the unsaved (Rev 20:5, 11-13).
