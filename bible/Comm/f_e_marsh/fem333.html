333. SAFETY

The following is a sevenfold illustration of the Christian's safety as found in the Old Testament.

1. Jehovah's name. The safety of the name of Jehovah is as a tower to befriend in the time of need (Pro 18:10).

2. Jehovah's presence. The safety of God's presence is a covering to protect in danger (Deu 33:12).

3. Jehovah's promise. The safety of God's promise is a refuge from the snare of fear (Pro 24:25).

4. Jehovah's power. The safety of God's power is an uplifter to put us out of the enemy's reach (Psa 12:5).

5. Jehovah's arm. The safety of God's arm is a strengthener to sustain at all times (Psa 119:117).

6. Jehovah's leading. The safety of God's leading is a safeguard in all difficulty (Psa 78:53).

7. Jehovah's keeping. The safety of God's keeping is a minister of calm repose (Psa 4:8).
