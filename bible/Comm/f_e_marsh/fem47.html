47. CHAIN OF GOOD THINGS

The believer in Christ ever says, with the Psalmist, "I have no good beyond Thee" (Psa 16:2, R.V.).

1. In ourselves there is no good thing (Rom 7:18).

2. But the good that has been planted within us (2Ti 1:14).

3. Which is the outcome of the good work of the Lord (Phl 1:6).

4. Is productive of good works (Eph 2:10).

5. So that by His grace we have a good conscience (1Ti 1:19).

6. And fight a good warfare (1Ti 6:12).

7. Thus are we good soldiers of Jesus Christ (2Ti 2:3).
