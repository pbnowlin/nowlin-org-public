29. BENEFITS OF CHRIST'S RESURRECTION

There are so many benefits associated with the resurrection of Christ, that we can only give a few. The following seven are among the many:―

1. Blessing (Act 3:26).

2. Justification (Rom 4:24-25).

3. Holiness (2Co 5:15).

4. Power (Eph 1:20).

5. Fruit-bearing (Rom 7:4).

6. Quickening (Rom 8:11).

7. Pledge of our loved ones who have fallen asleep coming with Christ (1Th 4:14).
