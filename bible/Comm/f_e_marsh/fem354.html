354. SEVEN POSITIONS THE LORD HOLDS
FOR HIS PEOPLE

1. Within us—Power. "Christ liveth in me" (Gal 2:20).

2. Before us—Example. "When He putteth forth His own sheep, He goeth before them" (Jhn 10:4).

3. Above us—Representative. "To appear in the presence of God for us" (Hbr 9:24).

4. Beneath us—Sustainer. "Underneath are the everlasting arms" (Deu 33:27).

5. Beside us—Guide. "He is at my right hand, I shall not be moved" (Psa 16:8).

6. Around us—Deliverer. "The Angel of the Lord encampeth round about them that fear Him" (Psa 34:7).

7. Behind us—Protector. "The God of Israel will be your Rereward" (Isa 52:12).
