402. THE DIVINE HELPER

"The Lord is my Helper" (Hbr 13:6).

1. Past Helper. "Thou hast been my Help" (Psa 63:7).

2. Present Helper. "A very present Help in trouble" (Psa 46:1).

3. Powerful Helper. "I have laid help upon One that is mighty" (Psa 89:19).

4. Protecting Helper. "The Lord God will help me" (Isa 50:7, 9).

5. Precious Helper. "I will help thee" (Isa 41:10, 13-14).

6. Providing Helper. "The Helper of the fatherless" (Psa 10:14).

7. Perpetual Helper. "The God of Jeshurun, who rideth upon the heaven, in thy help" (Deu 33:26).
