111. DEATH OF CHRIST

1. The death of Christ is the record of the greatest sin that was ever committed (Act 2:23).

2. The exhibition of the greatest love that was ever seen (Rom 5:8).

3. The manifestation of the greatest victory that was ever achieved (Hbr 2:14).

4. The introduction of the greatest force that has ever operated (1Jo 3:16).

5. The unlocking of the greatest problem that has ever engaged man's attention (1Pe 1:11).

6. The supply of the greatest incentive that has ever been given (2Cr 5:14).

7. The making known of the greatest example we can ever imitate (Eph 5:2).
