479. WHAT CHRIST WAS MADE

The following seven Scriptures, where the word "made" occurs, will indicate what Christ became and suffered:—

1. His Condescension. "Made flesh" (Jhn 1:14).

2. His Identification. "Made in the likeness of men" (Phl 2:7).

3· His Humiliation. "Made of a woman" (Gal 4:4).

4. His Compassion. "Made under the law" (Gal 4:4).

5. His Obedience. "Became1 obedient unto death" (Phl 2:8).

6. His Condemnation. "Made a curse" (Gal 3:13).

7. His Sin-bearing. "Made Him to be sin" (2Cr 5:21).

1Same word as in the previous verses.
