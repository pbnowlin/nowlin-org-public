472. WALK

Those who are saved by Christ, endeavour to fulfil His commands to—

1. Walk in Love. "Walk in love, as Christ also hath loved us" (Eph 5:2).

2. Walk in the Truth. "Thou walkest in the Truth" (3Jo 1:3).

3. Walk in the Spirit. "Walk in the Spirit" (Gal 5:16).

4. Walk by Faith. "We walk by faith" (2Cr 5:7).

5. Walk in Newness of Life. "Walk in newness of life" (Rom 6:4).

6. Walk Honestly. "Let us walk honestly" (Rom 13:13).

7. Walk Worthy of the Lord. "Walk worthy of the Lord unto all pleasing" (Col 1:10).
