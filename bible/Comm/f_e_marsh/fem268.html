268. LOVE OF JESUS

1. The Definiteness of Love. "Jesus loved Martha and her sister and Lazarus" (Jhn 11:5).

2. Perpetuity of Love. "Having loved His own which were in the world, He loved them unto the end" (Jhn 13:1).

3. Favour of Love. "Leaning on Jesus' bosom one of His disciples, whom Jesus loved" (Jhn 13:23).

4. Example of Love. "Love one another, as I have loved you" (Jhn 13:34).

5. Record of Love. "That disciple whom Jesus loved saith unto Peter, It is the Lord" (Jhn 21:7).

6. Attractiveness of Love. "Peter, seeth the disciple whom Jesus loved" (Jhn 21:20).

7. Commission of Love. "Jesus saw the disciple whom He loved" (Jhn 19:26).
