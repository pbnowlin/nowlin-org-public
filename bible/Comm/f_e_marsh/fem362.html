362. SEVEN TABLES

1. Table of shittim wood. "Thou shalt also make a table of shittim wood" (Exd 25:23).

2. Anointed table. "Thou shalt anoint…the table" (Exd 30:26).

3. Holy table. "Upon the pure table before the Lord" (Lev 24:6).

4. Provided table. "He shall set it in order before the Lord" (Lev 24:8).

5. Prepared table. "Thou preparest a table before me" (Psa 23:5).

6. The King's table. "While the King sitteth at His table" (Sgs 1:12).

7. The Lord's table. "Be partakers of the Lord's table" (1Cr 10:21).
