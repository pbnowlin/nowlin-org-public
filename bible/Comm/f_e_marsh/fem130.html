130. FACE OF JESUS

1. Strong face. "He stedfastly set His face to go to Jerusalem" (Luk 9:51).

2. Bowed face. "And fell on His face" (Mat 26:39).

3. An insulted face. "Then did they spit in His face" (Mat 26:67).

4. Covered face. "And to cover His face" (Mar 14:65).

5. Struck face. "They struck Him on the face" (Luk 22:64).

6. Shining face. "His face did shine as the sun" (Mat 17:2).

7. Glorious face. "Glory of God in the face of Jesus Christ" (2Cr 4:6).

8. Awe-inspiring face. "Hide us from the face of Him" (Rev 6:16; Rev 20:11).

9. Gladdening face. "And they shall see His face" (Rev 22:4).
