72. CHRIST, OUR LIFE

THERE are many words and phrases that are peculiar to John; for instance, the double "verily," "excepts," and "true" things. John's Gospel is peculiar in itself. Matthew shows us Christ as "King," Mark as the "Servant," Luke as the "Man," and John as the "Life." This may be traced right through John.
