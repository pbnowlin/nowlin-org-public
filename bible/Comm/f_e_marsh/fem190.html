190. "HIS NAME"

1. Separated to His Name. "To take out of them a people for His Name" (Act 15:14).

2. Sonship through faith in His Name. "Power to become the sons of God, even to them that believe on His Name" (Jhn 1:12).

3. Salvation in His Name. "Thou shalt call His Name Jesus, for He shall save His people from their sins" (Mat 1:21).

4. Suffering for His Name. "Rejoicing that they were counted worthy to suffer shame for His Name" (Act 5:41).

5. Separated by His Name. "Let every one that nameth the Name of Christ depart from iniquity" (2Ti 2:19).

6. Submission to His Name. "That the Name of our Lord Jesus Christ may be glorified in you" (2Th 1:12).

7. Service in His Name. "For My Name's sake hast laboured" (Rev 2:3).
