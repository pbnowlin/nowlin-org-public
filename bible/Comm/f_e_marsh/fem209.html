209. INQUIRING—DIRECTED

To inquire of the Lord is to be directed by Him (2Sa 2:1). The following seven links in the golden chain of the spiritual life are always connected, namely:—

1. Faith in the Lord (Jhn 3:16).

2. Dependence on the Lord (Psa 37:7).

3. Walking with the Lord (Mal 2:6).

4. Inquiry of the Lord (Psa 27:14).

5. Direction by the Lord (Act 13:2).

6. Blessing from the Lord (Deu 33:20).

7. Glory to the Lord (1Cr 10:31).

The blessing of the Lord's guidance always comes along the lines of prayer and obedience. Self acts upon its own authority, and is discomfited, as was Balaam (Num 22:22-35), but faith goes at God's bidding, and is delivered (Exd 14:15).
