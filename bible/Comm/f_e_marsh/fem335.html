335. SALVATION

What is comprehended in the most comprehensive word "salvation"? The following acrostic will indicate, in some small degree, Salvation.

From To
S    Sin (Mat 1:21). S    Sanctification (Hbr 10:10, 14).
A    Alienation (Eph 2:12). A    Adoption (Eph 1:5).
L    Lifelessness (Eph 2:1). L    Life eternal (Jhn 5:24).
V    Vileness (Psa 51:5). V    Virtue (2Pe 1:3).
A    Aversion (Rom 8:7). A    Acceptance (Eph 1:6).
T    Thoughtlessness (Isa 1:3). T    Thoughtfulness (Phl 4:8).
I     Independence (Psa 14:1). I     In-ness (2Cr 5:17).
O    Obstinacy (Isa 48:4). O    Obedience (Jhn 10:27).
N    Neglectfulness (Pro 13:4). N    Nobleness (Act 17:11).
