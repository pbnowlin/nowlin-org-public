181. "HALLELUJAH! WHAT A SAVIOUR"

1. Almighty Saviour to liberate us (Psa 106:21).

2. Bruised Saviour to redeem us (Act 5:30-31).

3. Comely Saviour to adorn us (2Pe 1:1).

4. Divine Saviour to bless us (Tts 3:4).

5. Eternal Saviour to secure us (Jud 1:25).

6. Faithful Saviour to keep us (Phl 3:20).

7. Great Saviour to deliver us (Isa 19:20).

8. Holy Saviour to sanctify us (Isa 43:3).

9. Indwelling Saviour to rule us (Isa 43:11).

10. Joyous Saviour to gladden us (Zep 3:17).

11. Kind Saviour to crown us (Tts 3:6).

12. Living Saviour to be with us (1Ti 4:10).

13. Mighty Saviour to enable us (2Pe 3:18).

14. Near Saviour to guard us (Exd 14:30).

15. Overcoming Saviour to defend us (Isa 63:9).

16. Providing Saviour to enrich us (Jhn 4:42).

17. Quickening Saviour to change us (Eph 5:23).

18. Righteous Saviour to clear us (Isa 45:21).

19. Strong Saviour to uphold us (Mat 14:31).

20. Tried Saviour to befriend us (Hbr 2:10).

21. Unlimited Saviour to save us (Hbr 7:25).

22. Virtuous Saviour to purify us (Mat 1:21).

23. Wise Saviour to teach us (Jam 1:21).

24. 'Xcelsior Saviour—no other like Him (Act 4:12).

25. Yearning Saviour to sympathize with us (2Ti 1:9).

26. Zealous Saviour to serve us (1Ti 1:15).
