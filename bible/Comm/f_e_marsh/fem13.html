13. ALPHABET ON THE HOLY SPIRIT

A. Advocate, to plead in us. "I will pray the Father, and He shall give you another Advocate" (Jhn 14:16, R.V., M.).

Anointing, for power. "Hath anointed us" (2Cr 1:21).

B. Baptism, to unite to Christ. "By one Spirit are we all baptised into one body" (1Cr 12:13).

C. Comforter, to cheer. "He shall give you another Comforter" (Jhn 14:16).

Convincer, to alarm. "He will reprove the world of sin" (Jhn 16:8).

D. Director, to guide. "Led by the Spirit of God" (Rom 8:14).

Dew, to refresh. "I will be as the dew" (Hsa 14:5).

E. Earnest, to assure. "Which is the Earnest of our inheritance" (Eph 1:14).

Enlightener, to reveal. "The true Light now shineth" (1Jo 2:8).

F. Fulness, to enrich. "Be filled with the Spirit" (Eph 5:18).

Fruit, to manifest. "The fruit of the Spirit is" &c. (Gal 5:22).

G. Guide, to lead. "He will guide you into all truth" (Jhn 15:13).

Spirit of Glory, to remind. "The Spirit of Glory and of God resteth on you" (1Pe 4:14).

H. Helper, to enable. "The Spirit also helpeth our infirmities" (Rom 8:26).

Holy Spirit, to sanctify. "Grieve not the Holy Spirit of God" (Eph 4:30).

I. Indweller, to occupy. "The Spirit of God dwelleth in you" (1Cr 3:16).

Illuminator, to reveal. "He will show you things to come" (Jhn 16:13).

J. Joy, to gladden. "Fill you with all joy…through the power of the Holy Ghost" (Rom 15:13).

K. King, to rule. "The Lord is that Spirit, and where the Spirit f the Lord is, there is liberty" (2Cr 3:17).

L. Life, to ennoble. "The Spirit of Life" (Rom 8:2).

Liberator, to free. "Hath made me free from the law of sin and death" (Rom 8:2).

M. Mark of God, that we are His. "Also sealed us and given us the earnest of the Spirit" (2Cr 1:22).

N. New nature Begetter. "That which is born of the Spirit is spirit" (Jhn 3:6).

O. Oil of gladness. "God hath anointed thee with the oil of gladness" (Psa 45:7).

P. Power, to strengthen. "Strengthened with might by His Spirit" (Eph 3:16).

R. Renewer, to invigorate. "The inward man is renewed day by day" (2Cr 4:16).

S. Searcher, to search. "The Spirit searcheth all things" (1Cr 2:10).

Sanctifier, to separate. "Sanctified by the Holy Ghost" (Rom 15:16).

T. Teacher, to instruct. "He shall teach you all things" (Jhn 14:26).

Spirit of Truth to arm. "The Spirit of Truth" (Jhn 15:26).

U. Unction, to empower. "Ye have an unction from the Holy One" (1Jo 2:20).

Understanding, to discern. "The Spirit of Wisdom and Understanding" (Isa 11:2).

W. Witness, to establish. "The Spirit Himself beareth witness" (Rom 8:16, R.V.).

Well of Living Water. "The water that I shall give him shall be in him a well of water" &c. (Jhn 4:14).

Z. Zeal, to inspire. "I will pour out of My Spirit upon all flesh" &c. (Act 2:17).
