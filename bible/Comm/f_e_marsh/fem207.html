207. "IN MY NAME"

1. Surrendering. Forsaking all for My Name sake. "Everyone that hath forsaken houses…for My Name's sake" (Mat 19:29).

2. Separation. Gathered in My Name. "Gathered together in My Name" (Mat 18:20).

3. Suffering. Hated for My Name's sake. "Ye shall be hated of all men for My Name's sake" (Mat 10:22).

4. Serving. Working in My Name. "Whosoever shall give you a cup of water to drink in My Name" (Mar 9:41).

5. Supplicating. Praying in My Name. "Whatsoever ye shall ask in My Name" (Jhn 14:13).

6. Sincerity. Labouring for My Name's sake. "For My Name's sake hast laboured" (Rev 2:3).

7. Strength. Holding fast in My Name. "Thou holdest fast My Name" (Rev 2:13).
