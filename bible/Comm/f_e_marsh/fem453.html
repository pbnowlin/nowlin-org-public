453. THINGS OF CHRIST IN PHILIPPIANS

1. Day of Christ (Phl 1:10). Yet to come.

2. Gospel of Christ (Phl 1:27). To be preached.

3. Work of Christ (Phl 2:30). Our employment.

4. Knowledge of Christ (Phl 3:8). Our assurance.

5. Faith of Christ (Phl 3:9). Our study.

6. Apprehended of Christ (Phl 3:12). Our security.

7. Cross of Christ (Phl 3:18). Our suffering.
