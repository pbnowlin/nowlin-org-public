96. CONDITIONS OF POWER

1. Saved by Christ's grace. "Thou hast neither part nor lot in this matter, for thy heart is not right in the sight of God" (Act 8:21).

2. Standing in Christ's acceptance. "The Gospel…which also ye have received and wherein ye stand" (1Cr 15:1).

3. Obedience to Christ. "The Holy Ghost, whom God hath given to them that obey Him" (Act 5:32).

4. Fellowship with Christ. "By Whom ye were called unto the fellowship of His Son, Jesus Christ our Lord" (1Cr 1:9).

5. Waiting upon Christ. "These all continued with one accord in prayer and supplication" (Act 1:14).

6. Expecting from Christ. Tarry ye in the city of Jerusalem until ye be endued with power from on high" (Luk 24:49).

7. Faith in Christ. "Doeth he it by the works of the law or by the hearing of faith?" (Gal 3:2, 5).
