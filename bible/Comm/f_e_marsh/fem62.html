62. CHRIST FOR US

Different characters in which Christ appears for us in Hebrews 9 & 10.

1. As the Propitiation, to atone (Hbr 9:12).

2. As the Purifier, to cleanse (Hbr 9:14).

3. As the Mediator, to act (Hbr 9:15).

4. As the Priest, to represent (Hbr 9:24).

5. As the Sin-Remover, to bless (Hbr 9:26).

6. As the Substitute, to suffer (Hbr 9:28).

7. As the Saviour, to deliver (Hbr 9:28).

8. As the Willing One in obedience (Hbr 10:5-9).

9. As the Sanctifier, to separate (Hbr 10:10).

10. As the Sin-offering, to save (Hbr 10:11-12).

11. As the Exalted Man in glory (Hbr 10:12).

12. As the Expectant One, to triumph (Hbr 10:13).

13. As the Perfecter of His people (Hbr 10:14).

14. As the Fore-runner, to procure (Hbr 10:19-20).

15. As the High Priest, to represent (Hbr 10:21).
