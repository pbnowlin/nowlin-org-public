182. HANDS OF CHRIST

1. On His hands, the privilege of believers (Isa 49:16).

2. Under His hand, or His hand upon us, the pavilion to hide (Exd 33:22; 1Ki 18:46; Ezr 7:6).

3. His hand under us, the position of benefit (Sgs 2:6; Isa 61:10).

4. In His hand the place of blessing (Deu 33:3).
