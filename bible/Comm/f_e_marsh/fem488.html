488. WHAT IS A CHRISTIAN?

1. A Christian is one who has life in Christ. "He that hath the Son hath life" (1Jo 5:12).

2. A Christian is one who has liberty by Christ. "The Spirit of life in Christ Jesus hath made me free" (Rom 8:2).

3. A Christian is one who has received light from Christ. "He that followeth Me…shall have the light of life" (Jhn 8:12).

4. A Christian is one who has likeness to Christ. "Changed into the same image" 2Co 3:18).

5. A Christian is one who seeks to love as Christ. "Ye love one another, as I have loved you" (Jhn 15:12).

6. A Christian is one who is under the Lordship of Christ. "Lord, both of the dead and living" (Rom 14:9).

7· A Christian is one who is learning of Christ. "Learn of Me" (Mat 11:29).
