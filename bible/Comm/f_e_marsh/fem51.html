51. CLEAVING TO CHRIST

ACTS 11:23

The following seven ways will indicate how we can cleave to Christ:—

1. By prayerful dependence upon Him (Phl 4:6).

2. By being taught by Him (Mat 11:29).

3. By ready response to His word (Jhn 14:21).

4. By whole-hearted surrender to Him (Rom 12:1).

5. By separation from the ungodly (2Cr 6:17).

6. By living a life of faith upon Him (Gal 2:20).

7. By waiting for Him (1Th 1:10).
