206. "IN ME"

What manifold evidence there was in the life of Paul to the truth, that Christ lived in him. See the following Scriptures, noticing especially the words, "In me":—

1. Salvation by Him. "Revealed His Son in me" (Gal 1:16).

2. Shining for Him. "They glorified God in me" (Gal 1:24).

3. Sanctification by Him. "Seen in me" (Phl 4:9; 1Ti 1:16).

4. Service with Him. "Christ speaking in me" (2Cr 13:3).

5. Settled confidence in Him. "Truth of Christ is in me (2Cr 11:10).

6. Suffering for Him. "Conflict in me" (Phl 1:30).

7. Spirit manifested. "Mighty in me" (Gal 2:8; Col 1:29).
