205. IN LOVE

1. Continue in love. "Continue ye in My love" (Jhn 15:9; Hbr 13:1).

2. Increase in love. "Make you to increase and abound in love" (1Th 3:12).

3. Abound in love. "Ye yourselves are taught of God to love one another…that ye increase more and more" (1Th 4:9-10).

4. Walk in love. "Walk in love, as Christ also hath loved us" (Eph 5:2).

5. Be fervent in love. "See that ye love one another with a pure heart fervently" (1Pe 1:22; 1Pe 4:8).

6. Unfeigned in love. "By love unfeigned" (2Cr 6:6; Rom 12:9).

7. Be perfected in love. "Perfect love casteth out fear" (1Jo 4:18).
