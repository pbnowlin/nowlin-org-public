409. THE HOLY SPIRIT* IN ROMANS 8

1. Spirit of Life to liberate. "The Spirit of life…hath made me free" (Rom 8:2).

2. Spirit of God to indwell. "The Spirit of God dwell in you" (Rom 8:9).

3. Spirit of Christ to identify. "Have not the Spirit of Christ, he is none of His" (Rom 8:9).

4. Spirit of Power to raise. "The Spirit of Him that raised up Jesus" (Rom 8:11).

5. Spirit of Might to quicken. "Quicken your mortal bodies by His Spirit" (Rom 8:11).

6. Spirit of Leading to guide. "Led by the Spirit of God" (Rom 8:14).

7. Spirit of Witnessing to cheer. "The Spirit Himself beareth witness" (Rom 8:16, R.V.).

8. Spirit of First-fruits to pledge. "The first-fruits of the Spirit" (Rom 8:23).

9. Spirit of Help to enable. "The Spirit also helpeth our infirmities" (Rom 8:26).

10. Spirit of Intercession to plead. "The Spirit Himself maketh intercession for us" (Rom 8:26, R.V.).

11. Spirit of Interpretation to make known. "The mind of the Spirit" (Rom 8:27).

* The word "spirit" which occurs in the rest of the chapter, refers to the new nature, as may be gathered from the R.V., where the term is given with a small s.
