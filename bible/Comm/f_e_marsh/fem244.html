244. JOYFUL ONES

There are at least seven parties who rejoice at the repenting of a soul.

1. The Father (Zep 3:17).

2. The Son (Luk 15:6).

3. The Holy Spirit (Luk 15:9).

4. The angels (Luk 15:7).

5. Loved ones (Hbr 12:23).

6. The Church (Act 15:3).

7. The one who repents (Luk 15:24).
