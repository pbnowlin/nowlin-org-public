369. SEVEN "THOU HASTS" IN PSALM 30

1. Salvation. "Thou hast lifted me up" (Psa 30:1).

2. Sanctification. "Thou hast healed me" (Psa 30:2).

3. Resurrection. "Thou hast brought up my soul from the grave" (Psa 30:3).

4. Preservation. "Thou hast kept me alive" (Psa 30:3).

5. Established. "Thou hast made my mountain to stand strong" (Psa 30:7).

6. Exultation. "Thou hast turned for me my mourning into dancing" (Psa 30:11).

7. Separation and gladness. "Thou hast put off my sackcloth and girded me with gladness" (Psa 30:11).
