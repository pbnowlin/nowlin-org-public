296. PAST AND PRESENT WALK OF THE ONE
WHO NOW BELIEVES IN CHRIST

1. Once walking away from God, as Adam. "And Adam…hid from the presence of the Lord" (Gen 3:8).

Now walking with God, as Enoch. "And Enoch walked with God" (Gen 5:22).

2. Once walking in our own way, as Balaam. "Balaam rose up in the morning…God's anger was kindled because he went" (Num 22:21-22).

Now walking in the truth, as the elect lady and her children. "Walking in truth" (2 John 1:4).

3. Once walking after the world, as Demas. "Demas hath forsaken me, having loved this present evil world" (2Ti 4:10).

Now walking after the world to come, as Abram. "He looked for a city…whose builder and maker is God" (Hbr 11:10).

4. Once walking in unbelief, as Israel. "Because of unbelief they were broken off" (Rom 11:20).

Now walking in faith, as Moses. "By faith he forsook Egypt" (Hbr 11:27).

5. Once walking in darkness, as Judas. "Went immediately out; and it was night" (Jhn 13:30).

Now walking in the light, as John. "If we walk in the light" (1Jo 1:7).

6. Once walking in hatred, as Saul of Tarsus. "Saul yet breathing out threatenings" (Act 9:1).

Now walking in love, as Paul. "Because ye were dear unto us" (1Th 2:8).

7. Once walking in sin, as the Ephesians. "Ye walked according to the course of this world" (Eph 2:2).

Now walking in Christ, as the early Church. "Walking in the fear of the Lord" (Act 9:31).
