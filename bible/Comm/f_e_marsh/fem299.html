299. PEACE

1. Peace is of heavenly origin as to its source. "God is not the author of confusion, but of peace" (1Cr 14:33).

2. Peace is a fruit of the Spirit as to its impartation. "The fruit of the Spirit is…peace" (Gal 5:22).

3. Peace is a product of the Cross as to its channel. "So making peace…by the Cross" (Eph 2:15-16).

4. Peace is a plant of obedience as to its condition. "If ye walk in My statutes, and keep My commandments, I will give peace" (Lev 26:3, 6).

5. Peace is a variegated plant as to its fulness. "Peace I leave with you, My peace I give unto you" (Jhn 14:27).
