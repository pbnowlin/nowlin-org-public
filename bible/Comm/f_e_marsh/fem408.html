408. THE GOSPEL PROCLAIMS

1. Pardon for the guilty (Act 26:18).

2. Peace for the troubled (Eph 2:17).

3. Power for the strengthless (Rom 5:6).

4. Purity for the defiled (Act 15:9).

5. Plenty for the needy (Luk 14:16, 23).

6. Pleasure for the dissatisfied (Phl 4:6-11).

7. Productiveness of the Spirit (Gal 5:22).
