368. SEVEN TIMES CHRIST IS SAID TO
SPEAK TO HIS FATHER

1. When men were rejecting Him. "I thank Thee, O Father…even so, Father" (Mat 11:25-26).

2. At the raising of Lazarus. "Father, I thank Thee that Thou hast heard Me" (Jhn 11:41).

3. When the Cross loomed before Him. "Father, save Me from this hour" (Jhn 12:27-28).

4. In the upper room. "Father, the hour is come" (Jhn 17:1, 5, 11, 21, 24, 25).

5. In the Garden of Gethsemane. "Father, if it be possible" (Mat 26:39, 42).

6. On behalf of His enemies. "Father, forgive them" (Luk 23:34).

7. His last act. "Father, into Thy hands I commend My spirit" (Luk 23:46).
