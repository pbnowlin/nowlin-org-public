269. LOVE'S CHARACTER

The character of His love—

1. Great (Eph 2:4).

2. Inexpressible (Jhn 3:16).

3. Free (Hsa 14:4).

4. Inconceivable (Eph 3:19).

5. Unselfish (1Jo 4:10).

6. Unchanging (Jhn 13:1).

7. Inseparable (Rom 8:35-39).

8. Everlasting (Jer 31:3).

9. Unquenchable (Sgs 8:7).

10. Strong (stronger) as death (Sgs 8:6).

11. Perfect (1Jo 4:18).
