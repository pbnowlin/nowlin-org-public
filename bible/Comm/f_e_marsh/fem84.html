84. CHRIST, THE PROPHET

JESUS was recognised as the Prophet that Moses had spoken of and promised to Israel (Deu 18:15-18).

1. Powerful prophet (Mat 13:54).

2. Royal prophet (Mat 16:16).

3. Omnipotent prophet (Luk 7:16).

4. Penetrating prophet (Jhn 4:19).

5. Healing prophet (Mar 5:29).

6. Enlightening prophet (Jhn 7:40).

7. Truthlul prophet (Jhn 3:34).
