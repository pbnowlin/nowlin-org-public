35. BLINDED

2 CORINTHIANS 4:4

Some are blinded with—

1. Pride, as Naaman (2Ki 5:11, 13).

2. Self-righteousness, as the Pharisee (Luk 18:11).

3. Riches, as the Rich Ruler (Luk 18:23).

4. Worldliness, as Demas (2Ti 4:10).

5. Covetousness, as Balaam (Jud 1:11).

6. Self-will, as Saul (1Sa 15:22-23).

7. Prejudice, as the Rulers (Jhn 7:47).

8. Lust, as Esau (Hbr 12:16-17).
