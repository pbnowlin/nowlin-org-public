113. DEGREES OF FAITH

1. No faith. "How is it that ye have no faith?" (Mar 4:40).

2. Little faith. "O ye of little faith" (Mat 16:8).

3. Great faith. "O woman, great is thy faith" (Mat 15:28).

4. So great faith. "I have not found so great faith" (Mat 8:10).

5. Full of faith. "Full of faith and of the Holy Ghost" (Act 6:5).

6. Weak in faith. "And be ye not weak in faith" (Rom 4:19).

7. Strong in faith. "Was strong in faith, giving glory to God" (Rom 4:20).
