312. PRAYER OF FAITH

1. Confession of sin. "To seek by prayer and supplications" (Dan 9:3-20).

2. Cleansing from sin. "Thy sin purged" (Isa 6:7).

3. Consecration to the Saviour. "Behold, he prayeth" (Act 9:11-20).

4. Confidence in the Saviour. "Said unto the Lord, increase our faith" (Luk 17:5).

5. Courage from the Saviour. "They lifted up their voice to God with one accord" (Act 4:24-33).

6. Calmness in the Saviour. "The peace of God…shall keep your hearts and minds" (Phl 4:6-7).

7. Communion with the Saviour. "Did not our heart burn…while He talked with us" (Luk 24:32).
