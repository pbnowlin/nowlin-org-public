217. "I WILLS" IN PSALM 132

1. The "I will" of exaltation. "Will I set upon thy throne" (Psa 132:11).

2. The "I will" of dwelling. "Here will I dwell" (Psa 132:14).

3. The "I will" of blessing. "I will abundantly bless" (Psa 132:15).

4. The "I will" of satisfaction. "I will satisfy her poor" (Psa 132:15).

5. The "I will" of clothing. "I will also clothe her priests with salvation" (Psa 132:16).

6. The "I will" of growing. "There will I make the horn of David to bud" (Psa 132:17).

7. The "I will" of degradation. "His enemies will I clothe with shame (Psa 132:18).
