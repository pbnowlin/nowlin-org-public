43. CALEB

There are seven things that characterise Caleb.

He was a thorough believer, for he followed the Lord fully (Num 14:24).

He was a courageous witness, for he would not listen to the popular voice (Num 13:30).

He was a separated saint, for he was of "another spirit" than the others (Num 14:24).

He was a prompt actor. "Let us go up at once and possess the land," is his cry (Num 13:30).

He was a confident man. "We are well able to overcome," is his unhesitating cry (Num 13:30).

He was a persecuted servant. The people are not willing to hearken to Joshua and Caleb, but want to stone them (Num 14:10).

He was a rewarded disciple (Num 14:24; Num 32:11-12), for he was privileged to see, enter, and enjoy the land of promise (Jos 14).
