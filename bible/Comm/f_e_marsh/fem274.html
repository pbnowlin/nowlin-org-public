274. MAN OF FAITH

ACTS 6:5

Faith is the root grace that unites the soul to the soil of God's love and power, and causes all the other graces to flourish, hence a "man full of faith" is a—

1. Man of Prayer, as Elijah was. "Elias was a man…he prayed earnestly" (Jam 5:17).

2. Man of Work, as Noah was. "By faith Noah…prepared an ark" (Hbr 11:7).

3. Man of obedience, as Abram was. "Abraham, when he was called…went out not knowing whither he went" (Hbr 11:8).

4. Man of Communion, as Enoch was. "By faith Enoch…pleased God" (Hbr 11:5-6).

5. Man of Separation, as Moses was. "By faith he forsook Egypt," &c. (Hbr 11:25-28).

6. Man of Victory, as David was. "I come to thee in the Name of the Lord of Hosts, the God of the armies of Israel, whom thou hast defied" (1Sa 17:45).

7. Man of Faithfulness, as Samuel was. "Samuel said unto him, the Lord hath rent the kingdom of Israel from thee this day" (1Sa 15:28, 33).
