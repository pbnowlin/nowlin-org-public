146. FOLLOWING CHRIST

"Follow Me" (Jhn 12:26) is Christ's command, after we have believed in Him. We should follow Him—

1. Willingly, as a sheep (Jhn 10:27).

2. Wholly, as a servant (Jhn 12:26).

3. Patiently, as a sufferer (1Pe 2:21).

4. Heartily, as a saved one (Mar 10:52).

5. Promptly, as a disciple (Luk 5:11, 28).

6. Constantly, as a loving one (Jhn 21:20).

7. Imitatingly, as a child of God (Eph 5:1).
