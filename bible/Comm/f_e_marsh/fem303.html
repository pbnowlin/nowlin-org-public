303. PERSONALITY OF THE HOLY SPIRIT

1. The Personal Comforter for fellowship (Jhn 14:16).

2. The Personal Companion to cheer. "He dwelleth with you" (Jhn 14:17).

3. The Personal Teacher to instruct. "He shall teach" (Jhn 14:26).

4. The Personal Witness to testify. "He shall testify of Me" (Jhn 15:26).

5. The Personal Ambassador to represent (Jhn 16:7).

6. The Personal One manifested. "He is come" (Jhn 16:8).

7. The Personal Reprover convicting. "He will convict" (Jhn 16:8, R.V.).

8. The Personal Title-giver. "He, the Spirit of Truth" (Jhn 16:13).

9. The Personal Guide directing. "He will guide" (Jhn 16:13).

10. The Personal Servant listening. "He shall not speak from Himself" (Jhn 16:13, R.V.).

11. The Personal Hearer speaking. "He shall hear" (Jhn 16:13).

12. The Personal Speaker declaring. "That shall He speak" (Jhn 16:13).

13. The Personal Revealer unfolding. "He will show" (Jhn 16:13).

14. The Personal Work accomplished. "He shall glorify Me" (Jhn 16:14).

15. The Personal Receiver making known. "He shall receive of Mine" (Jhn 16:14).
