344. SEEING

"He…came seeing" (Jhn 9:7), so it is said of the blind man. The believer sees the following seven things:—

1. Sin in his members. "I see another law in my members" (Rom 7:23).

2. Jesus crowned with glory. "We see Jesus" (Hbr 2:9).

3. The calling of grace. "Ye see your calling, brethren" (1Cr 1:26).

4. An open heaven. "Hereafter ye shall see heaven open" (Jhn 1:51).

5. God, as he is pure in heart. "Blessed are the pure in heart, for they shall see God" (Mat 5:8).

6. The evil of unbelief. "We see that they could not enter in because of unbelief" (Hbr 3:19).

7. The day of the Lord approaching. "Ye see the day approaching" (Hbr 10:25).
