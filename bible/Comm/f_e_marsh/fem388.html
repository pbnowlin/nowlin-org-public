388. TAKE

1. Salvation. "Take the cup of salvation" (Psa 116:13).

2. Service. "Take My yoke" (Mat 11:29).

3. Separation. "Take ye away the stone" (Jhn 11:39).

4. Suffering. "Take up His cross" (Mat 16:24).

5. Sufficiency. "Take hold of My strength" (Isa 27:5).

6. Supplication. "Take with you words" (Hsa 14:2).

7. Submission. "Take no thought for your life" (Mat 6:25).
