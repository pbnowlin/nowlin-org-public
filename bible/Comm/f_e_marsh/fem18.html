18. ANGELIC MINISTRY

1. Angels point out the way we should go (Exd 23:20; Exd 32:34).

2. Angels prepare us for coming conflict (Jdg 5:11-22).

3. Angels provide for our need in times of discouragement (1Ki 19:5).

4. Angels protect us in times of danger (2Ki 6:17; Psa 34:7).

5. Angels prevent harm from coming to us (Dan 3:28; Dan 6:22).

6. Angels pull us out of difficulties (Gen 19:16; Act 12:7-11).

7. Angels preserve us in all our journeyings (Gen 24:7, 40; Isa 43:9).
