263. LOVE

"GOD IS LOVE" (1 JOHN 4:8, 16).

We have the—

1. God of love (2Cr 13:11). Source of love.

2. Love of God (Rom 5:5). Love overflowing to us.

3. Love of the Father (1Jo 3:1). Manner of His love.

4. Love of Christ (Rom 8:35). Manifestation of His love.

5. Spirit of love (2Ti 1:7). Power of His love.

6. Comfort of love (Phl 2:1). Consolation of His love.

7. Labour of love (Hbr 6:10). His love constraining.
