61. CHRIST BESIDE THE BELIEVER

1. As the Strength, to uphold. "I will uphold thee with the right hand of My righteousness" (Isa 41:10).

2. As the Companion, for fellowship. "Did not our heart burn within us, while He talked with us by the way" (Luk 24:32).

3. As the Friend to counsel. "The sweetness of man's friend by hearty counsel" (Pro 27:9).

4. As the Guide, to direct. "When He, the Spirit of Truth, is come, He will guide you into all truth" (Jhn 16:13).

5. As the High Priest, to succour. "We have not an High Priest which cannot be touched with the feeling of our infirmities" (Hbr 4:15).

6. As the Comforter, to cheer. "He shall give you another Comforter, that He may abide with you for ever" (Jhn 14:16).

7. As the Teacher, to teach. "He shall teach you all things, and bring all things to your remembrance" (Jhn 14:26).
