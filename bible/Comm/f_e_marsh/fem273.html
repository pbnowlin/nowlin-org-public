273. MAN FULL OF GRACE

ACTS 6:8, R.V.

Those who are full of grace are—

1. Responsive to grace. "Helped them much, which had believed through grace" (Act 18:27).

2. Built up by grace. "I commend you to God, and to the word of His grace, which is able to build you up" (Act 18:27).

3. Testify of grace. "To testify the Gospel of the grace of God" (Act 20:24).

4. Dependent upon grace. "We believe that through the grace of the Lord Jesus Christ, we shall be saved" (Act 15:11).

5. Holy through grace. "Great grace was upon them all" (Act 4:33).

6. Continue in grace. "Persuaded them to continue in the grace of God" (Act 13:43).

7. Evidenced by grace. "When he had seen the grace of God, was glad" (Act 11:23).
