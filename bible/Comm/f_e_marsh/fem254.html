254. LEARN

1. Learn to fear the Lord. "That they may learn to fear Me" (Deu 4:10).

2. Learn the Word of God. "That I might learn Thy statutes" (Psa 119:71, 73).

3. Learn to do well. "Learn to do well" (Isa 1:17).

4. Learn to maintain good works. "And let ours also learn to maintain good works" (Tts 3:14).

5. Learn to be contented. "For I have learned in whatsoever state I am, therewith to be content" (Phl 4:11).

6. Learn to be obedient. "Yet learned He obedience" (Hbr 5:8).

7. Learn of Christ. "Learn of Me" (Mat 11:29).
