286. OBEDIENCE

1. Obedience of faith. "A great company of the priests were obedient to the faith" (Act 6:7).

2. Obedience of love. "If ye love Me keep My commandments" (Jhn 14:15).

3. Obedience to death. "Became obedient unto death" (Phl 2:8).

4. Obedience to Christ. "Bringing into captivity every thought to the obedience of Christ" (2Cr 10:5).

5. Obedience unto righteousness. "Of obedience unto righteousness" (Rom 6:16).

6. Obedience is remembered. "He remembereth the obedience of you all" (2Cr 7:15).

7. Obedience is spoken of. "Your obedience is come abroad unto all men" (Rom 16:19).
