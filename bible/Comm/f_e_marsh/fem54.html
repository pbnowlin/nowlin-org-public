54. CHRIST ABOVE THE BELIEVER

1. As the Finisher of His work on our behalf. "After He had offered one sacrifice for sins for ever, sat down" (Hbr 10:12).

2. As the Conqueror over His and our enemies. "He might destroy him" (Hbr 2:14).

3. As our High Priest, to represent. "To appear in the presence of God for us" (Hbr 9:24).

4. As our Advocate, to plead. "We have an Advocate" (1Jo 2:1).

5. As the Firstfruits of the harvest. "Christ the Firstfruits" (1Cr 15:23).

6. As the Sun to warm. "The Lord God is a Sun" (Psa 84:11).

7. As the Shield, to protect. "I am thy Shield" (Gen 15:1).
