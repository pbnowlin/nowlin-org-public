279. MIGHTY GOD

ISAIAH 9:6

He is mighty in many ways. His mighty acts declare His power.

1. He is mighty to save (Isa 63:1).

2. He is mighty to deliver (Deu 3:24; Deu 7:8, 19).

3. He is mighty to conquer (Jos 4:24; 2Cr 10:4).

4. He is mighty to strengthen (Gen 49:24).

5. He is mighty to use in His service (Col 1:29).

6. He is mighty in word (Luk 24:19).

7. He is mighty in work (Mar 6:2).
