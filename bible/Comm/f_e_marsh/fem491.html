491. WHAT WE ARE TO DO WITH THE WORD OF GOD

1. Receive the Word with meekness, and thus be saved from all wickedness (Jas 1:21).

2. Let it dwell in us richly, that we may teach others (Col 3:16).

3. Keep it, and thus have His love perfected in us (1Jo 2:5).

4. Continue in it, that it may be manifest that we are His disciples indeed (Jhn 8:31).

5. Hold it fast, that we may be able to convince gainsayers (Tts 1:9).

6. Be doers of it, and not deceive ourselves by thinking that hearing is enough without the doing (Jam 1:22).

7. Speak it out boldly, that the Lord may be honoured (Phl 1:14).

8. Hold it forth faithfully, by living it out truly (Phl 2:16).
