185. HEAVENLY PEOPLE

1 CORINTHIANS 15:48

Israel is God's earthly people. The Church is God's heavenly people. They are—

1. Born with a heavenly birth (Jhn 1:13).

2. Seated in heavenly places (Eph 2:6).

3. Blessed with heavenly blessings (Eph 1:3).

4. Called with a heavenly calling (Hbr 3:1).

5. Kept for a heavenly inheritance (1Pe 1:4).

6. Belonging to a heavenly citizenship (Phl 3:20, R.V.)

7. Represented by a heavenly High Priest (Hbr 9:24).
