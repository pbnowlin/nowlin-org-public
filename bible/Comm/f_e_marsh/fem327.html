327. REST

1. Rest of Salvation. "Stand still, and see the salvation of the Lord" (Exd 14:13).

2. Rest of Conscience about our sins. "I will give you rest" (Mat 11:28).

3. Rest from the fear of Judgment. "There is, therefore, now no condemnation" (Rom 8:1; Jhn 5:24).

4. Rest from fear of death. "Though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil" (Psa 23:4).

5. Rest from the power of sin. "Because we have sought the Lord…He hath given us rest" (2Ch 14:7).

6. Rest of heart in fellowship with Christ. "Take My yoke…ye shall find rest" (Mat 11:29).

7. Rest in Service from doubt and anxiety. "We which have believed do enter into rest" (Hbr 4:3).

8. Rest from the presence of sin in glory. "There remaineth therefore a rest to the people of God" (Hbr 4:9).
