176. GRACE

1. The grace that is in Christ Jesus is a saving grace. "By grace ye are saved" (Eph 2:5).

2. The grace that is in Christ Jesus is a sufficient grace. "My grace is sufficient for thee" (2Cr 12:9).

3. The grace that is in Christ Jesus is a stimulating grace. "By the grace of God I am what I am" (1Cr 15:10).

4. The grace that is in Christ Jesus is a serving grace. "Let us have grace whereby we may serve God acceptably" (Hbr 12:28).

5. The grace that is in Christ Jesus is a stablishing grace. "It is a good thing that the heart be established with grace" (Hbr 13:9).

6. The grace that is in Christ Jesus is a sacrificing grace. "Ye know the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ, that, though He was rich, yet for your sakes He became poor" (2Cr 8:9).

7. The grace that is in Christ Jesus is a supplying grace. "Of His fulness have all we received, and grace for grace" (Jhn 1:16).
