470. "UPON"

The Gospel according to "Upon," that is, as revolving around the preposition "Epi," is full of instruction.

1. The Substitute upon the Tree frees the Believer from sin's consequences. "Bare our sins in His own body on (Epi) the tree" (1Pe 2:24).

2. The Righteousness of God upon the Believer makes him beautiful. "Upon all them that believe" (Rom 3:22).

3. Being built on Christ, the Foundation makes the Believer to partake of His life. "Build upon this foundation" (1Cr 3:12).

4. To cast all care upon the Lord is the Believer's Privilege. "Casting all your care upon Him" (1Pe 5:7).

5. The Spirit of God resting upon the Believer is his Glory and Power. "Spirit of God resteth upon you" (1Pe 4:14).

6. The Power of Christ upon the Believer, is the Strengthener for every trial. "Power of Christ may rest upon me" (2Cr 12:9).

7. The Grace of the Holy Spirit upon the Believer is the Outcome of Whole-hearted Surrender to Him. "Great grace was upon them all" (Act 4:33).
