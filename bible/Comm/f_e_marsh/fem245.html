245. JUSTIFICATION

1. Self-judgment is its fore-runner. "This man went down to his house justified" (Luk 18:14).

2. God is its Author. "God that justifieth" (Rom 8:33).

3. Grace is its Spring. "Justified freely by His grace" (Rom 3:24).

4. Blood of Christ is its Purchaser. "Justified by His blood" (Rom 5:9).

5. The resurrection is its Proclaimer. "Raised again for our justification" (Rom 4:25).

6. Faith is its Accepter. "Being justified by faith" (Rom 5:1).

7. Union is its Confirmation. "He that is dead is freed (margin, justified) from sin" (Rom 6:7).

8. The Holy Spirit is its Effector. "Justified…by the Spirit" (1Cr 6:11).

9. All things are in its Scope. "Justified from all things" (Act 13:39).

10. Works are its Evidence. "By works a man is justified" (Jam 2:24).
