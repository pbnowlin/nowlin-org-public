465. UNFAILING THINGS

Things that have to do with man fail, but the things which have to do with God, are like Himself—unfailing (Zep 3:5).

Things which do not fail—

1. The Lord's Presence. "The Lord thy God, He it is that doth go with thee; He will not fail thee" (Deu 31:6).

2. The Lord's Faithfulness. "Nor suffer My faithfulness to fail" (Psa 89:33).

3. The Lord's Compassions. "His compassions fail not" (Lam 3:22).

4. The Lord's Care. "Neither shall the cruise of oil fail" (1Ki 17:14).

5. The Lord's Law. "It is easier for heaven and earth to pass, than one tittle of the law to fail" (Luk 16:17).

6. The Lord's Christ. "Thy years shall not fail" (Hbr 1:12).

7. The Lord's Promise. "There failed not ought of any good thing which the Lord had spoken" (Jos 21:45).

8. The Lord's Love. "Love never faileth" (1Cr 13:8, R.V.).

9. The Lord's Stars. "Not one faileth" (Isa 40:26).
