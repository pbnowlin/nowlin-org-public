183. HAND OF THE LORD

What does this signify? Omnipotence.

1. His power to carry out His purpose. "To do whatsoever Thy hand and Thy counsel determined before to be done" (Act 4:28).

2. His power to provide. "Thou openest Thine hand and satisfiest the desire of every living thing" (Psa 145:16).

3. His power to preserve. "Neither shall any pluck them out of My hand" (Jhn 10:28-29).

4. His power to protect. "Thy right hand, O Lord, is become glorious in power" (Exd 15:6).

5. The power of His presence. "Behold an hand touched me which set me upon my knees and upon the palms of my hands" (Dan 10:10).

6. His power to perform His promise. "The Lord thy God brought thee out thence through a mighty hand, and by a stretched out arm" (Deu 5:15).

7. His power to punish. "David…saw the angel of the Lord…having a drawn sword in His hand" (1Ch 21:16).
