139. FAITHFULNESS OF GOD

1. He is faithful that called us unto the fellowship of His Son. "God is faithful, by whom ye were called" (1Cr 1:9).

2. He is faithful, and will not suffer us to be tempted above what we are able to bear (1Cr 10:13).

3. The Lord is faithful to sanctify us wholly. "The very God of peace sanctify you wholly" (1Th 5:23).

4. He is faithful to stablish and keep. "The Lord is faithful, who shall stablish you, and keep you from evil" (2Th 3:3).

5. His own character the ground of His faithfulness. "If we believe not, yet He abideth faithful; He cannot deny Himself" (2Ti 2:13).

6. He is faithful to keep to His promises. "He is faithful that promised" (Hbr 10:23).

7. He is faithful to forgive, if we confess our sins. "If we confess our sins, He is faithful and just to forgive" (1Jo 1:9).
