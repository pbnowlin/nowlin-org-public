164. "GO"

There are several commands that are associated with the monosyllable "go" which we may take to illustrate the commands of Christ.

1. Power. "Go in this thy might" (Jdg 6:14).

2. Prayer. "In Thy name we go against this multitude" (2Ch 14:11).

3. Progress. "Go forward" (Exd 14:15; Psa 84:7).

4. Practice. "Go and do thou likewise" (Luk 10:37).

5. Proclamation. "Go tell this people" (Isa 6:9; Act 28:26).

6. Perseverance. "Go out into the highways," &c. (Luk 14:21-23; Mat 22:9).

7. Presence. "My presence shall go with thee" (Exd 33:14).
