262. LOOKS OF CHRIST

1. The look of anger and grief, because of unbelief. "Looked round about on them with anger, being grieved for the hardness of their hearts" (Mar 3:5).

2. Look of recognition and oneness. "Looked round about on them which sat about Him" (Mar 3:34).

3. Look of enquiry and encouragement. "He looked round about to see her that had done this thing" (Mar 5:32).

4. Look of rebuke. "When He had turned about and looked on His disciples, He rebuked Peter" (Mar 8:33).

5. Look of discouragement. "Jesus looked round about, and saith unto His disciples" (Mar 10:23).

6. The look of stimulus. "Jesus, looking upon them, saith,…. with God all things are possible" (Mar 10:27).

7. Look of inspection. "When He had looked round about upon all things" (Mar 11:11).

8. The look of reproach. "The Lord turned, and looked upon Peter" (Luk 22:61).
