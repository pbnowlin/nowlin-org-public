216. "I WILL"

Note the seven "I wills" in Exodus 6:6-8, and see how they correspond to the blessings of the Gospel—

1. The "I will" of salvation. "I will bring you," &c. (Eph 2:5).

2. The "I will" of severance. "I will rid you," &c. (Col 1:13).

3. The "I will" of power. "I will redeem you," &c. (Eph 1:19-23; Eph 2:1-6).

4. The "I will" of separation. "I will take you to me," &c. (Jhn 15:16).

5. The "I will" of relationship. "I will be to you a God" (2Cr 6:17-18).

6. The "I will" of leading. "I will bring you," &c. (Jhn 17:24).

7. The "I will" of heritage. "I will give," &c. (1Pe 1:3-4).
