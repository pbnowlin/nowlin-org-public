214. IN THE SPIRIT

1. As Warriors. Walk in the Spirit. "Walk in the Spirit, and ye shall not fulfil the lust of the flesh" (Gal 5:16).

2. As Redeemed Ones. Continuing in the Spirit. "Having begun in the Spirit," &c. (Gal 3:3).

3. As Suppliants. Praying in the Spirit. "Praying always with all prayer and supplication in the Spirit" (Eph 6:18).

4. As Worshippers. Worship God in the Spirit. "Which worship God in the Spirit" (Phl 3:3).

5. As Brethren. Love in the Spirit. "Declared unto us your love in the Spirit" (Col 1:8).

6. As United Ones. Access in the Spirit. "Have our access in one Spirit" (Eph 2:18, R.V.)

7. As Obedient Ones. Live in the Spirit. "If we live in the Spirit, let us," &c. (Gal 5:25).
