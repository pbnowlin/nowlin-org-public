2. Psalm 121. Divine aid, or kept by the Lord.

3. Psalm 122. Dwelling, or the house of the Lord.

4. Psalm 123. Direction, or guided by the Lord.

5. Psalm 124. Deliverance, or saved by the Lord.

6. Psalm 125. Defence, or shielded in the Lord.

7. Psalm 126. Delight, or rejoicing in the Lord.

8. Psalm 127. Protection, or blest by the Lord.

9. Psalm 128. Promise, or fearing the Lord.

10. Psalm 129. Persecution, or suffering with the Lord.

11. Psalm 130. Prayer, or waiting upon the Lord.

12. Psalm 131. Practical sanctification, or humble before the Lord.

13. Psalm 132. Perseverance, or communion with the Lord.

14. Psalm 133. Power, or anointed by the Lord.

15. Psalm 134. Praise, or glorifying the Lord.
