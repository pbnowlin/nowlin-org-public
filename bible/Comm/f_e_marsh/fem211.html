211. IN THE LORD

1. Unity. Brethren in the Lord. "Many of the brethren in the Lord" (Phl 1:14).

2. Faith. Trust in the Lord. "I trust in the Lord" (Phl 2:19, 24).

3. Fellowship. In the Lord. "Receive him, therefore, in the Lord" (Phl 2:29).

4. Rejoicing. Rejoice in the Lord. "Rejoice in the Lord alway" (Phl 4:4, 10).

5. Stability. Stand fast in the Lord. "Stand fast in the Lord" (Phl 4:1).

6. Oneness. Same mind in the Lord. "That they be of the same mind in the Lord" (Phl 4:2).

7. Witness. Example of rejoicing in the Lord. "I rejoiced in the Lord greatly" (Phl 4:10).
