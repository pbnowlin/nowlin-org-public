73. CHRIST, OUR OBJECT

1. He is the Object of God's Counsels. "I was daily His delight" (Pro 8:30).

2. He is the Object of the Scriptures. "Search the Scriptures…and they are they which testify of Me" (Jhn 5:39).

3. He is the Object of our Faith. "Behold the Lamb of God" (Jhn 1:29, 36).

4. He is the Object of our Worship. "And he worshipped Him" (Jhn 9:38).

5. He is the Object of our Life. "Should not henceforth live unto themselves, but unto Him" (2Cr 5:15).

6. He is the Object of our Imitation. "Christ also suffered for us, leaving us an Example" (1Pe 2:21).

7. He is the Object of our Hope. "Lord Jesus Christ, which is our Hope" (1Ti 1:1).
