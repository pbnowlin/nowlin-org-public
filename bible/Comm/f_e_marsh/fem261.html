261. LOOK BACK

As the Lord reminded Israel what they were (Deu 15:15), and from what He had saved them (Deu 8:14); so He bids us not to forget what we were, and from what He has saved us.

It is well for us to remember…

1. The pit from which we have been rescued (Psa 40:2).

2. The rock from whence we have been hewn (Isa 51:1).

3. The darkness from which we have been brought (Col 1:13).

4. The pollution from which we have been cleansed (1Cr 6:9-11).

5. The wrath from which we are delivered (Jhn 3:36).

6. The death from which we have passed (Jhn 5:24).

7. The desolation from which we have been taken (Eph 2:12).
