11. "ALLS" OF SEPARATION

We have the life of good king Josiah summed up under seven "alls."

1. The "all" of separation. "All the abominations…did Josiah put away" (2Ki 23:24).

2. The "all" of affection. "Turned to the Lord with all his heart" (2Ki 23:25).

3. The "all" of soul or life. "Turned to the Lord with all his soul" (2Ki 23:25).

4. The "all" of strength. "All his might" (2Ki 23:25).

5. The "all" of fidelity. "According to all the law of Moses" (2Ki 23:25).

6. The "all" of perseverance. "All his days they departed not from following the Lord" (2Ch 34:33).

7. The "all" of influence. "The king commanded all the people, saying, Keep the passover unto the Lord" (2Ki 23:21).
