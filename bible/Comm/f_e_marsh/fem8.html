8. ALIVE FOR EVERMORE

1. Alive to quicken. "Living Stone" (1Pe 2:4).

2. Alive to satisfy. "Living Bread" (Jhn 6:51).

3. Alive to refresh. "Living Water" (Jhn 4:10).

4. Alive to keep. Living High Priest (Hbr 7:25).

5. Alive to save. "Saved in His life" (Rom 5:10, R.V., M.).

6. Alive to reproduce. "Christ liveth in me" (Gal 2:20).

7. Alive to glorify. "When Christ, who is our Life" (Col 3:4)
