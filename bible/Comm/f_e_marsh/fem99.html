99. CONVERSION

The following sevenfold contrast will demonstrate what conversion is:—

1. "Dead in sins" (Eph 2:1). "Passed from death" (Jhn 5:24).

2. "Condemned" (Jhn 3:18). "Justified" (Rom 5:1).

3. "Lost" (Luk 19:10). "Saved" (Eph 2:8).

4. "Under sin" (Gal 3:22). "Under grace" (Rom 6:14).

5. "Without Christ" (Eph 2:12). "In Christ" (2Cr 12:2).

6. "Sinners" (1Ti 1:15). "Saints" (1Cr 1:2).

7. "Astray" (Isa 53:6). "Abiding" (Jhn 15:4).
