60. CHRIST BENEATH THE BELIEVER

1. As the Foundation on whom we are built. "For other Foundation can no man lay" (1Cr 3:11).

2. As the Way to walk in. "I am the Way" (Jhn 14:6).

3. As the Shoes of Peace, to protect. "Your feet shod with the preparation of the Gospel of peace" (Eph 6:15).

4. As the Arms to carry. "Underneath are the everlasting arms" (Deu 33:27).

5. As the Shoulders to bear. "Layeth it on His shoulders" (Luk 15:5).

6. As the Bosom to rest in. "Which also leaned on His breast" (Jhn 21:20).

7. As the Staff to support. "Thy rod and Thy staff" (Psa 23:4).
