259. LISTENING TO CHRIST

1. Listening to Him as our Teacher, we shall be instructed by Him (Luk 10:39).

2. Listening to Him as our Captain, we shall have victory through Him (1Ti 6:12).

3. Listening to Him as our Guide, we shall be led safely by Him (Mat 28:19-20).

4. Listening to Him as our Lover, we shall be jealous for Him (Jhn 14:15).

5. Listening to Him as our Lord, we shall render service to Him (Jhn 13:13-15).

6. Listening to Him as our Helper, we shall be upheld by Him (Isa 41:10).

7. Listening to Him as our Shepherd, we shall follow Him (Jhn 10:27).
