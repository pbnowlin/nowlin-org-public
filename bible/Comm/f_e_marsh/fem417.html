417. THE MAN OF FAITH

1. Testimony of Faith. "There shall be no loss of any man's life among you" (Act 27:22).

2. Fellowship of Faith. Presence of the Lord. "There stood by me this night the angel of God" (Act 27:23).

3. Promise of Faith. "Fear not, Paul; thou must be brought before Caesar" (Act 27:24).

4. Confidence of Faith. Power. "I believe God that it shall be" (Act 27:25).

5. Rest of Faith. Peace. "Abide in the ship" (Act 27:31).

6. Cheerfulness of Faith. Praise. "They were all of good cheer" (Act 27:36).

7. Reward of Faith. Preserved. "They escaped all safe to land" (Act 27:44).
