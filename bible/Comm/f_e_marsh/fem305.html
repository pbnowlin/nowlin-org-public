305. PLEASING THE LORD

There are several ways by which we, as believers in Christ, may please the Lord.

1. By not pleasing ourselves (Rom 15:1).

2. By walking worthy of the Lord (Col 1:10).

3. By suffering wrongfully (1Pe 2:19-20).

4. By walking with God (Hbr 11:5).

5. By believing in God (Hbr 11:6).

6. By ministering to the need of others (Hbr 13:16).
