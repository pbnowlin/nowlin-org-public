160. GENESIS 28:15

1. Presence in loneliness. "I am with thee" (Mat 28:20).

2. Preservation in danger. "Will keep thee" (Jhn 17:11-12; 1Pe 1:5).

3. Protection at all times. "In all places" (Jhn 13:1).

4. Promise fulfilled. "Will bring thee again into this land" (Jhn 14:3).

5. Perpetual companionship. "I will not leave thee" (Hbr 13:5).

6. Perseverance in blessing. "Until I have done," &c. (Phl 1:6).
