226. JEHOVAH IN PSALM 27

1. Light, to direct. "The Lord is my Light" (Psa 27:1).

2. Salvation, to deliver. "And my Salvation" (Psa 27:1).

3. Strength, to uphold. "The Lord is the Strength of my life" (Psa 27:1).

4. Object of desire. "One thing have I desired of the Lord" (Psa 27:4).

5. House of shelter. "That I may dwell in the house of the Lord" (Psa 27:4).

6. Beauty, to admire. "The beauty of the Lord" (Psa 27:4).

7. Teacher, to instruct. "To enquire in His temple" (Psa 27:4).

8. Protector, to hide. "He shall hide me in His pavilion" (Psa 27:5).

9. Treasurer, to keep. "In the secret of His tabernacle shall He hide me" (Psa 27:5).

10. Uplifter, to save. "He shall set me up upon a rock" (Psa 27:5).

11. Lord, to worship. "I will sing praises unto the Lord" (Psa 27:6).

12. Hearer of prayer. "Hear, O Lord, when I cry" (Psa 27:7).

13. Director of saints. "Seek ye My face" (Psa 27:8).

14. God of salvation. "O God of my salvation" (Psa 27:9).

15. Gatherer of forsaken. "The Lord will take me up" (Psa 27:10).

16. Leader, to guide. "Lead me in a plain path" (Psa 27:11).

17. Goodness, to bless. "The goodness of the Lord" (Psa 27:13).

18. Encourager, to strengthen. "Be of good courage, and He shall strengthen thine heart" (Psa 27:14).
