257. LIBERTY

1. Freedom from the slavery of sin. "To set at liberty them that are bruised" (Luk 4:18).

2. Freedom from anxiety. "And to let him have liberty" (Act 24:23).

3. Freedom to be. Hence to the right or authority. "To them gave He the right (M.) to become the sons of God" (Jhn 1:12).

4. Freedom of approach. "Boldness (M., liberty), to enter into the holiest" (Hbr 10:19).

5. Freedom to go where one will. "Where the Spirit of the Lord is, there is liberty" (2Cr 3:17; Jam 1:25).
