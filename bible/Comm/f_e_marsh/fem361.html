361. SEVEN SUPPERS

1. Sinners' supper of pleasure. "Herod…made a supper to his lords" (Mar 6:21).

2. Gospel supper. "A certain man made a great supper" (Luk 14:16).

3. Entertaining the Master, the supper of communion. "There they made Him a supper, and Martha served" (Jhn 12:2).

4. Supper of love. "When thou makest a supper" (Luk 14:12).

5. Marriage supper of the Lamb. "Blessed are they which are called unto the marriage supper of the Lamb" (Rev 19:9).

6. Supper of the Great God. "Unto the supper of the Great God" (Rev 19:17).

7. Supper of remembrance. "To eat the Lord's supper" (1Cr 11:20).
