100. CONVICTION OF SIN

The things that come to the heart and produce conviction of sin are a consciousness of—

1. The law broken by us (Gal 3:10).

2. The heart defiled by sin (Mat 15:19, 20).

3. The nature wrong (Jhn 3:6).

4. Failure to come up to God's glory (Rom 3:23).

5. Helpless condition (Rom 5:6).

6. Hopeless state (Eph 2:12).

7. Indebtedness to God (Luk 7:41-42).
