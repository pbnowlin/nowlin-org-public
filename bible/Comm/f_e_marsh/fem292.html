292. OUGHTNESS OF THE CHRISTIAN LIFE

All God's children feel there are many things we ought to do, but we do not always rise to the occasion.

1. The oughtness of Prayer. "Men ought always to pray" (Luk 18:1).

2. The oughtness of Ministry. "Ought to wash one another's feet" (Jhn 13:14).

3. The oughtness of Obedience. "Ought to obey God" (Act 5:29).

4. The oughtness of God-pleasing. " Ought to walk and to please God" (1Th 4:1).

5. The oughtness of Walking. "Ought himself also to walk, even as He walked" (1Jo 2:6).

6. The oughtness of Love. "We ought also to love one another" (1Jo 4:11).

7. The oughtness of Obligation. "We ought to lay down our lives for the brethren" (1Jo 3:16).
