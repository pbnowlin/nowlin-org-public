304. PLACES OF BLESSING

1. On His shoulders. The place of strength. "He layeth it on His shoulders" (Luk 15:5).

2. On His bosom. The place of confidence. "He then lying on Jesus' breast, saith unto Him, Lord, who is it?" (Jhn 13:25).

3. On His heart. The place of affection. "And they shall be upon Aaron's heart" (Exd 28:15-30).

4. On His hands. The place of remembrance. "I have graven thee upon the palms of My hands" (Isa 49:16).

5. On His forehead. The place of thought. "And it shall be upon Aaron's forehead" (Exd 28:38).

6. On His wings. The place of power. "They shall mount up with wings as eagles" (Isa 40:31).

7. In His arms. The place of support. "Underneath are the everlasting arms" (Deu 33:27).

8. At His feet. The place of blessing. "Mary, which also sat at Jesus' feet, and heard His word" (Luk 10:39).

9. In His hand. The place of use. "I the Lord thy God will hold thy right hand saying unto thee, fear not…thou shalt thresh the mountains" (Isa 41:13-15).
