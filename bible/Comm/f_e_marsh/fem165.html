165. GOD'S CARE FOR HIS PEOPLE

1. He watches over them with His eyes (Psa 34:15).

2. He listens to their prayers with His ears (1Pe 3:12).

3. He instructs them by His word (Deu 33:3).

4. He loves them with His heart (1Jo 3:1).

5. He supports them by His hands (Hsa 1:3).

6. He kisses them with His lips (Sgs 1:2).

7. He leads them by His Spirit (Rom 8:14).

8. He encourages them by His promises (2Pe 1:4).
