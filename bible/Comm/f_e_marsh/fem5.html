5. A BRIGHT PROMISE

"I will never leave thee, nor forsake thee" (Hbr 13:5).

The frequency of the promise of God's unfailing presence is very marked in God's Word.

1. The Promiser of this promise―Jehovah (Gen 28:15).

2. The ground of this promise―covenant (Deu 4:31).

3. The power of this promise in conflict (Deu 31:6).

4. The suitability of this promise for special work (Deu 31:8).

5. The certainty of this promise (Jos 1:5).

4. The reason of the fulfilment of this promise (1Sa 12:22).

5. The stimulus of this promise (1Ch 28:20).

6. The condition to be fulfilled to know the truth of this promise (1Ki 6:12-13).

7. The presentness, perfectness, and perpetuity of this promise (Psa 37:28-33).

8. This promise is valid, although saints may not always realize it (Psa 94:14).

9. The class of people that appreciate this promise (Isa 61:17).

10. The immutability of this promise (Hbr 13:5-6).
