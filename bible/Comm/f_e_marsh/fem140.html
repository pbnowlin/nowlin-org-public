140. FEASTS OF JEHOVAH

There are seven feasts mentioned in the twenty-third of Leviticus, all of which are typical, and especially shadow forth, in a brief way, the events from the cross to the glory.

1. The Passover is typical of Christ our Passover sacrificed for us (1Cr 5:7).

2. The Unleavened Bread of the holy life of the believer (1Cr 5:8).

3. The Firstfruits, of the resurrection of Christ (1Cr 15:23).

4. Pentecost, of the coming of the Holy Spirit (Act 2:1-4).

5. The Trumpets, of the coming of Christ (1Th 4:16).

6. The Day of Atonement, of the time when the Jews shall see Christ as their Atonement (Zec 12:10).

7. The Feast of Tabernacles, of the rest and peace of the millennium (Isa 11).
