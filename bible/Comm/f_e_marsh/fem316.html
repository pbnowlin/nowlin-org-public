316. PRESENCE OF THE LORD

The presence of the Lord to His people means:—

1. Light in trouble. "Lord, lift Thou up the light of Thy countenance upon us" (Psa 4:6).

2. Joy in sorrow. "In Thy presence is fulness of joy" (Psa 16:11).

3. Hiding in persecution. "Thou shalt hide them in the secret of Thy presence" (Psa 31:20).

4. Help in discouragement. "I shall yet praise Him for the help of His countenance" (Psa 42:5).

5. Health in disquietude. "Who is the health of my countenance and my God" (Psa 42:11).

6. Encouragement in life. "His countenance doth behold the upright" (Psa 11:7).

7. Gladness in responsibility. "Thou hast made him exceeding glad with Thy countenance" (Psa 21:6).
