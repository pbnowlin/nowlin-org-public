407. THE GOSPEL

1. Divine Gospel. "Separated unto the Gospel of God" (Rom 1:1).

2. Gracious Gospel. "To testify the Gospel of the grace of God" (Act 20:24).

3. Wondrous Gospel. "The beginning of the Gospel of Jesus Christ, the Son of God" (Mar 1:1).

4. Life-giving Gospel. "In Christ Jesus I have begotten you through the Gospel" (1Cr 4:15).

5. Saving Gospel. "The Gospel of your salvation" (Eph 1:13).

6. Peace-securing Gospel. "Your feet shod with the preparation of the Gospel of peace" (Eph 6:15).

7. Full Gospel. "I shall come in the fulness of the blessing of the Gospel of Christ" (Rom 15:29).

8. Powerful Gospel. "I am not ashamed of the Gospel of Christ, for it is the power of God unto salvation" (Rom 1:16).

9. Practical Gospel. "Only let your conversation be as it becometh the Gospel of Christ" (Phl 1:27).

10. Glorious Gospel. "Lest the light of the glorious Gospel of Christ" (2Cr 4:4).

11. Everlasting Gospel. "Having the everlasting Gospel to preach unto them" (Rev 14:6).
