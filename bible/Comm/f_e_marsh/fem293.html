293. "OUR GOD"

1. The God of Truth to sanctify. "A God of truth and without iniquity" (Deu 32:4).

2. God of all Grace to establish. "But the God of all grace…make you perfect, stablish, strengthen, settle you" (1Pe 5:10).

3. The God of Peace to calm. "The God of peace shall be with you" (Phl 4:9).

4. The God of Love to enrich. "Be perfect, be of good comfort, be of one mind, live in peace, and the God of love and peace shall be with you" (2Cr 13:11).

5. The God of all Comfort to console. "The God of all comfort, who comforteth us in all our tribulations" (2Cr 1:3-4).

6. The God of Patience to enable. "Now the God of patience and consolation grant you to be like-minded one toward another, according to Christ Jesus" (Rom 15:5).

7. The God of Hope to cheer. "Now the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace in believing" (Rom 15:13).

8. The God of Glory to attract. "The God of glory appeared unto our father Abraham" (Act 7:2).

9. The God of Judgment to search. "The Lord is a God of judgment" (Isa 30:18).

10. The Father of Mercies to bless. "Blessed be God…the Father of mercies" (2Cr 1:3).
