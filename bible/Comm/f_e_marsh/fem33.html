33. BLAMELESSNESS

Blameless. Faultless we can never be; blameless we must be.

1. Blamelessness of Heart. "To the end that He may stablish your hearts unblameable" (1Th 3:13).

2. Blamelessness of Life. "Unblameably we behaved ourselves" (1Th 2:10).

3. Blamelessness in Testimony. "That ye may be blameless and harmless…in the midst of a crooked and perverse nation" (Phl 2:15).

4. Blamelessness in Service. "Walking in all the commandments and ordinances of the Lord blameless" (Luk 1:6).

5. Blamelessness in the Whole Being. "I pray God your whole spirit and soul and body be preserved blameless" (1Th 5:23).
