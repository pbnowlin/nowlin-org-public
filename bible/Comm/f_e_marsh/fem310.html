310. PRACTICAL POWER OF CHRIST'S
RESURRECTION

The resurrection of Jesus in its practical power is associated with our blessing (Act 3:26).

1. Our conduct towards others (Rom 14:9-10).

2. Our object in life (2Cr 5:15).

3. Our walk in newness of life (Rom 6:4).

4. Our stay in service (2Ti 2:8).

5. Our fruit-bearing (Rom 7:4).

6. Our power for every emergency (Eph 1:19-20).
