3. ABOUNDING

1. Abound in Hope. "That ye may abound in hope" (Rom 15:13).

2. Abound in Love. "That your love may abound" (Phl 1:9).

3. Abound in Faith. "As ye abound in everything, in faith" (2Cr 8:7).

4. Abound in our Walk. "Ye ought to walk…so ye would abound" (1Th 4:1).

5. Abound in Grace. "If these things be in you and abound" (2Pe 1:8).

6. Abound in every Good Work. "In all things may abound to every good work" (2Cr 9:8).

7. Abound in Suffering. "The sufferings of Christ abound in us" (2Cr 1:5).
