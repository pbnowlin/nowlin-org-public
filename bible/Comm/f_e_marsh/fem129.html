129. EXAMPLE IN SERVICE

The Servant—the Son of God. All God's servants are sons, and all God's sons are servants.

1. Wise Servant. "The people were astonished at His doctrine" (Mat 7:28).

2. Obedient Servant. "I delight to do Thy will, O My God" (Psa 40:8).

3. Suffering Servant. "Now is My soul troubled" (Jhn 12:27).

4. Confident Servant. "The word…not Mine, but the Father's which sent Me" (Jhn 14:24; Jhn 16:32).

5. Persevering Servant. "He stedfastly set His face" (Luk 9:51).

6. Accepted Servant. "Whatsoever ye shall ask the Father in My name, He will give it you" (Jhn 16:23).

7. Victorious Servant. "I have glorified Thee on the earth; I have finished the work" (Jhn 17:4).
