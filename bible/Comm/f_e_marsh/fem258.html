258. "LIBERTY"

1. The purpose of Christ. He came "to set at liberty" (Luk 4:18).

2. We are called to liberty. "Ye have been called unto liberty" (Gal 5:13).

3. We have the Spirit of liberty. "Where the Spirit of the Lord is, there is liberty" (2Cr 3:17).

4. We have liberty into the presence of God. "Having therefore, brethren, liberty to enter into the holiest by the blood of Jesus" (Hbr 10:19, mar.)

5. We have the law of liberty, which we are to look into. "Whoso looketh into the perfect law of liberty and continueth therein…this man shall be blessed" (Jam 1:25).

6. We are to stand fast in the liberty. "Stand fast therefore in the liberty wherewith Christ hath made us free" (Gal 5:1).

7. We are waiting for the glorious liberty. "Delivered from the bondage of corruption into the glorious liberty of the children of God" (Rom 8:21).
