12. ALL TIMES

1. Praise. "I will bless the Lord at all times" (Psa 34:1).

2. Trust. "Trust in Him at all times" (Psa 62:8).

3. Righteousness. "Blessed…he that doeth righteousness at all times" (Psa 106:3).

4. Longing. "My soul breaketh for the longing that it hath unto Thy judgments at all times" (Psa 119:20).

5. Friendship. "A friend loveth at all times" (Pro 17:17).

6. Zeal. "Good to be zealously affected at all times" (Gal 4:18 R.V.).

7. Peace. "The Lord of peace Himself give you peace at all times" (2Th 3:16, R.V.)
