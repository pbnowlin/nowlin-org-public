352. SEVEN L'S IN RELATION TO THE HOME IN BETHANY

1. Loved by Christ. "Jesus loved Martha, and her sister, and Lazarus" (Jhn 11:5).

2. Listening to Christ. "Sat at Jesus' feet, and heard His word" (Luk 10:39).

3. Labouring for Christ. "Martha…serving" (Luk 10:40).

4. Longing for Christ. "His sisters sent unto Him" (Jhn 11:3).

5. Light through Christ. "Take ye away the stone" (Jhn 11:39).

6. Life in Christ. "Lazarus, come forth" (Jhn 11:43).

7. Liberty by Christ. "Loose him, and let him go" (Jhn 11:44).
