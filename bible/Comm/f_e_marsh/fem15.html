15. ALPHABET ON WHAT GOD IS TO HIS PEOPLE

A. Almighty God, to bless us. "I am the Almighty God" (Gen 17:1).

B. Blessed God, to cheer us. "Glorious gospel of the blessed God" (1Ti 1:11).

C. Compassionate God, to bear with us. "For the Lord's portion is His people," &c. (Deu 32:9-13).

D. Defending God, to protect us. "The Lord is my Strength," &c. (Exd 15:2-3).

E. Eternal God, to secure us. "The eternal God is thy Refuge" (Deu 33:27).

F. Faithful God, to assure us. "Judged him faithful Who had promised" (Hbr 11:11).

G. Gracious God, to bless us. "Thou art a gracious God, and merciful, slow to anger," &c. (Jon 4:2).

H. Holy God, to sanctify us. "I am God…the Holy One" (Hsa 11:9).

I. Indwelling God, to establish us. "God is in the midst of her; she shall not be moved" (Psa 46:5).

J. Just God, to clear us. "A just God and a Saviour" (Isa 45:21).

K. Kind God, to supply us. "Who crowneth thee with lovingkindness" (Psa 103:4).

L. Loving God, to cherish us. "Yea, I have loved thee" (Jer 31:3).

M. Mighty God, to deliver us. "With His mighty power" (Deu 4:37).

N. Near God, to sustain us. "He is near that justifieth me" (Isa 50:8).

O. Omniscient God, to watch over us. "Behold He that keepeth Israel," &c. (Psa 121:4-7).

P. Powerful God, to strengthen us. "The Lord stood with me and strengthened me" (2Ti 4:17).

Q. Quickening God, to change us. "Hath quickened us together with Christ" (Eph 2:5).

R. Righteous God, to justify us. "Establish the just: for the righteous God trieth the hearts and reins" (Psa 7:9).

S. Saving God, to free us. "This is our God…and He will save us" (Isa 25:9).

T. Truth-keeping God, to encourage us. "Which keepeth truth for ever" (Psa 146:6).

U. Unchanging God, to secure us. "I am the Lord, I change not" (Mal 3:6).

V. Victorious God, to overcome for us. "Thanks be to God, which giveth us the victory" (1Cr 15:57).

W. Wise God, to enlighten us. "If any lack wisdom, let him ask of God," &c. (Jam 1:5).

Y. Yearning God, to look after us. "Yet will I not forget thee. Behold I have graven thee upon the palms of My hands" (Isa 49:15-16).

Z. Zealous God, to keep us. "According to His Divine power hath given unto us all things," &c. (2Pe 1:3).
