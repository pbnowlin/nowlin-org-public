36. BLOOD OF CHRIST

Fourteen distinct and direct blessings that come to the believer through the Blood.

1. Remission. "For this is My blood…shed for many, for the remission of sins" (Mat 26:28).

2. Redemption. "In Whom we have redemption through His blood" (Eph 1:7; Col 1:14; 1Pe 1:19).

3. Reconciliation. "Peace through the blood of His cross, by Him to reconcile all things unto Himself" (Col 1:20).

4. Justification. "Much more then, being now justified by His blood" (Rom 5:9).

5. Removal. "How much more shall the blood of Christ…purge your conscience from dead works" (Hbr 9:14).

6. Meetness. "Made white in the blood of the Lamb" (Rev 7:14).

7. Access. "Boldness to enter into the holiest by the blood of Jesus" (Hbr 10:19).

8. Nearness. "Made nigh by the blood of Christ" (Eph 2:13).

9. Liberty. "Loosed us from our sins by His blood" (Rev 1:5, R.V.).

10. Sanctification. "That He might sanctify the people with His own blood" (Hbr 13:12).

11. Cleansing. "The blood of Jesus Christ His Son cleanseth us from all sin" (1Jo 1:7).

12. Claimed. Purchased. "Which He hath purchased with His own blood" (Act 20:28).

13. Communion. "The communion of the blood of Christ" (1Cr 10:16).

14. Victory. "Overcame him by the blood of the Lamb" (Rev 12:11).
