314. PRESENCE OF CHRIST

1. The presence of Christ is protection, as the three Hebrew young men found (Dan 3:25, 27).

2. The presence of Christ is preservation, as Peter experienced (Mat 14:30-31).

3. The presence of Christ is power, as Paul knew (2Cr 12:9).

4. The presence of Christ is peace, as the disciples were made to know (Jhn 20:21, 26).

5. The presence of Christ is progress, as we see from John 10:27, for the sheep follow Him.

6. The presence of Christ is paradise, as John found in the Isle of Patmos (Rev 1:13-18).

7. The presence of Christ is provision, as we see in His feeding the multitude (Jhn 6:11).
