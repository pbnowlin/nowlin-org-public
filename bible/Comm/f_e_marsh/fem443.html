443. THINGS WHICH WE OBTAIN BY FAITH

1. Salvation. "By grace are ye saved through faith" (Eph 2:8).

2. Justification. "Being justified by faith, we have peace with God" (Rom 5:1).

3. Introduction. "By Whom also we have access by faith" (Rom 5:2).

4. Adoption. "For ye are all the children of God by faith in Christ Jesus" (Gal 3:26).

5. Sanctification. "Sanctified by faith that is in Me" (Act 26:18).

6. Purification. "Purifying their hearts by faith" (Act 15:9).

7. Occupation. "That Christ may dwell in your hearts by faith" (Eph 3:17).

8. Progression. "We walk by faith, not by sight" (2Cr 5:7).

9. Preservation. "Kept by the power of God through faith" (1Pe 1:5).

10. Expectation. "We, through the Spirit, wait for the Hope of Righteousness by faith" (Gal 5:5).
