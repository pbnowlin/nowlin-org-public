66. "CHRIST JESUS" IN PHILIPPIANS

1. "Saints in Christ Jesus" (Phl 1:1). Our standing.

2. "Let this mind be in you which was also in Christ Jesus" (Phl 2:5). Our example.

3. "Rejoice in Christ Jesus" (Phl 3:3). Our rejoicing.

4. "Apprehended of Christ Jesus" (Phl 3:12). Our aim.

5. "High calling of God in Christ Jesus" (Phl 3:14). Our goal.

6. "Peace of God keep your hearts and minds through Christ Jesus" (Phl 4:7). Our rest.

7. Need supplied according to God's "riches in glory by Christ Jesus" (Phl 4:19). Our supply.
