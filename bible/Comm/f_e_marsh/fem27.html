27. BELIEVERS' ATTITUDES & RELATIONS
TO CHRIST

1. Walking with Him, as the two disciples to Emmaus (Luk 14:15), is revelation.

2. Talking with Him, as Moses and Elias did on the mount of transfiguration (Luk 9:30), is fellowship.

3. Listening to Him, as Mary of Bethany (Luk 10:39), is instruction.

4. Abiding with Him, as the two disciples mentioned in Jhn 1:39, is joy.

5. Living with Him, as the disciples of John, is oneness (Jhn 1:39).

6. Waiting upon Him, as the early Christians, is power (Act 4:31).

7. Watching for Him, as the people were waiting for Christ, is expectancy (Luk 8:40).
