353. SEVEN LOOKS

1. Outward look of salvation. "Look unto Me, and be ye saved" (Isa 45:22).

2. Outward look in service. "Look on the fields" (Jhn 4:35).

3. Upward look of faith. "Will I direct my prayer unto Thee, and will look up" (Psa 5:3).

4. Inward look of enquiry. "Whoso looketh into the perfect law of liberty" (Jam 1:25).

5. Inward look of examination. "Look to yourselves" (2Jo 1:8).

6. Onward look of perseverance. "Let thine eyes look right on" (Pro 4:25). "Looking unto Jesus" (Hbr 12:2).

7. Onward look of expectancy. "We look for the Saviour" (Phl 3:20).
