97. CONFESSION, THE FORERUNNER
OF BLESSING

Confession is the forerunner of blessing.

1. Abraham said, "I am dust and ashes" (Gen 18:21), and got all he asked for.

2. Isaiah, "I am unclean," and got cleansed (Isa 6:5).

3. Job, "I am vile," and got blessing (Job 40:4).

4. David, Born in sin, and got covered (Psa 51:5; Psa 32:1).

5. Peter, "I am a sinful man," and received the keys of the kingdom (Luk 5:8; Mat 16:19).

6. Publican, "the sinner," and went home justified (Luk 18:13, R.V., M.).

7. The prodigal, "I am not worthy," and was feasted (Luk 15:21).
