423. THE OBSERVANT CHRIST

"HE SAW"

No needy object escaped the beneficent look of Christ. "He saw" are notes which Holy Spirit plays upon continually, in drawing our attention to the swiftness of Christ's gaze in noting those who were needing His help and His readiness to aid them.

1. Blessing. He saw the faith of others, and blessed for their faith's sake (Luk 5:20).

2. Calling. He saw one who was willing to serve Him, and called him to follow after Him (Luk 5:27).

3. Comforting. He saw a sorrowing one, and comforted her by raising her son from the dead (Luk 7:13).

4. Delivering. He saw one bound by Satan, and delivered her (Luk 13:13).

5. Cleansing. He saw those who were diseased, and cleansed them (Luk 17:14).

6. Saving. He saw a seeking one, and saved him (Luk 19:5).

7. Rescuing. He saw those who were distressed, and rescued them (Mar 6:48).

8. Revealing. He saw the blind man, and revealed Himself to him (Jhn 9:1).
