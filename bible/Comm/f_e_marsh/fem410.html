410. THE HAPPY MAN IN PSALM 32

The blessed man. "Blessed is he" (Psa 32:1).

The forgiven trangressor. "Whose transgression is forgiven" (Psa 32:1).

The covered sinner. "Whose sin is covered" (Psa 32:1).

The absolved perverter. "Unto whom the Lord imputeth not iniquity" (Psa 32:2).

The guileless spirit. "In whose spirit there is no guile" (Psa 32:2).

The silent convicted one. "When I kept silence" (Psa 32:3).

The burdened soul. "Thy hand was heavy upon me" (Psa 32:4).

The sapless tree. "My moisture is turned into the drought of summer" (Psa 32:4).

The open confessor. "I acknowledged my sin unto Thee" (Psa 32:5).

The glad witness. "Thou forgavest the iniquity of my sin" (Psa 32:5).

The godly suppliant. "For this, shall everyone that is godly, pray unto Thee" (Psa 32:6).

The safe refugee. "The floods of great waters shall not come nigh unto him" (Psa 32:6).

The hidden saint. "Thou art my Hiding-place" (Psa 32:7).

The preserved believer. "Thou shalt preserve me from trouble" (Psa 32:7).

The environed singer. "Thou shalt compass me about with songs of deliverance" (Psa 32:7).

The instructed disciple. "I will instruct thee" (Psa 32:8).

The watched child. "Mine eye upon thee" (Psa 32:8, R.V.).

The trustful recliner. "He that trusteth in the Lord" (Psa 32:10).

The righteous rejoicing. "Rejoice, ye righteous" (Psa 32:11).
