76. CHRIST'S PERFECTION

CHRIST was perfect in all He was, in all He said, in all He did, and in all His life.

1. There was no rift in the lute of His character, for He did always the things which were pleasing to His Father (Mat 3:17).

2. There was no flaw in the devotion of His life, for He knew no sin (2Cr 5:21).

3. There was no fault in the consecration of His service, for He finished the work given Him to do (Jhn 17:4).

4. There was no stain in the texture of His nature, for He was without sin (1Jo 3:5).

5. There was no alloy in the gold of His holiness, for He was the Holy One of God (Mar 1:24).

6. There was no spot in the sun of His testimony, for He did no sin (1Pe 2:22).

7. There was no want in the obedience of His love, for He was faithful to death (Phl 2:8).
