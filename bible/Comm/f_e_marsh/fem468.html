468. UNSURPASSED GLORY OF THE LORD

1. His Ways are Untrackable. "His ways past finding out" (Rom 11:33).

2. His Riches are Unsearchable. "The unsearchable riches of Christ" (Eph 3:8).

3. His Love is Unknowable. "The love of Christ which passeth knowledge" (Eph 3:19).

4. His Joy is Unspeakable. "Joy unspeakable, and full of glory" (1Pe 1:8).

5. His Goodness is Unsurpassable. "Behold therefore the goodness…of God" (Rom 11:22).

6. His Peace is not Understandable. "Peace of God, which passeth all understanding" (Phl 4:7).

7. He Himself is Indescribable. "He is altogether lovely" (Sgs 5:16).
