475. WEAK ONES

1. The weak ones whom Jehovah uses are tested ones (Jdg 7:2-7)

2. The weak ones whom the Lord uses are not anxious about their own comfort (Jdg 7:6).

3. The weak ones whom God uses are obedient (Jdg 7:17-20).

4. The weak ones whom God uses are dependent ones (Jdg 7:18, 20).

5. The weak ones whom God uses are united (Jdg 7:22).

6. The weak ones whom the Lord uses are victorious ones (Jdg 7:21).

7. The weak ones are used as reflectors of God's glory (Jdg 7:2).

8. The used of God are sure to have their action called in question by their brethren (Jdg 8:1).

9. The instruments of God are persevering, although faint (Jdg 9:4).

10. The instruments of the Lord are faithful (Jdg 7:8).

11. The instruments of the Lord may fall into sin unless careful (Jdg 8:24, 27).

12. The weak ones are a source of blessing to others (Jdg 8:28).
