365. SEVEN THINGS THE LORD DOES FOR THE
BELIEVER IN PSALM 23

1. A sheep fed. "I shall not want" (Psa 23:1).

2. A child led. "He leadeth me beside the still waters" (Psa 23:2).

3. A backslider restored. "He restoreth my soul" (Psa 23:3).

4. A friend comforted. "Thy rod and Thy staff they comfort me" (Psa 23:4).

5. A warrior feasted. "Thou preparest a table before me" (Psa 23:5).

6. A priest anointed. "Thou anointest my head" (Psa 23:5).

7. A pilgrim housed. "I will dwell in the house of the Lord for ever" (Psa 23:6).
