246. KEEP

1. Keep the sayings of Christ. "If a man keep My sayings, he shall never see death" (Jhn 8:51-52).

2. Keep the commandments of Christ. "If ye love Me, keep My commandments" (Jhn 14:15).

3. Keep the unity of the Spirit. "Endeavouring to keep the unity of the Spirit" (Eph 4:3).

4. Keep unspotted from the world. "And to keep himself unspotted from the world" (Jam 1:27).

5. Keep in the love of God. "Keep yourselves in the love of God" (Jud 1:21).

6. Keep the Faith. "I have kept the faith" (2Ti 4:7).

7. Keep pure. "Keep thyself pure" (1Ti 5:22).
