295. PATHS

1. Path of life. "Thou wilt show me the path of life" (Psa 16:11).

2. Plain path. "Teach me Thy way, O Lord, and lead me in a plain path" (Psa 27:11).

3. Path of uprightness. "Who leave the paths of uprightness to walk in the ways of darkness" (Pro 2:13).

4. Path of the just. "The path of the just is as the shining light" (Pro 4:18).

5. Paths of righteousness. "He leadeth me in the paths of righteousness for His Name's sake" (Psa 23:3).

6. Paths of peace. "Her ways are ways of pleasantness, and all her paths are peace" (Pro 3:17).

7. Straight paths. "And make straight paths for your feet, lest that which is lame be turned out of the way" (Hbr 12:13).
