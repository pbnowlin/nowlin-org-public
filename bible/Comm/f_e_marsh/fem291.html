291. OPEN THINGS

The open tomb of Jesus (Mar 16:4) proclaims—

1. An opened Fountain for sin (Zec 13:1).

2. An opened Heaven for blessing (Mar 1:10).

3. An opened heart for Christ (Act 16:14).

4. An opened understanding for Christ (Luk 24:45).

5. An opened Bible for comfort (Luk 24:32).

6. An opened mouth for testimony (Eph 6:19).

7. An open face for privilege (2Cr 3:18).

8. An open door for revelation (Rev 3:8).
