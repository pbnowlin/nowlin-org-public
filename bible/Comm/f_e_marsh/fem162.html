162. GLORIOUS PROTECTION

We may take the meaning of the names of the Cities of Refuge as illustrating the protection that Christ gives, and the kind of refuge Christ is (Num 35; Jos 20; Jos 21).

1. "Kedesh." Kedesh means holy or separated. Christ is a Holy Refuge, and He is made such to His people (1Cr 1:30).

2. "Shechem." Shechem means shoulder, or strength. Christ is a Strong Refuge. No shot of the enemy can pierce Him, He is impregnable (Psa 18:2).

3. "Hebron." Hebron means company, or fellowship. Christ is a Living Refuge, with whom we have been called to have fellowship (1Cr 1:9).

4. "Bezer." Bezer means strong, or a stronghold. Christ is a Secure Refuge. Nothing can separate us from His love (Rom 8:39), no one can condemn us (Rom 8:34), and no one can take us from our hiding place (Col 3:3).

5. "Ramoth." Ramoth means high, or high places. Christ is a High Refuge. We are made to sit with Him in heavenly places (Eph 2:6).

6. "Golan." Golan means a circle, or enclosed. Christ is a Perfect Refuge. The Lord has hedged His people in, on every side (Job 1:10).
