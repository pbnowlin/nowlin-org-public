473. WALK OF THE BELIEVER

There is a seven-fold walk of the child of God:—

1. Wisely. In the house (Psa 101:2).

2. Lovingly. In the Church (Eph 5:1-2).

3. Circumspectly. In the world (Eph 5:15).

4. Thoroughly. In the truth (2Jo 1:4).

5. Continually. In the light (1Jo 1:7).

6. Dependently. In the Spirit (Gal 5:16, 25).

7. Submissively. In the Lord (Col 2:6).
