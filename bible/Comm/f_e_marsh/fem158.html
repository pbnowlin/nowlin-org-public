158. GATHERED TOGETHER

The "Gathered togethers" of Scripture are associated with the fellowship of God's saints.

1. Gathered together in sorrow (Luk 24:33).

2. Gathered together for worship (Mat 18:20).

3. Gathered together for discipline (1Cr 5:4).

4. Gathered together for testimony (Act 14:27).

5. Gathered together in service (Ezr 3:1; Neh 8:1).

6. Gathered together for prayer (2Ch 20:4; Act 12:12).

7. Gathered together in glory (Psa 50:5; 2Th 2:1).
