148. FORGET NOT

1. Person. "Forget not the Lord" (Deu 8:11).

2. Provocation. Forget not past failure. "Forget not how thou provokedst the Lord thy God" (Deu 9:7).

3. Prayer. Forget not the poor. "Forget not the congregation of Thy poor for ever (Psa 74:19).

4. Punishment. Forget not thine enemies. "Forget not the voice of Thine enemies" (Psa 74:23).

5. Praise. Forget not benefits. "Forget not all His benefits" (Psa 103:2).

6. Practice. Forget not commandments. "Forget not My law, but let thine heart keep My commandments" (Pro 3:1).

7. Pleasing God. Forget not to do good. "But to do good and to communicate forget not" (Hbr 13:16).
