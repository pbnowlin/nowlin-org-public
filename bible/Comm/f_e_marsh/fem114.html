114. DEITY OF CHRIST

1. GOD the Father affirmed it. "This is My beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased" (Mat 3:17).

2. Christ proclaimed it. "Tell us whether Thou be the Christ, the Son of God." "Jesus saith unto him, Thou hast said" (Mat 26:63-64; Mar 14:61-62).

3. The angel asserted it to Mary. "That holy thing, which shall be born of thee, shall be called the Son of God" (Luk 1:35).

4. John the Baptist announced it. "And I saw, and bare record that this is the Son of God" (Jhn 1:34).

5. A legion of demons knew it. "What have we to do with Thee, Jesus, Thou Son of God?" (Mat 8:29).

6. The disciples were sure of it. "Of a truth Thou art the Son of God" (Mat 14:33).

7. The Roman centurion avowed it. "Truly this was the Son of God" (Mat 37:54).

8. The blind man by his action owned it. "He said, Lord, I believe" (Jhn 9:35-38).

9. Unclean spirits acknowledged it. "Thou art the Son of God" (Mar 3:11).

10. The Eunuch believed it. "I believe that Jesus Christ is the Son of God" (Act 8:37).

11. Paul preached it. "He preached Christ in the synagogues, that He is the Son of God" (Act 9:20).

12. Peter confessed it. "Simon Peter answered and said, Thou art the Christ, the Son of the living God" (Mat 16:16).

13. The Evangelists declared it. "The beginning of the Gospel of Jesus Christ, the Son of God" (Mar 1:1).

14. Nathaniel professed it. "Nathaniel answered and said unto Him, Rabbi, Thou art the Son of God" (Jhn 1:49).

15. The apostle wrote of it. "These are written, that ye might believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God" (Jhn 20:31).

16. The Holy Spirit confirmed it. "Declared to be the Son of God with power, according to the Spirit of Holiness" (Rom 1:4).
