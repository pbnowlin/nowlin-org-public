469. UNTO HIM

1. Gathering. Unto Him let us gather. "Unto Him shall the gathering of the people be" (Gen 49:10).

2. Listening. Unto Him let us listen. "Then drew near unto Him all the publicans and sinners for to hear Him" (Luk 15:1).

3. Coming. Unto Him let us bring everything. "Mary was come…unto Him" (Jhn 11:32).

4. Suffering. Unto Him let us go outside the camp bearing His reproach. "Let us go forth therefore unto Him without the camp, bearing His reproach" (Hbr 13:13).

5. Living. Unto Him let us live. "Should not henceforth live unto ourselves, but unto Him" (2Cr 5:15).

6. Looking. Unto Him that is able to do, let us look. "Unto Him that is able to do exceeding abundantly" (Eph 3:20).

7. Glory unto Him we shall soon be gathered in the glory.

"By our gathering together unto Him" (2Th 2:1).
   And our song then, as now, shall be, "Unto Him be glory" (Eph 3:21).
