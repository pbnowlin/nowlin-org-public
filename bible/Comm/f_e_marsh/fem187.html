187. HIDDEN

"HID" (HEBREWS 11:23).

1. Under the blood we are hidden in safety from the judgment of God's wrath against sin, as Israel was in Egypt (Exd 12:13).

2. In the Lord's hand we are hidden from all danger (Jhn 10:28-29).

3. In His tabernacle we are hidden in secrecy for communion with Him (Psa 37:5; Psa 91:1).

4. In His presence we are hidden for protection from our enemies (Psa 31:20).

5. Under His wings we are hidden for comfort (Psa 61:4)

6. In His Word we hide for victory and assurance (Psa 119:114).

7. In His quiver we hide to be used (Isa 49:2).

The hiding of Moses was an act of faith, as we are told in Hbr 11:23. The parents of Moses had confidence in God, and therefore hid their child in opposition to the king's commandment. If we would have the hiding that the Lord is able to give, we must put implicit confidence in Him at all times.
