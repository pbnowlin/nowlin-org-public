323. PUT ON

1. Put Him on as Jesus, to save from and to the uttermost. "Jesus…able also to save them to the uttermost" (Hbr 7:22-25).

2. Put Him on as Lord, to rule and regulate the actions. "Put ye on the Lord Jesus Christ" (Rom 13:14).

3. Put Him on as Christ, to transform the life like His. "As many of you as have been baptised into Christ, have put on Christ" (Gal 3:27).

4. Put Him on as Holiness, to manifest Himself. "Put on the new man…created in righteousness and true holiness" (Eph 4:24; Col 3:10).

5. Put Him on as Armour, to protect in all offensive and defensive warfare. "Put on the whole armour of God" (Eph 6:11).

6. Put Him on as Power, to act through in service. "Until ye be endued" (same word as "put on") "with power from on high" (Luk 24:49).

7. Put Him on as Light, that He may be seen. "Let us put on the armour of Light" (Rom 13:12).
