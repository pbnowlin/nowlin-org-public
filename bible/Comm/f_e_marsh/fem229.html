229. JESUS

There is no Gospel which brings out, in such a striking manner the humanity of Christ, as the one which presents in such a remarkable way the Deity of Jesus, namely, the Gospel according to John; and chapter 11 is an illustration of this fact. This may be gathered if the name "Jesus" is pondered over. It occurs no less than twenty-four times.

1. The Attention of Jesus (Jhn 11:4).

2. The Love of Jesus (Jhn 11:5).

3. The Response of Jesus (Jhn 11:9).

4. The Word of Jesus (Jhn 11:13).

5. The Plainness of Jesus (Jhn 11:14).

6. The Coming of Jesus (Jhn 11:17).

7. The Approach of Jesus (Jhn 11:20).

8. The Lordship of Jesus (Jhn 11:21).

9. The Encouragement of Jesus (Jhn 11:23).

10. The Unfolding of Jesus (Jhn 11:25).

11. The Tarrying of Jesus (Jhn 11:30).

12. The Person of Jesus (Jhn 11:32).

13. The Eyes of Jesus (Jhn 11:33).

14. The Sympathy of Jesus (Jhn 11:35).

15. The Groaning of Jesus (Jhn 11:38).

16. The Command of Jesus (Jhn 11:39).

17. The Reminder of Jesus (Jhn 11:40).

18. The Faith of Jesus (Jhn 11:41).

19. The Direction of Jesus (Jhn 11:44).

20. The Work of Jesus (Jhn 11:45).

21. The Testimony about Jesus (Jhn 11:46).

22. The Atonement of Jesus (Jhn 11:51).

23. The Retirement of Jesus (Jhn 11:54).

24. The Hunting of Jesus (Jhn 11:56).
