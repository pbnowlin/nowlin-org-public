212. IN THE LORD'S HANDS

1. The place of Blessing. "All His saints are in Thy hand…everyone shall receive of Thy words" (Deu 33:3).

2. The place of Chastisement. "Let us fall now into the hand of the Lord" (2Sa 24:14).

3. The place of Creation. "Thine hands have made me, and fashioned me together round about" (Job 10:8).

4. The place of Life. "In whose hand is the soul of every living thing and the breath of all mankind" (Job 12:10).

5. The place of Keeping. "My times are in Thy hand" (Psa 31:15).

6. The place of Supply. "Thou openest Thine hand, they are filled with good" (Psa 104:28).

7. The place of Disposal. "The King's heart is in the hand of the Lord…He turneth it whithersoever He will" (Pro 21:1).

8. The place of Hiding and Usefulness. "In the shadow of His hand hath He hid me, and made me a polished shaft" (Isa 49:2).

9. The place of Security. "Neither shall any pluck them out of My hand" (Jhn 10:28-29).
