85. CHRIST, THE TRUE ONE

1. He is the True Light, to illuminate (Jhn 1:9).

2. He is the True Bread, to satisfy (Jhn 6:32).

3. He is the True Vine, to fructify (Jhn 15:1).

4. He is the True God, to assure (1Jo 5:20).

5. He is the True Witness, to speak (Rev 3:14).

6. He is the True Judge, to determine (Rev 19:11).
