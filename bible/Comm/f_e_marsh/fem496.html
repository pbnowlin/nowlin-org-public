496. WISDOM

The following points illustrate how we may fulfil the injunction, "Be wise":

1. Building. Building on the rock in obedience to the truth (Mat 7:24).

2. Receiving. Receiving the truth of the Scriptures, and thus being wise unto salvation (2Ti 3:15).

3. Possessing. Having the oil of the grace of the Holy Spirit (Mat 25:2-5).

4. Living. Living a good life in meekness (Jas 3:13, R.V.).

5. Walking. Walking carefully in the world (Eph 5:15, R.V.).

6. Dwelling. Being in the environment of Christ's presence, and not being influenced by men (1Co 4:10).

7. Seeking. Ever seeking the good, and thus avoiding the evil (Rom 16:19).
