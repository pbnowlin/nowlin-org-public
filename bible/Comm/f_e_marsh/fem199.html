199. "I KNOWS" OF PAUL

1. "I know that in me, that is, in my flesh, dwelleth no good thing" (Rom 7:18).

2. "I know nothing by (against) myself" (1Cr 4:4).

3. "I know in part; but then shall I know even as also I am known" (1Cr 13:12).

4. "I know" of all things working for good (Phl 1:19).

5. "I know that I shall continue with you all" (Phl 1:25).

6. "I know how to be abased, and I know how to abound" (Phl 4:12).

7. "I know whom I have believed" (2Ti 1:12).
