363. SEVEN THATS

1. The that of encouragement. "I desire that ye faint not" (Eph 3:13).

2. The that of strengthening. "That He would grant you…to be strengthened with might" (Eph 3:16).

3. The that of indwelling. "That Christ may dwell in your hearts" (Eph 3:17).

4. The that of stability. "That ye, being rooted and grounded in love" (Eph 3:17).

5. The that of fulness. "That ye might be filled with all the fulness of God" (Eph 3:19).

6. The that of ability. "Now unto Him that is able to do" (Eph 3:20).

7. The that of almightiness. "Exceeding abundantly above all that we ask or think" (Eph 3:20).
