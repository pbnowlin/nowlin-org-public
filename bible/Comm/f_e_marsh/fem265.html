265. LOVE

Love is a debt we owe to all, and a payment ever to be rendered. Love is a duty; the highest form of duty (Rom 13:8-10). He that loves not, believes not; and he that believes not, loves not.

1. Love is the Divine mark that we are born again (1Jo 4:16-17).

2. Love is the unanswerable testimony of allegiance to Christ (Jhn 14:15).

3. Love is the legible seal that we know the love of God (1Jo 4:17).

4. Love is the unmistakable evidence that we are taught of God (1Jo 4:9).

5. Love is the beautiful livery of heaven (Col 3:14).

6. Love is the royal badge of discipleship (Jhn 13:35).

7. Love is the plain assurance of having passed from death unto life (1Jo 3:14).
