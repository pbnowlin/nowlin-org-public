276. MANNER OF GOD'S LOVE

1 JOHN 3:1

The manner of His love—

1. He loves as a Father (1Jo 3:1). Heartily.

2. He loves as a Mother (Gen 25:28). Tenderly.

3. He loves as a Bridegroom (Gen 24:67). Earnestly.

4. He loves as a Brother (Jhn 15:9). Affectionately.

5. He loves as a Shepherd (Luk 15:4-6). Perseveringly.

6. He loves as a Husband (Eph 5:25). Devotedly.

7. He loves as a Sister (Jhn 11:32). Sincerely.

8. He loves as a Friend (Pro 17:17). Constantly.
