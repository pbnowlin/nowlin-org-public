208. "IN NO WISE"

1. The "in no wise" of fulfilment. "In no wise pass from the law, till all be fulfilled" (Mat 5:18).

2. The "in no wise" of sinnership. "No, in no wise…all under sin" (Rom 3:9).

3. The "in no wise" of helplessness. "Could in no wise lift up herself" (Luk 13:11).

4. The "in no wise" of the Work of Christ. "A work which ye shall in no wise believe" (Act 13:41).

5. The "in no wise" of assurance. "I will in no wise cast out" (Jhn 6:37).

6. The "in no wise" of reward. "He shall in no wise lose his reward" (Mat 10:42).

7. The "in no wise" of judgment. "Shall in no wise enter therein" (Luk 18:17; Rev 21:27).
