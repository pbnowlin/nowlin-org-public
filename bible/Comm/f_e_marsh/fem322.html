322. PROVISION OF THE GOSPEL

When we call to mind the ample provision God has provided in the death and person of His Son, we discover an ocean of grace. The following alphabet will illustrate in some small degree the fulness of the Gospel. In the Gospel we find—

A…Acceptance for the unworthy (Eph 1:6).

B…Blessing for the cursed (Gal 3:13).

C…Cleansing for the polluted (1Jo 1:7).

D…Deliverance for the captive (2Cr 1:10).

E…Enrichment for the poor (2Cr 8:9).

F…Fulness of pardon for the guilty (Luk 7:42).

G…Grace for the prodigal (Luk 15:22).

H…Heaven for the hell-deserving (Col 1:13).

I…Inheritance for the beggar (1Pe 1:3-5).

J…Joy for the miserable (Luk 2:10).

K…Kiss of welcome for the wanderer (Luk 15:20).

L…Life for the dead (Jhn 5:25).

M…Might for the feeble (Rom 5:6).

N…Nearness for those who are afar off (Eph 2:13).

O…Open door for the undeserving (Jhn 10:9).

P…Peace for the enemy (Col 1:20).

Q…Quietness for the troubled (Luk 7:50).

R…Righteousness for the unrighteous (2Cr 5:21).

S…Salvation for the lost (Eph 2:5).

T…Truth for the untrue (Jhn 14:6).

U…Union for the severed (Rom 11:17).

V…Virtue for the depraved (1Cr 6:11).

W…Welcome for all (Rev 22:17).
