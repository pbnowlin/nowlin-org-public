248. KEEPING CHRIST'S WORDS

JOHN 14:15, 21, 23

To keep Christ's words is to evidence our love to Him. We should keep His words and commands and observe His directions—

1. Faithfully, as Adam was to keep the Garden of Eden (Gen 2:15).

2. Constantly, as the eyelid does the eye (Psa 17:8).

3. Wholly, as the Levites kept the charge of the tabernacle (Num 3:7; Num 31:30, 47).

4. Diligently, as Jonathan did in looking out for David's enemies (1Sa 19:2).

5. Vigilantly, as Saul's messengers watched for David (1Sa 19:11).

6. Attentively, as we mark the example of a godly man and follow it (Psa 37:37).

7. Carefully, as God did Israel in bringing them to Canaan (Jos 24:17).
