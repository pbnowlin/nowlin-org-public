90. CIRCLE OF BLESSING

Satan said that the Lord had put a hedge about Job on every side (Job 1:10), so that he could not touch him. The Lord has put a hedge of blessing around the believer, so that he can say—

1. Isa 44:22, is the Pledge of my forgiveness.

2. Gal 2:20, is the Confession of my faith.

3. Jhn 5:24, is the Assurance of my salvation.

4. Isa 41:10, is the Comfort of my life.

5. Psa 55:22, is the Joy of my unceasing privilege.

6. Luk 19:13, is the Burden of my present responsibility.

7. Phl 3:20-21, is the Outlook of my constant expectation.
