236. JESUS

The Name of Jesus in the Epistle to the Hebrews.

1. As Son of Man. "But we see Jesus, who was made a little lower than the angels for the suffering of death" (Hbr 2:9).

2. As our High Priest. "We have a great High Priest, who is passed into the heavens, Jesus, the Son of God" (Hbr 4:14, and Hbr 6:20).

3. As our Surety. "By so much was Jesus made a Surety of a better testament" (Hbr 7:22).

4. As the Way into the holiest. "Having therefore, brethren, boldness to enter into the holiest by the blood of Jesus" (Hbr 10:19).

5. As the Author of faith. "Looking unto Jesus, the Author and Finisher of our faith" (Hbr 12:2).

6. As our Mediator. "And to Jesus the Mediator of the new covenant" (Hbr 12:24).

7. As our Sanctifier. "Wherefore Jesus also, that He might sanctify the people with His own blood, suffered without the gate" (Hbr 13:12).
