366. SEVEN THINGS THE WORD OF GOD DOES
IN PSALM 19

1. Restores the soul. "Converting the soul" (Psa 19:7).

2. Instructs the mind. "Making wise the simple" (Psa 19:7).

3. Gladdens the heart. "Rejoicing the heart" (Psa 19:8).

4. Enlightens the understanding. "Enlightening the eyes" (Psa 19:8).

5. Squares the life. "True and righteous altogether" (Psa 19:9).

6. Purifies the spirit. "The fear of the Lord is clean" (Psa 19:9).

7. Satisfies the nature. "More to be desired…than gold" (Psa 19:10).
