238. JONAH

The Book of Jonah is like an album in which we have many likenesses of Jonah.

1. A runaway prophet (Jon 1:3).

2. A disobedient servant (Jon 1:3).

3. A sleeping saint (Jon 1:5).

4. A rebuked believer (Jon 1:6).

5. A marked man (Jon 1:7).

6. A minister of evil (Jon 1:8).

7. A true witness (Jon 1:9).

8. A conscious offender (Jon 1:12).

9. A "down-grade" Christian (Jon 1:15).

10. A substitute (Jon 1:15).

11. A type of Christ (Jon 1:17; Mat 12:40).

12. A suppliant (Jon 2:1).

13. A distressed sinner (Jon 2:2).

14. A prophetic voice (Jon 2:5; Psa 69:1-4).

15. A bargaining pleader (Jon 2:9).

16. A restored backslider (Jon 2:10).

17. A dull scholar (Jon 3:1).

18. An obedient servant (Jon 3:3).

19. A faithful messenger (Jon 3:4).

20. A successful evangelist (Jon 3:5).

21. A displeased servant (Jon 4:1).

22. A discouraged servant (Jon 4:3).

23. A questioned disciple (Jon 4:4).

24. A watching rebel (Jon 4:5).

25. A shadowed servant (Jon 4:6).

26. A gladdened servant (Jon 4:6).

27. A chastened child (Jon 4:7).

28. A fainting pilgrim (Jon 4:8).
