191. HIS PRESENCE

"THAT I MAY DWELL AMONG THEM" (EXODUS 25:8).

1. Power of His presence. "Glory and honour are in His presence" (1Ch 16:27).

2. Rest of His presence. "My presence shall go with thee, and I will give thee rest" (Exd 33:14-15).

3. Security of His presence. "Thou shalt hide them in the secret of Thy presence" (Psa 31:20).

4. Joy of His presence. "In Thy presence is fulness of joy" (Psa 16:11).

5. Refreshing of His presence. "Times of refreshing shall come from the presence of the Lord" (Act 3:19).

6. Searching of His presence. "The upright shall dwell in Thy presence" (Psa 140:13).

7. Good cheer of His presence. "Be of good cheer; it is I; be not afraid" (Mat 14:27).
