219. "I WILLS" OF JOHN 14

1. The "I will" of hope. "I will come again" (Jhn 14:3).

2. The "I will" of answered prayer. "If ye shall ask anything in My Name, I will do it" (Jhn 14:14).

3. The "I will" of intercession. "I will pray the Father" (Jhn 14:16).

4. The "I will" of comfort. "I will not leave you comfortless" (Jhn 14:18).

5. The "I will" of presence. "I will come to you" (Jhn 14:18).

6. The "I will" of love. "I will love him" (Jhn 14:21).

7. The "I will" of silence. "I will not talk much with you" (Jhn 14:30).
