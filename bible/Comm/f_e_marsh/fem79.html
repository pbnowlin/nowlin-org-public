79. CHRIST'S VOICE

1. Powerful Voice. "His voice as the sound of many waters" (Rev 1:15).

2. Convicting Voice. "Heard a voice saying unto him, Saul, Saul, why persecutest thou Me?" (Act 9:4).

3. Quickening Voice. "He cried with a loud voice, Lazarus, come forth" (Jhn 11:43).

4. Inviting Voice. "If any man thirst let him come unto Me and drink" (Jhn 7:37).

5. Comforting Voice. "Jesus saith unto her, thy brother shall rise again" (Jhn 11:23).

6. Guiding Voice. "My sheep hear My voice…and they follow Me" (Jhn 10:27).

7. Pleading Voice. "If any man hear My voice" (Rev 1:5).
