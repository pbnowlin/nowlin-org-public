213. IN THE MIDST

JESUS was the Centre of Attraction, as well as occupying a central position. It is of interest to mark how often we read of Christ being "in the midst."

"In the midst"—

1. As the Centre of Attraction (Mat 18:20).

2. As the Questioner (Luk 2:46).

3. As the Servant (Luk 22:27).*

4. As the Living One (Luk 24:36).

5. As the Unknown One (Jhn 1:26).*

6. As the Sufferer (Jhn 19:18).

7. As the Peace-Giver (Jhn 20:19).

8. As the Revealer (Jhn 20:26-27).

9. As the Inspirer (Hbr 2:12).

10. As the Glorified One (Rev 1:13).

11. As the Searcher (Rev 2:1).

12. As the Lamb (Rev 5:6).

13. As the Supplier (Rev 7:17).

* The word "Mesos" in these places is rendered "among."
