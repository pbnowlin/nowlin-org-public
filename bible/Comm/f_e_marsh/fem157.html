157. FULNESS OF SUPPLY

When Christ gave His disciples a draught of fishes He gave them such a supply that the boats were filled (Luk 5:6-7). Christ never gives with a niggardly hand. There is sure to be an overplus when He gives. When He fed the people there were basketsful over.

1. He gives "fulness of blessing" to enrich us (Rom 15:29).

2. Fulness of Grace to bless us (Jhn 1:16).

3. Fulness of Joy to gladden us (Jhn 15:11).

4. Fulness of the Spirit to empower us (Eph 5:18)

5. Fulness of God to sanctify us (Eph 3:19; Col 1:19; Col 2:9).

6. Fulness of Faith to assure us (Hbr 10:22).

7. Fulness of Himself to satisfy us (Eph 4:13).
