430. THE SECRET OF VICTORY

2 CHRONICLES 20

They who side with God are always sure of having the Lord on their side. See the case of the Levites in Exodus 32:26.

1. Seeking the Lord is the forerunner of victory (2Ch 20:3).

2. Praying to the Lord is the plea for victory (2Ch 20:12).

3. Dependence upon the Lord is the attitude for victory (2Ch 20:4).

4. Recognising that the battle is the Lord's is an essential in victory (2Ch 20:15-17).

5. Worhipping before the Lord is praising the Victor (2Ch 20:18-19).

6. Faith in the Lord is assurance for victory (2Ch 20:20).

7. Gladness in the Lord is the result of victory (2Ch 20:27).

8. Rest in the Lord is another outcome of victory (2Ch 20:30).
