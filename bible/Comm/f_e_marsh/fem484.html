484. WHAT IS A CHRISTIAN?

1. Believer in faith. " Believeth on the Son" (Jhn 3:36).

2. Servant in responsibiIity. "Blessed is that servant" (Luk 12:43).

3. Branch in union. "I am the Vine, ye are the branches" (Jhn 15:5).

4. Friend in privilege. "I have called you friends…I have made known unto you" (Jhn 15:15).

5. Witness in testimony. "Thou shalt be His witness unto all men" (Act 22:15).

6. Steward in trust. "As good stewards of the manifold grace of God" (1Pe 4:10).

7. Light in influence. "Ye are the light of the world…Let your light so shine" (Mat 5:14, 16).

8. Salt in power. "Ye are the salt of the earth" (Mat 5:13).
