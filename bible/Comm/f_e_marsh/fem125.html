125. ETERNAL BLESSINGS OF THE BELIEVER

1. We possess eternal Life. "Whosoever believeth in Him…have eternal life" (Jhn 3:15).

2. We are secured in an eternal Covenant. "Through the blood of the everlasting Covenant" (Hbr 13:20).

3. We are saved with an eternal Salvation. "He became the Author of eternal salvation" (Hbr 5:9).

4. We are liberated by an eternal Redemption. "Having obtained eternal redemption for us" (Hbr 9:12).

5. We have an eternal House. "A house not made with hands, eternal in the heavens" (2Cr 5:1).

6. We are called to eternal Glory. "Who hath called us unto His eternal glory" (1Pe 5:10).

7. We are kept for an eternal Inheritance. "Might receive the promise of an eternal inheritance" (Hbr 9:15).

8. We are cheered by eternal Comfort. "Hath given us everlasting consolation" (2Th 2:16).

9. We know there is an eternal Weight of glory in the future. "Worketh for us a far more exceeding and eternal weight of glory" (2Cr 4:17).

10. We shall dwell in eternal Tabernacles. "Receive you into everlasting habitations" (Luk 16:9).

11. We are aiming to have an abundant entrance into the eternal Kingdom. "An entrance shall be ministered unto you abundantly…everlasting kingdom" (2Pe 1:11).
