101. COUNTENANCE OF THE LORD

1. The grace of His countenance. "The Lord lift up His countenance upon thee, and give thee peace" (Num 6:26).

2. Reflection of His countenance. "Lord, lift Thou up the light of Thy countenance upon us" (Psa 4:6).

3. Joy of His countenance. "Thou hast made him exceeding glad with Thy countenance" (Psa 21:6).

4. Saving of His countenance. "For the help of His countenance" (Psa 42:5, 11).

5. Renewing of His countenance. "Who is the health of my countenance and my God" (Psa 43:5).

6. Power of His countenance. "Neither did their own arm save them…but the light of Thy countenance" (Psa 44:3).

7. Place for the believer to walk in. "They shall walk, O Lord, in the light of Thy countenance" (Psa 89:15).

8. Discernment of His countenance. "Our secret sins in the light of Thy countenance" (Psa 90:8).
