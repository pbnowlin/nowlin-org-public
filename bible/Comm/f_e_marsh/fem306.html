306. POSITION OF CHRIST

The Position of Christ. "Sit Thou on My right hand." He sits at the right hand of God—

1. As our Intercessor to plead for us (Rom 8:34).

2. As the Honoured One for us (Mar 16:19).

3. As the Channel of blessing to us (Act 2:33).

4. As the Waiting One to receive us (Act 7:56).

5. As Head of the body, to minister to us (Eph 1:20-22).

6. As the Object to attract us (Col 3:1).

7. As the Sin-purger who has cleared us (Hbr 1:3).

8. As the Glorified One who is to reign (Hbr 1:13).

9. As our Representative (Hbr 8:1).

10. As the Accomplisher of His work (Hbr 10:12).

11. As the Conqueror over His foes (Hbr 12:2).

12. As the Almighty One (1Pe 3:22).

13. As the Expecting One (Psa 110:1).
