454. TOWARDNESS OF OUR BELOVED

"I AM my Beloved's, and His desire is toward me" (Sgs 7:10).

1. Peace towards us. "Peace on earth, good will toward men" (Luk 2:14).

2. Love towards us. "God commendeth His love toward us" (Rom 5:8).

3. Life towards us. "In this was manifested the love of God toward us…that we might live through Him" (1Jo 4:9).

4. Mercy towards us. "Great is Thy mercy toward me" (Psa 86:13).

5. Supply towards us. "He hath abounded toward us" (Eph 1:7-8).

6. Power towards us. "God is able to make all grace abound toward you" (2Cr 9:8).

7. Kindness towards us. "His kindness toward us through Christ Jesus" (Eph 2:7).
