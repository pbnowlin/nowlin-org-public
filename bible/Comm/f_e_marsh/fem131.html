131. FAINT NOT

1. Prayer. Faint not in prayer. "Pray and not faint" (Luk 18:1).

2. Perseverance. Faint not in confidence. "We faint not" (2Cr 4:1).

3. Preservation. Faint not in hope. "For which cause we faint not" (2Cr 4:16).

4. Preparation. Faint not in work, "Reap if we faint not" (Gal 6:9).

5. Power. Faint not in trusting. "I desire that ye faint not" (Eph 3:13).

6. Practice. Faint not in well doing. "Faint not in well doing" (2Th 3:13, margin).

7. Patience. Faint not under chastening. "Nor faint when thou art rebuked" (Hbr 12:5).
