149. FORGIVENESS

1. Unmerited forgiveness. "When they had nothing to pay, He frankly forgave them both" (Luk 7:42).

2. Divine forgiveness. "To the Lord our God belong mercy and forgivenesses" (Dan 9:9).

3. Purchased forgiveness. "In Whom we have redemption through His blood, the forgiveness of sins" (Eph 1:7).

4. Free forgiveness. "That they may receive forgiveness of sins…by faith that is in Me" (Act 26:18).

5. Full forgiveness. "Having forgiven you all trespasses" (Col 2:13).

6. Practical forgiveness. "Forgive us our debts, as we forgive" (Mat 6:12).

7. Known forgiveness. "Your sins are forgiven you for His name's sake" (1Jo 2:12).
