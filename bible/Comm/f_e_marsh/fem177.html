177. GRACE

1. Justified by grace. "Justified freely by His grace" (Rom 3:24).

2. Standing in grace. "This grace wherein we stand" (Rom 5:2).

3. Saved by grace. "By grace ye are saved" (Eph 2:5).

4. Elected by grace. "A remnant according to the election of grace" (Rom 11:5).

5. Strengthened by grace. "My grace is sufficient…My strength is made perfect" (2Cr 12:9).

6. Working by grace. "I laboured…yet not I, but the grace of God" (1Cr 15:10).

7. Growing in grace. "Grow in grace" (2Pe 3:18).
