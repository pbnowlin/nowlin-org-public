290. ONLY

The first commandment is God's claim to have the absolute place in the heart and life (Exd 20:3). Jehovah will not share with another. Christ repeats this same truth when Satan would have Him to worship himself (Mat 4:10). The following Scriptures where the word "only" occurs will illustrate the absoluteness of God's claim:

1. Saved by the Lord only (2Ki 19:19; Isa 37:20).

2. Separated only unto the Lord (1Sa 7:3-4).

3. Sacrificing only to the Lord (Exd 22:20).

4. Serving only the Lord (Mat 4:10).

5. Speaking only the word of the Lord (Num 22:35).

6. Seeing only the Lord (Mat 17:8).

7. Singing only of the Lord (Rom 16:27).
