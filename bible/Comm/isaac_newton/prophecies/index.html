Caution: We have included Sir Isaac Newton's Interpretation of the books of Daniel and Revelation for your edification and study. However, it is necessary to note that he holds to a non-creedal interpretation of the Trinity, and therefore, his theology is considered to be heterodox. This means that he is not necessarily a heretic, but holds to certain unorthodox interpretations. We do not believe that any of his heterodoxy is evident in his interpretation of Daniel and Revelation—yet if you find any objectionable content please do not hesitate from contacting us.
