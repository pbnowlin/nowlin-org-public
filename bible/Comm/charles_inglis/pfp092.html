EIGHT CONDITIONS OF HAPPINESS

  • Happy is he whom God correcteth……Job 5:17
  • Happy is he who trusteth in the Lord……Pro 16:20
  • Happy is he that hath mercy on the poor……Pro 14:21
  • Happy is the man that feareth the Lord……Pro 28:14
  • Happy is he that findeth wisdom……Pro 3:13
  • Happy are they, who knowing His will, do it……Jhn 13:17
  • Happy are ye, if reproached for His name……1Pe 4:14
  • Happy are they that endure……Jam 5:11
