PRAYER

WHERE TO PRAY: —

  • In private……Mat 6:6
  • In public……Act 1:14

HOW TO PRAY: —

  • In the Holy Ghost……Jud 1:20
  • In faith……Jam 1:6
  • With the understanding……1Cr 14:15

WHAT WE SHOULD PRAY FOR: —

  • All saints……Eph 6:18
  • All men……1Ti 2:1
  • All things……Phl 4:6

WHEN GOD ANSWERS PRAYER: —

  • Sometimes at once……Isa 65:24
  • Sometimes after delay……Luk 18:7
  • Sometimes differently from what we expect……2Cr 12:8-9

WHAT HINDERS OUR PRAYERS: —

  • Asking amiss……Jam 4:3
  • Unbelief……Jam 1:6-7
  • Iniquity in the heart……Psa 66:18
