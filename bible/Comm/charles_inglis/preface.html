Preface

The object of this volume is to help those who seek to speak for the Lord Jesus, and yet have little time at their disposal for the preparation of their message.

Most of these Bible Readings it has been our privilege to deliver to thousands of workers in all parts of the world, and many hearers who have received blessing and profit have urged their issue in a printed form.

We have gone through some thousands of references with a great deal of care, but should our readers find a blemish here or there, they will remember that many of these Bible Readings have been searched out on long railway journeys and ocean voyages.

Should this volume be the means of stirring up any to search the precious Word of God in these days, when its fundamental truths are being denied, we shall feel that our labors have not been in vain.

C.I.
