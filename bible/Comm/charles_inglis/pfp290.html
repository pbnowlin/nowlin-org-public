SEVEN CHARACTERISTICS OF THE SHEEP
IN JOHN 10

  • They are SAVED by Him……Jhn 10:9
  • They have LIBERTY from Him……Jhn 10:9
  • They are CALLED by Him……Jhn 10:3
  • They are SECURED in Him……Jhn 10:28
  • They are FED by Him……Jhn 10:9
  • They are OBEDIENT to Him……Jhn 10:4
  • They have KNOWLEDGE of Him……Jhn 10:14
