LESSONS FROM THE AXE HEAD
IN 2 KINGS 6

  • It was lost……2Ki 6:5
  • It was in a place of death……2Ki 6:4-5
  • It had to be accounted for (borrowed)……2Ki 6:5
  • It was recovered……2Ki 6:6-7
  • The means used — branch cut down……2Ki 6:6; Isa 11:1
  • Person who recovered it — man of God……2Ki 6:6
