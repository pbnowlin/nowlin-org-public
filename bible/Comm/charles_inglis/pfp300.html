SEVEN POINTS
IN JEREMIAH 1

  • God knowing him “I knew thee.”
  • God sanctifying him “I sanctified thee.”
  • God ordaining him “I ordained thee.”
  • God sending him “I shall send.”
  • God commanding him “I command.”
  • God encouraging him “Be not afraid.”
  • God speaking through him
       “I have put my words in thy mouth.”
