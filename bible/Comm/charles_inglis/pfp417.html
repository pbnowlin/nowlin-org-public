THE WORD OF GOD

What it is —

  • The SON OF GOD……Jhn 1:14
  • Likened to SEED……Luk 8:11
  • Likened to a SWORD……Eph 6:17
  • Likened to a FIRE……Jer 23:29
  • Likened to a HAMMER……Jer 23:29

The character of it —

  • POWERFUL……Hbr 4:12
  • PURE……Pro 30:5
  • UNALTERABLE……Isa 40:8

What we are to do with it —

  • DESIRE it……1Pe 2:2
  • RECEIVE it……Jam 1:21
  • KEEP it……Jhn 17:6
  • HOLD it fast……Tts 1:9
  • PREACH it……2Ti 4:2
  • REMEMBER it……Luk 24:8
  • Be DOERS of it……Jam 1:22

The effect of the Word —

  • We are QUICKENED by it……Psa 119:25
  • We are BORN AGAIN by it……1Pe 1:23
  • We are CLEANSED by it……Psa 119:9
  • We are SANCTIFIED by it……Jhn 17:17
  • We are STRENGTHENED by it……Psa 119:28
  • We OVERCOME by it……Rev 12:11
