SEVEN CLASSES IN THE DISCIPLES’ PRAYER
IN MATTHEW 6

  • “Our Father” — The children……Mat 6:9
  • “Hallowed be Thy name” — Worshippers……Mat 6:9
  • “Thy Kingdom come” — The subjects……Mat 6:10
  • “Thy will be done” — The servants……Mat 6:10
  • “Give us this day” — The suppliants……Mat 6:11
  • “Forgive us our debts” — The confessors……Mat 6:12
  • “Deliver us from evil” — The dependents……Mat 6:13
