  • POWERFUL men — Men of might……1Ch 12:8
  • WARLIKE men — Men of War……1Ch 12:8

  • BOLD men — Faces like lions……1Ch 12:8
  • DILIGENT men — Swift as roes……1Ch 12:8
  • VICTORIOUS men — Put to flight the enemy……1Ch 12:15
