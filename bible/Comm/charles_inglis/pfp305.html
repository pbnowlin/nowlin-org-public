SEVEN THINGS ABOUT THE RICH MAN
IN LUKE 16

  • His CONDITION — rich……Luk 16:19
  • His CHARACTER — religious (Jew)……Luk 16:24
  • His POSITION — in need……Luk 16:23
  • His PRAYER — “have mercy”……Luk 16:24
  • His SUFFERING — “I am tormented”……Luk 16:24
  • His CONSCIENCE — Son, remember……Luk 16:25
  • His SEPARATION — great gulf fixed……Luk 16:26
