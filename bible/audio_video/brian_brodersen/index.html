Brian Brodersen—Audio Commentaries

Pastor Brian Brodersen has been involved in pastoral ministry since 1983, and currently serves as Senior Pastor of Calvary Chapel Costa Mesa in Southern California Prior to, Pastor Brian served as Associate Pastor of Calvary Chapel Costa Mesa, Senior Pastor of Calvary Chapel Vista, and Senior Pastor of Calvary Chapel Westminster (London, England). Pastor Brian has been extensively involved in missionary work throughout various parts of Europe, and is the featured speaker on the Bible teaching program “Back to Basics”. He is known for his clear and challenging exposition of the Scriptures. Brian and his wife Cheryl have four children, and reside in Southern California.
