Margaret Ashmore — Audio / Video Commentaries

Raised in the heart of Texas, Margaret Ashmore came to Christ in the 1970s, abandoning her self-avowed feminist mindset to become transformed by the grace of God. She’s been a member of the Denton Bible Church since 1980, where she teaches women’s studies and serves as lay counselor. Miss Ashmore currently maintains a full schedule as a conference speaker, declaring the sufficiency of Jesus Christ through the power of His Word.
