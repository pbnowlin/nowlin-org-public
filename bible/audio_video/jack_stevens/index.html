Jack Stevens — Audio / Video Commentaries

Pastor Jack Stevens was the senior pastor of Calvary Chapel Cypress for over 30 years. Pastor Stevens went home to be with the Lord in 2010. We are truly blessed to have his audio teaching tapes available to the readers of the Blue Letter Bible.

Our thanks to Firefighters For Christ for their efforts in encoding these tapes.
