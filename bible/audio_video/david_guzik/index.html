David Guzik — Audio / Video Commentaries

David Guzik is currently the teaching pastor of Calvary Chapel Santa Barbara.

Pastor David is married to Inga-Lill, and they have three adult children. His 35 years of pastoral ministry and Christian service include two church plants and leading an international Bible College in Siegen, Germany. Pastor David is also a popular Bible commentator, with his written, audio, and video Bible resources available for free at Enduring Word.

Visit the Enduring Word website for books, software, and tapes by Pastor Guzik.
