Brett Peterson — Audio / Video Commentaries

Pastor Brett Peterson has been in full time ministry since 1985. He has been a youth Pastor, missionary, associate Pastor, Gen-X Pastor, and was the Administrator of Calvary Chapel Bible College in Rancho Santa Margarita, California and Jerusalem. Currently he is President of Coastland University. He is also a Chaplain with the Orange County Fire Authority. Active in evangelistic outreach and street ministry in Rancho Santa Margarita as well as teaching a Bible study in Ladera Ranch, CA, that Bible study has become Living Water Community Church in Wagon Wheel/Ladera Ranch, California. His wife, Cheryl, runs the women’s ministry and his son Cody is pursing his Masters in Theology.
