Bob Davis — Audio / Video Commentaries

Pastor Bob Davis received Jesus Christ as his Lord and Savior in 1973 on the island of Guam while serving with the U.S. Navy Seabees. He has been pastoring with Calvary Chapels for the past 25 years, serving churches in Colorado and Arizona. Bob also taught for almost 5 years at Calvary Chapel's Bible College located in southern California. Currently Pastor Bob is the senior pastor of North Country Chapel, a Calvary Chapel Fellowship, located in Post Falls, Idaho. The fellowship began over 10 years ago as a simple Friday night Bible Study and North Country Chapel was born and continues to grow. Pastor Bob teaches verse by verse through the Bible and is heard nationwide on the radio program Apply Within.
