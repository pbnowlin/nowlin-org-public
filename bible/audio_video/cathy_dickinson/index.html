Cathy Dickinson — Audio / Video Commentaries

Cathy Dickinson has been married to her husband Dale for over forty-two years and they have two married children and six grandchildren. They attended and served at Calvary Chapel Costa Mesa before taking over the pastorate at Calvary Chapel El Cajon, California, in 1997. As of August, 2013, the name of the fellowship changed to Jericho Road Christian Fellowhip. She currently heads their women’s Bible study, and helps with counseling at the church. Cathy has a passion for mentoring women in the faith and often speaks at retreats and conferences.
