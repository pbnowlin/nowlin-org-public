Cheryl Brodersen — Audio / Video Commentaries

Cheryl Brodersen, speaker and author, currently heads the Joyful Life Women’s Ministry at Calvary Chapel Costa Mesa, California, where she teaches weekly Bible studies. She’s the youngest daughter of Chuck and Kay Smith, and has been married to Pastor Brian Brodersen for over thirty years. They’ve raised four children and have four wonderful grandsons. Following in her mother’s footsteps, the teaching baton was graciously passed on to Cheryl and today her Scripture-based lectures can be heard on radio stations around the world.
