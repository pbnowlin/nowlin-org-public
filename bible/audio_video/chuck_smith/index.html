Chuck Smith — Audio Commentaries

Pastor Chuck Smithwas the senior pastor of Calvary Chapel Costa Mesa since 1965, and has been influential in the Christian community for many years. Pastor Chuck is best known for his expository style of preaching, as he unpacked the truths of Scripture while working his way throught the entire Bible from Genesis to Revelation. These teachings are still being broadcast daily around the world on his radio program The Word for Today.
