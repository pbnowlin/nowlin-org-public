Rob Douglass — Audio / Video Commentaries

Pastor Rob Douglass is the Founder and President of Ends of the Earth Ministries (EEM). The primary goal of EEM is to share the Good News of Jesus Christ with those who have never before heard His name. EEM focuses 100% of their efforts to complete the great commission among totally unreached people. Currently, EEM is working in Sudan and Sri Lanka targeting 20 million unreached souls. Through mainly national missionaries, EEM has been able to bring a culturally relevant Gospel message to previously closed and hostile people.
