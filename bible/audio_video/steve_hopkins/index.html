Steve Hopkins — Audio / Video Commentaries

Pastor Steve Hopkins attended Southern California College (now known as Vanguard University) in Costa Mesa, California where he met his wife, Debra. He was a police officer in Southern Oregon for 11 years before going to work on the staff of Applegate Christian Fellowship. He has been the senior pastor at Calvary Chapel Christian Fellowship in Salem Oregon since 1996.
