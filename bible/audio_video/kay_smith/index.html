Kay Smith — Audio / Video Commentaries

Kay Smith is the former wife of Pastor Chuck Smith (deceased), the founding pastor of Calvary Chapel Costa Mesa. Kay has taught Bible studies to women at Calvary Chapel for over three decades. Her influence has reached far beyond Calvary Chapel, touching the lives of countless women who have been inspired by both her example and godly exhortation. She lives and minister in Southern California.
