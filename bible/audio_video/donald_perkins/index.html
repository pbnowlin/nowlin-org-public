Donald Perkins — Audio / Video Commentaries

Evangelist Donald Perkins is founder and President of According to Prophecy Ministries. Evangelist Perkins has been teaching Bible prophecy over twenty seven years, and is an ordained Minister of the Gospel of Jesus Christ. His is a member of the Pre-Trib Research Study Group, founded by Dr. Tim LaHaye. He is a contributing author in the “The Dictionary of Premillennial Theology,” produced by Kregel Publishers. Evangelist Perkins is an instructor on Bible prophecy at several Bible colleges and travels throughout the United States and the world as a conference speaker and in the local churches.
