Trent Douglass — Audio / Video Commentaries

Calvary Chapel Saving Grace started out as a home Bible study around a campfire. It grew from two to four to eight. As people became hungry for God’s word, more came; twenty, forty, sixty. More and more people came, and lives were changed as they met the risen Lord. As time progressed, God raised up a teacher, Trent Douglass.
