Raul Ries — Audio / Video Commentaries

Raul Ries is the Senior Pastor of Calvary Chapel Golden Springs in Diamond Bar, CA. He is also the director of Somebody Loves You Ministries, which includes a daily syndicated radio program, the facilitating of crusade evangelism, concert outreaches, and film/video production. He holds a Doctor of Divinity from Fuller Theological Seminary and has authored several books, including Fury to Freedom (the story of his early life and dramatic conversion—translated in six languages). Pastor Raul has also produced three films: Fury to Freedom, A Quiet Hope, and A Venture in Faith (a documentary of the history of the Calvary Chapel movement).
