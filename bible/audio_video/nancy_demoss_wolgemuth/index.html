Nancy DeMoss Wolgemuth — Audio / Video Commentaries

A gifted and passionate speaker, Nancy encourages her listeners to seek God in the pages of His Word, to love and serve Him whole-heartedly, and to readily share the Gospel message.

Born in Philadelphia, Nancy DeMoss grew up in Christian family that was deeply committed to serving the Lord through the Church and world evangelism. She carries on that family history of ministry by hosting and teaching on two daily radio programs, Revive Our Hearts and Seeking Him. Her messages can also be heard in Spanish at Aviva Nuestros Corazones. For more than 25 years, Nancy has communicated her burden for both personal and corporate revival in conferences and retreats throughout North America and abroad. She is the author of more than 20 books and is the general editor of Biblical Womanhood in the Home.

Nancy and Robert Wolgemuth exchanged wedding vows on November 14, 2015. To view their Christ-exalting engagement and ceremony, visit Unexpected Grace: Nancy & Robert’s Story.
