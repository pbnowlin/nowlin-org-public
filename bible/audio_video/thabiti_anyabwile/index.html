Thabiti Anyabwile — Audio / Video Commentaries

Pastor Thabiti Anyabwile holds BS and MS degrees from North Carolina State University. He previously served at Capitol Hill Baptist Church in Washington DC, and in 2006 became Senior Pastor at First Baptist Church of Grand Cayman. Pastor Thabiti returned to Capitol Hill Baptist Church in 2014, and serves as assistant pastor for church planting. He is a council member with The Gospel Coalition, a lead writer for 9Marks Ministries, and regularly blogs at Pure Church. Pastor Thabiti is also co-founder of The Front Porch – a website that hosts conversations about biblical faithfulness in African-American churches and beyond. He and his wife, Kristie, have been married for over twenty years and have three children.

Thabiti has authored numerous books including:

  • Captivated: Beholding the Mystery of Jesus' Death and Resurrection
  • Ephesians: God's Big Plan for Christ's New People
  • Finding Faithful Elders and Deacons
  • Proclaiming a Cross-centered Theology (Contributor)
  • The Decline of African American Theology
  • The Faithful Preacher
  • The Life of God in the Soul of the Church
  • The Gospel for Muslims
  • What Is a Healthy Church Member?

We wish to thank Pastor Thabiti and First Baptist Church of Grand Cayman for freely providing his audio messages.
