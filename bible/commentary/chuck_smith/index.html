Text Commentaries
Chuck Smith
Pastor Chuck Smith was best known for his expository style of preaching, as he unpacked the truths of Scripture while working his way through the entire Bible from Genesis to Revelation. These teachings are still being broadcast daily around the world on his radio program Word for Today. He founded and pastored Calvary Chapel Costa Mesa, California, in the late 1960’s and faithfully served there until October 3, 2013, when he entered into the eternal presence of our Lord.

If you have an Internet connection, we also have over 300 audio commentaries of Pastor Smith's, which are available from the Audio/Video Menu.
