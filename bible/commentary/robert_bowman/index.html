Robert M. Bowman, Jr.

Robert M. Bowman, Jr. is the founder of the Center for Biblical Apologetics. He graduated with a B.A. from California State University, Dominguez Hills (1978) and also obtained a Master's degree in Biblical Studies and Theology from Fuller Theological Seminary (1981). As an accomplished author he has written many books and articles pertaining to apologetics, cults, and theology. In the 1980s he was a researcher working with the late Walter Martin at the Christian Research Institute (CRI). Since then he has worked with a number of apologetics ministries, including the Atlanta Christian Apologetics Project, Watchman Fellowship, and Apologetics.com. Rob is currently an instructor in the M.A. in Christian Apologetics program at Biola University, where he teaches courses on religious cults and on biblical apologetics. Rob is also well known from Christian radio, having been a regular on the nationally syndicated "Bible Answer Man" program (1990-91) and the host of "Truth Talk" in Atlanta (1994-96) and the "Apologetics.com Radio Show" in Los Angeles (2002-2003). Rob and his wife Cathy have been married since 1981 and have four children—Beth, Robbie, John, and Maria.
