David Terasaka, M.D.
Dr. David Terasaka (1955-2024) was an acclaimed medical doctor in Syracuse, New York. As a believer and with his medical background, he had unique insight into the depths of the excruciating pain our Lord Jesus went through as God's sin offering for us on the cross. He had a personal relationship with our ministry, and provided us the below articles with his findings and thoughts on the matter. He went home to be with his Savior on May 7, 2024.
