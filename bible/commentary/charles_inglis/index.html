Charles Inglis (1734-1816) was a Church of England clergyman, bishop, and author. He was raised in a clerical family of Scottish descent. Inglis was ordained in 1764, and became the acting president of King's College in 1770. He went on to consecrate many churches. He spent the majority of his life learning and teaching the Word of God.
