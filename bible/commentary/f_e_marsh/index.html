English-born, Dr. Frederick Edward Marsh (1858-1931) devoted his life to studying the Bible and unfolding its treasures to others through his preaching and his many books and outlines. In addition to being a well-known conference speaker, he served as a minister of the Bethesda Free Church, in Sunderland, U.K. from July 1887 to 1907. In his work on “The Structural Principles of the Bible”, Marsh encourages diligent study of the Word of God for the enrichment of every believer. His publications were read and admired by the late F.B. Meyer.
