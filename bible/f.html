Free Bible Tools
Following are some free tools for searching the Blue Letter Bible that you can put on your own website or computer.

(Internet connection required)

BLB ScriptTagger: A free tool that you can integrate into your website or blog to automatically create a hover-over display for all Bible references on the page. The hover-over reveals the verse text and provides a link for further study at the Blue Letter Bible website.

BLB Web Search Tool: If you would like to use the Blue Letter Bible's search capabilities from your own site, this resource will show you how to do that.

ScriptureMark: Introducing ScriptureMark—the powerful new study tool that enables personalized formatting and markups of Bible passages—to help you better understand and teach others God's Word.
