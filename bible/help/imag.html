How to Find Images, Maps, and Hymns
(How to Use the I-Button)

1. To select an image from a selected passage of Scripture, first go to that portion of Scripture in the Blue Letter Bible (the easiest means to this end is to search out the verse from our home page).


2. Once on the page with the desired passage, you will notice that each verse of Scripture is prefaced by an assortment of buttons (generally , , , , , and/or ). The button is for a listing of images and hymns related to the selected verse. If one appears, click on the button as illustrated below.


3. This will pop up a window featuring a drop-down menu allowing access to the appropriate images.


4. If you click directly on the menu or the down arrow , a list of image and map choices will appear. Choose one of these by clicking on it (as illustrated below).


5. Now you need only click on the button to be simply whisked to a table containing the image information you have selected.


6. When you choose an image or map, a table with a number of image listings will appear. In the first column is the name of the image or map — in the example below, "The Creation (engraving)" — and in the following column are verse references and dictionary topics linked to the image — in the illustration below, "Gen 1:1-2; creation." By clicking on the image name, you will be sent directly to the image designated. By clicking on the verse reference, you will be sent back to that reference in the Blue Letter Bible. By clicking on the topic, you will be sent to a results page as if you had run a word search for the topic (in this case, "creation" would send you to a page with every verse of Scripture containing the word "creation" as well as three dictionary references.
