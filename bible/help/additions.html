Version 2.40

  • Updated the Holman Christian Standard Bible to the Christian Standard Bible.
  • Added new Devotionals
    • Faith's Checkbook by Charles Spurgeon
    • Daily Promises by the Staff of Blue Letter Bible
  • Lexicon Results found under the "C" button have been extensively edited.
    • The Outline of Biblical Usage was reformatted. When the meaning of a proper noun is given, it is shown on its own separate line. The part of speech serves as a major topic with definitions indented beneath. Any additional information or commentary given has been separately identified.
    • Abbreviations for parts of speech have been replaced by the full terms, e.g., 'v' becomes 'verb'; 'n' becomes 'noun'; 'part.' become 'participle', etc.
    • Added the original Strong's Definitions for both Hebrew and Greek words to the Concordance pages.
    • Converted all Hebrew text to unicode to present the Hebrew characters, eliminating the need to install a Hebrew font.
    • Greek text now appears correctly.
    • Other Strong's numbers referenced in the definition have been linked to that definition.
    • Special markers in the Strong's definition are now explained by an added legend.
  • Added Bill Acton's text commentaries on the Book of Acts and the Book of Romans to both his author page under commentaries and to the Commentaries drop down on the Bible pages under the "L" button. This added over 10,000 new links.
  • Added Chuck Missler's studies on Daniel, John, and Revelation to the Commentaries drop down on the Bible pages found under the "L" button". This added over 10,000 new links.
  • Removed the Word and Phrase Search from the Search Tools and placed it on the Home page.
  • Removed the Free Offer from the Search Tools as it is already on the left hand menu.
  • Added a Search the ISBE to the Search Tools. It was once located under Study Tools.
  • Removed the videos from the Passion Week Player as they were of poor quality and new better versions could not be created.
