Why can't I find the words that I am searching for?
(AKA does the BLB have spelling errors?)

When you do a search for a word on the "Phrase Search / Concordance" you might experience a problem looking for your specific word. The "Phrase Search / Concordance" searches the King James Version. The edition of the King James Version that we use is the 1769 Edition. Because of this, there are some minor spelling variations, even from more recent editions of the King James Version.

Here are some examples:

1769 Edition of the KJV Updated Editions of the KJV
to day (Hbr 4:7) today (Hbr 4:7)
publick (Mat 1:19) public (Mat 1:19)
stedfast (1Cr 15:58) steadfast (1Cr 15:58)
vail (1Cr 3:16) veil (1Cr 3:16)
