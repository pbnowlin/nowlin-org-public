How to Enter a Reference in the "Go to Bible" Tool

When entering a reference on the "Go to Bible" tool, the reference can take the following forms.

1 John 5:1
1 John 5:1
1 John 5.1 (may use a period instead of a colon)
1 john 5:1 (upper or lower case accepted)
1John 5:1 (spaces are not required)
1 John 5 (if no verse is specified, verse = 1)
1 John (if no chapter is specified,

chapter and verse = 1)
