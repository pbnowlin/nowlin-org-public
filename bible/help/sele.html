How to Select New Verses From Within Bible

1. At the top of every page of Bible navigation, there is a 5-buttoned bar allowing you to navigate backwards or forward through both chapter and book, or to selecet a specific verse. To proceed directly to a particular point in Scripture, click on the button (as demonstrated in the illustration below).


2. This will send you to the bottom of the page, where you will find a similar navigation bar (with the exception of the replacement of the button with a button) and input boxes for Book, Chapter, and Verse.


3. If you click on the menu box that says "Genesis," a scrollable list of available books will appear. Click on the book you would like to select.


4. Now enter the Chapter and verse of the selected book that you would like to view.


5. Now simply click on the button to be whisked to your selected verse.
