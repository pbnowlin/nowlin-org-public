Classic Sabacc — target 23

Deck: 76 cards. Four suits — Sabers, Flasks, Coins, Staves — 1–11, plus Commander (12), Mistress (13), Master (14), Ace (15). Plus 16 face cards, two each: Idiot (0), Queen of Air and Darkness (−2), Endurance (−8), Balance (−11), Demise (−13), Moderation (−14), Evil One (−15), Star (−17).

  • Closest to +23 or −23 wins. Over 23, under −23, or exactly 0 bombs out.
  • Pure Sabacc — exactly 23 or −23 — wins the hand pot and the Sabacc pot.
  • Idiot's Array — Idiot + a 2 + a 3 — beats everything, even Pure Sabacc.
  • After each round the dice are rolled. Doubles = a Sabacc Shift: every unfrozen hand is redealt. Freeze yours for 10 credits.
  • At the same distance, positive beats negative: +21 beats −21, which beats +20.

Sources: West End Games' Crisis on Cloud City rules, as summarised at pagat.com and sabacc.starwars.run. Face-card value for the Queen is −2 in those sources (some summaries say −1).

Corellian Spike — target 0

Deck: 62 cards. Three staves — Circles, Triangles, Squares — each +1 to +10 and −1 to −10. Plus two Sylops (0).

  • Closest to 0 wins. Exactly 0 wins the hand pot and the Sabacc pot.
  • Pure Sabacc — both Sylops, and nothing else — is the best hand.
  • Then Full Sabacc (+10, +10, −10, −10, Sylop), Fleet (Sylop + four of a kind), Yehhaa (Sylop + a pair). Then other zero hands, then closest to 0. Positive beats equal negative.
  • Three rounds. Each round: draw / swap / stand / junk, then the dice. Doubles = every hand is discarded and redealt.
  • No bomb-out in Spike — a big total just loses.

Sources: the Galaxy's Edge boxed-set booklet (Disney/Lucasfilm), as checked at findtherules.com, and Wookieepedia's Corellian Spike entry. The official booklet has no betting; this version adds chips so the two variants play the same way.
